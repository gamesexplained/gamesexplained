## 0.0.155 · 2026-10-12 · Gauntlet (C64) · chunkypixel with Claude

- **Run decimal arithmetic at its borrow.** Gauntlet's poison takes 100
  health with decimal mode off and no test for a borrow. At 2000 the
  slip reads as a stray digit; at 50 health it wraps to `$FF50`, which the
  decimal clock takes 16,550 ticks to count down. Reading the routine
  found the first case; only a sweep of the subtraction over every value,
  zero included, showed the second, and it is the one a player can use.
  When a game keeps a number in BCD, run each routine that changes it at
  0, at each digit's 9 and 0, and below zero, beside the routine that
  undoes it.
