## 0.0.66 · 1 October 2026 · Jupiter Lander · 64kramsystem with Codex

**Preserve unknown memory when importing annotations.** Jupiter Lander's
symbol-map round trip exposed a coverage increase when omitted ranges were
filled as byte data: the coverage ledger gave renamed variables a longer
reach. Explicit gaps prevent the disassembler from decoding runtime buffers
as code, but those gaps must stay untyped. `symbols_import.py` now fills gaps as
`Undefined`; a round trip of an existing game's symbol map checks that the
per-byte coverage and ownership stay unchanged. The Ghidra importer also
names and bundles its custom exporter, with a fixture generated by Ghidra,
so a contributor can reproduce the accepted layout instead of guessing
stock ASCII export settings.
