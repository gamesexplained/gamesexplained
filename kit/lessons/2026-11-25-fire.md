## next · 25 November 2026 · Fire (Game & Watch) · icyriver2023 with Claude

- **A port held in lockstep is a second reading of the listing.** The
  sample of 60 comments found 9 wrong. Writing the Play tab's port found
  two of them before the checker's verdicts came back, and the first
  played lockstep run found another the sample had not drawn: the jump's
  chance calls the helper that tests bit 3 of the seconds, and the
  comment, the notes and the first draft of the port had all read it as
  bit 2. Each instruction has to be written out in the port, so a
  misread helper or a swapped pair of cells shows as a divergence at the
  exact address.
- **A branch between two readings of an input cannot be reached in
  lockstep.** The lockstep changes the buttons only at checkpoints. In
  Fire, as in Parachute, the routine that reads left and right drops a
  press of both at once, and the routine it hands a single press to has
  its own test for both, which runs only when the second button comes
  within a few instructions of the first. Both runs listed that test
  among the states the sessions must reach, and both then had to drop
  it; the state to list is the one the program does take, the dropped
  press.
