## next · 2026-10-25 · Kane (C64) · air with Claude

- **Count a loop's pace before a comment states one.** The first pass
  over the game's code took its main loops to run once a frame. A sample
  of 60 comments, checked on 25 October 2026, found four of the fourteen
  wrong ones wrong for that reason alone: the ride's loop, the train's
  step, the shoot-out's loop and the gunmen's delay. Counting checkpoints
  on the last raster handler and on each loop's top then showed the
  ride's loop making 54 passes a frame, since it waits on nothing, and
  the shoot-out's one pass every 2.8 frames and the canyon's one every
  3.7, since both wait on a delay routine (about 46,000 and 57,600
  cycles) that reads as one frame's wait. Every other comment that gave
  a time in frames from the same belief was corrected with them. The Way
  of the Exploding Fist's run met it too: its exchange loop was taken for
  once a frame, and the bout clock was written as seconds until
  checkpoints showed 195 passes in 250 frames. `50-coverage` now says
  that a pace in a comment is a measurement, and what to write until it
  has been counted.
