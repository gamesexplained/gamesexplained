## 0.0.278 · 2026-10-31 · Explorer (C64) · air with Claude

- **Look at a picture drawn by the game's own code before describing
  it.** Explorer keeps Spectrum pictures, cell by cell with an attribute
  byte, and one attribute means "keep the colour already on the screen".
  A renderer written for the run painted those cells white, and eight
  comments described white stones and pale trunks that the game never
  draws. The checker caught one; drawing every picture with the game's
  `draw_image` on the kit's 6502, from the listing's bytes, caught the
  rest. A decoder's guess at what a byte means reads as right in every
  description it feeds.
- **Hand the sample to the checker unread.** The run read the 60 drawn
  comments, saw two that numbered the fragments from 0 where the game
  numbers them from 1, and corrected them before the checker had the
  list, so the checker read corrected text. The measured rate came out two
  lower than the listing deserved, with nothing to show it. Those two are
  counted as wrong in the published 8 of 60.
- **Measure every share a page states, with code that is tested.** "About
  one cell in 154" for the doorways had nothing behind it; the game's own
  terrain routine on 150,000 cells gave one in 255, and a third of them on
  open ground where the page said nearly all were jungle.
