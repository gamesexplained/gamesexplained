## 0.0.151 · 2026-10-10 · Valhalla (ZX Spectrum) · air

- **A drawing seeded by its place can be drawn whole in a simulator.**
  Valhalla draws each of its 81 places when you arrive, from the place's
  number. Porting the BASIC that builds the drawing commands, and running
  each command through the game's own graphics entry in SkoolKit's Z80
  simulator, drew every place; the emulator checked 13 of them to the
  byte, and a test holds the page's picture sheet to the simulator. Wait
  for the drawing to finish before comparing: 600 frames after arriving,
  the emulator's picture was 14 bytes short.
- **Freeze the other characters with one token.** A live test of a fight
  failed when the other fighter walked off while the command was being
  typed. In a game whose logic is BASIC, one byte of the program in the
  snapshot stops them without touching the rule under test: `PEEK`
  changed to `ABS` made the line that starts each character's action
  return at once. Say beside the result which lines were changed.
- **Name the routines that take arguments after their `CALL`.** A drawer
  that reads its arguments from the bytes after the call sends a static
  trace into those bytes and from there anywhere; Valhalla's took
  `codemap.py` through 14 KB of BASIC. Give each one to the trace
  (`--inline ADDR:N`) as soon as the annotation finds it.
