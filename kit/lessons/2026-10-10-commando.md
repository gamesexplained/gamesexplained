## 0.0.147 · 2026-10-10 · Commando (C64) · RealSnoddas and air

- **In a loop that runs one handler per slot, check what every handler's
  callees leave in the index register.** Commando's enemy loop keeps the
  slot number in X. The box of grenades frees its slot by X, but before
  that it adds its points, and an extra life on the way asks for a sound
  effect, whose routine returns with X = 0. The box then frees enemy 0's
  slot instead of its own, the loop carries on from enemy 1 and pays for
  the box a second time. Nothing in the box's own routine looks wrong;
  the audit found it by listing every LDX, TAX, INX and DEX reachable from
  each handler, callees included, and the simulator and then the emulator
  confirmed it.
- **A loop's count is not how many things it shows.** The attract mode
  counts eight views and the comment said eight. The eighth is set up and
  drawn, and the jump back to the title at the end of the same pass wipes
  it before a frame is seen. Stopping at each view's set-up in the
  emulator and looking at the screen 60 frames later found it.
- **Hold a drawn map against play memory in that map's own area.** The
  maps drawn from the hand-over image differed from play where every
  life's start stamps a shut gate and three bunkers into them. A play
  snapshot in the first area could not show the bunkers, which are in the
  second; a capture taken there did.
