## 0.0.68 · 2 October 2026 · Wizard · jankfoundry with Codex

**Follow the program inside the interpreter.** Wizard's game and
construction menus are compiled bytecode. Tracing their shared 6502
runtime alone leaves the actual game decisions unread. `50-coverage`
now says to derive the bytecode format from its handlers, validate its
branches and describe its routines. It also says to look for executable
callbacks in level headers and patch storage, preserving each load's
meaning separately.

**A ROM call does not make the RAM beneath it disposable.** Wizard keeps
sprites under KERNAL. A flow trace follows ROM calls into those pixels
because the snapshot contains RAM. `tool-regen2000` now says to restore
their data type and explain the two meanings, excluding bytes only when
they are proved not to be authored data.
