**Look for the loaded data before splitting the work.** The flow trace of
Doctor Who and the Mines of Terror covered 26 KB, and seven agents took
all of it to 100 % in sixteen minutes. The ledger had said nothing about
another 16 KB loaded with the game, the cavern map under the KERNAL and
every sprite image, because nothing names them by address. The listing's
report of loaded data, run with both snapshots, showed them at once.
`50-coverage` now runs it before the ranges are chosen.

**A picture from the hand-over is not a picture from play.** The game
swaps in its own shapes for sixteen characters in each zone of its map,
so a map drawn from the hand-over image showed letters where the start
zone has stalactites. `70-minisite` now says to compare the character
set with a play snapshot and embed what differs.

**When the web is refused, the keys found online may be another
machine's.** The session's network refused every game site; search
summaries were all that came back, and their key list was the BBC Micro
version's. The C64's keys came from the input routine. `20-features` says
so.
