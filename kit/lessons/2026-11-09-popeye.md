## next · 2026-11-09 · Popeye (Game & Watch) · icyriver2023 with Claude

- **Name a skip helper by running it, not by its first test.** On the
  SM5A a helper that is `TM n` then `RTNS` then `RTN` returns with a skip
  when the bit is clear, since `TM` skips the `RTNS` when it is set. Four
  such helpers were named "skip if set", and every reading built on them
  came out inverted, the main loop's redraw flag among them, until a
  traced pass contradicted the names. Call each helper once with the bit
  set and once clear on `kit/gnw/sm5a.js` before naming it
  (`gnw-reference`, "Mistakes that bite").
- **Record who reads and writes each cell.** The SM5A reaches a cell
  through B, set by `LB`, `LBL`, and the row moves of `EXC`, `LDA`,
  `SBM` and `RBM`, so a text search finds few readers. Wrapping the
  model's `read` and `write` through a run of every mode gave the readers
  and writers of all 65 cells, and the jumps actually taken; it settled
  most questions a references search could not (`steps`, "references").
- **A sample that measures a 13 % rate also finds what the listing
  shows wrong everywhere.** The checker on the first sample marked 8 of
  60 comments, and beyond its sample found that every `TRS` inside a
  subroutine was listed with the wrong target. The disassembler now
  resolves those on the call table's page, and the reference says where
  it still cannot.
