## 0.0.121 · 9 October 2026 · Spy vs Spy · chunkypixel with Claude

**Build the cases a snapshot lacks with the game's own code.** The
computer player's route search runs over the room records of the
embassy in play, and the play snapshot held one level's. Rather than
test the port on that level alone, or build the other seven from the
layout tables by hand (which would test the port against the same
reading of the tables that wrote it), the test ran the game's own
embassy builder in the 6502 simulator for each level, then the game's
route search and the port side by side over every start and goal: 6,667
searches on eight layouts. Two deliberate mistakes in the port's order
of search were each caught by thousands of them. When the original
routine's input is built by another routine, call that one too.

**Time a game's "seconds" against the frames before quoting one.** The
clock shows minutes and seconds, and the code counts a hundredths digit
from 99 to 0 once a frame before taking a second off: on a 60 Hz machine
a second on the clock lasts 1.67 real seconds, and "7 minutes" last 11 min
40 s. Every duration the game states in seconds, penalties and fuses
included, stretches by the same factor. A live count settled it: in 600
frames a spy's clock lost 6 seconds. Before a page gives a time in
seconds, count the frames one of the game's seconds takes.
