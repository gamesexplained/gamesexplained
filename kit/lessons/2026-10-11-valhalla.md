## 0.0.152 · 2026-10-11 · Valhalla (ZX Spectrum) · air with Claude

- **A sample's error rate is what its checker found.** Valhalla's full
  audit re-read the 60 comments its first sample had checked, and found
  6 more wrong among the 53 the checker had passed and a second wrong
  detail in 3 of the 7 it had flagged: the checker caught about half.
  Report a sample's rate as a lower bound, and ask the checker for every
  wrong detail in a comment, not the first it meets.
- **Fix the belief, not the comment.** 16 of Valhalla's audit findings
  were one misreading of the unit `draw_ridge` counts its runs in, as 16
  of Commando's were one claim that the enemies run every frame. Each
  wrong comment a sample finds is a search for the others that say the
  same.
- **Callers come from a search.** A caller list, an "only" or a "never"
  was the commonest wrong detail in the audits of Commando, Mr. Hat,
  Mayhem in Monsterland and Valhalla: the annotator saw one caller and
  wrote it up as the rule. Annotators take them from the references
  search, or leave them out.
