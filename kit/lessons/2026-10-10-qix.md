## next · 2026-10-10 · Qix (C64) · chunkypixel

- **A code map recorded with `--under-rom` can hold the KERNAL's code.**
  Qix banks the KERNAL in to load a file between levels and after every
  game, then runs its own music player from the RAM at `$E000`. VICE's
  memmap marks execution by address, so the map came back with 307
  KERNAL addresses ($E9xx-$FFxx: LOAD, the serial routines, the vectors)
  mixed in with the player's. Nothing complained: `check_listing.py`
  would have required those bytes of tune data to be typed as code. The
  map was cleaned by tracing the player from its entries and its three
  dispatch tables, and a second map recorded on the title alone, with no
  load, confirmed which addresses the player runs.
- **Name a walk by the direction it goes straight on.** Four edge-walk
  routines were named by the first neighbour each one tests, and every
  name came out a quarter turn wrong; the independent check of the
  listing found it, not the annotation pass. When a routine steps through
  a fixed order of neighbours, find the step it takes when nothing is in
  the way, and name the routine after that.
