## 0.0.107 · 7 October 2026 · Armalyte · air with Claude

**Read a rate from the whole path to the call.** Armalyte's main loop
runs its enemy paths and wave spawning on one frame and its animation
and pod checks on the next, by a byte its interrupt flips every frame.
Every routine behind those branches was read correctly on its own and
described as running every frame, and the decoder that wrote 640 path
comments counted each step in frames where each was two. An independent
check of 80 comments found 13 wrong, ten of them this mistake. Listing
every reader of the flipped byte found the whole class at once. A rate
taken from the code needs either a count of the routine's calls against
the frames that pass or every branch between the loop's top and the
call.

**A measurement that comes out "about" the prediction is a failed
test.** The generators were described as ticking every 60, 50, 40, 30
or 18 frames, from the table. A live check measured about 460 frames for
eight ticks where 480 were predicted, and the gap was let go. The
counter is compared with the table's value and ticks on `BCS`, so it
runs from 0 to the value and each tick takes one frame more than the
table says: 61, 51, 41, 31 and 19, eight ticks in 488 frames.

**Read the punctuation from the glyphs too.** The title font's letters
and digits sat in an order easy to guess, and its punctuation was taken
by ASCII's: the full stop, comma and colon are `$2C`, `$2D` and `$2E`,
so "CREDITS." in the notes and on the page read "CREDITS:" on the
screen. The second independent check caught it.
