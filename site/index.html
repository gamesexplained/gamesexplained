Contribute

Bring your own copy of a game; nothing here hosts or accepts game binaries. Then paste one line into your agent: Clone https://github.com/gamesexplained/gamesexplained and follow kit/START.md. Or clone it yourself, read kit/START.md and AGENTS.md first, and tell your agent to follow kit/START.md. Every page has a Source tab you can check the claims against.

The kit

Every game is analysed with the same kit of rules, skills and scripts, and every game changes it. How the kit has changed, game by game.
