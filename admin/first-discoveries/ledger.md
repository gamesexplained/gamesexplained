## Partly known

- Into the Eagle's Nest (C64): [Secret: type %MAGGOTS!& as your name](https://gamesexplained.com/c64/into-the-eagles-nest/discoveries.html#the-cheat). 29 Oct 2026. [Plus/4 World](https://plus4world.powweb.com/software/Into_The_Eagles_Nest) gives a cheat with the same effects (a key in hand, unlimited ammunition, no hits) typed into the high score table of the Plus/4 port, with the name `"ANDY"`, a space, a skull and two spaces, and says that one "was already in the original game". The C64 word turned up nowhere, and the C64 tape holds no `ANDY`. Searched `"Into the Eagle's Nest" C64 cheat MAGGOTS`, `"Eagles Nest" C64 cheat highscore name maggots OR "%MAGGOTS"`, `"Eagle's Nest" C64 "POKE 24639"` and `"Into the Eagle's Nest" C64 cheats pokes tips`; [C64-Wiki](https://www.c64-wiki.com/wiki/Into_the_Eagle%27s_Nest) lists only pokes and a cracked release's trainer.
- Into the Eagle's Nest (C64): [Secret: opening a chest is safe, and shooting what is inside it is not](https://gamesexplained.com/c64/into-the-eagles-nest/discoveries.html#the-chests). 29 Oct 2026. The [manual](https://mirror.cyberbits.eu/textfiles.com/piracy/SOFTDOCS/eaglesnest.txt) (Mindscape, 1987) says a shot opens a closed chest and warns not to shoot an open one, which may hold explosives. That the contents are rolled at the moment the chest opens, three in four empty, turned up nowhere.
