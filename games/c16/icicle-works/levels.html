Icicle Works

Every room, its exits, and the toy that opens its doors.

01 · Room browser

The sixteen rooms

The maps are stored at $A800 through $C81F. A number beginning with $ is a hexadecimal address, a location in the computer's memory. Each room has 513 packed bytes plus one extra byte kept beside them. A byte holds two tile numbers. Reading its high half first gives the tiles in room order. The game loads each room into two grids that track its objects. Both grids matched this decoding in all sixteen rooms.

A single arrow means there is no door straight back between those two rooms. Rooms are arranged to keep the connections readable. The links come from the door destinations at $8F11, assigned as the game reads each map row from left to right, working down from the top. A map by Ulysses777, preserved by Plus/4 World, provides an independent visual reference.

03 · Assembly order

The toy for this room

The lists at $8F51 and $9051 choose a picture piece and its position for each pickup. These sixteen pictures use all 163 stored pieces.

Finishing a toy turns that room's remaining parcels into bonuses. Returning to a finished room starts the clock at 40. Each player has a separate record of finished rooms. The game saves that room's result when the player leaves through a door.
