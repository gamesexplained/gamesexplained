1985 · Mastertronic · Commodore 16 / Plus/4

Tutti Frutti

Six layouts reuse three sets of rules. The level number continues when the maps repeat.

The six orchard layouts

Each map takes 96 bytes. The title border starts the table at $3500, followed by the six orchards. The game reads the high half of a byte first, then the low half. Each half names one tile in a row of 16.

Loading the listing…

The original map routine at $2940 draws the ground and fruit. The square outline is the selected tile. The spawn box appears at column 8, row 7 once play begins.

The orchards repeat at a higher speed

Level 7 loads the first layout again. The difficulty tables continue through their own entries. The amount added to the enemy movement counter grows from 120 to 208. The table sets an opening reserve of zero to eight extra Acid Apples beyond the five already scheduled.

The layout counter wraps in $315F. The reserve and movement tables start at $34B0 and $34C0.
