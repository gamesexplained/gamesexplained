1985 · Mastertronic · Commodore 16 / Plus/4

Tutti Frutti

A leftover timer delays the first slide. Four well-placed apples can clear the orchard.

Bug: The first slide waits 126 ticks

The first successful push after a cold boot can leave the fruit sitting in place. The unpacker leaves $7E in the timer for the sliding fruit. The setup at $2D34 gives the fruit a speed and a direction and leaves that timer alone.

Both traces start with the same push. In the second run the timer at $033F starts at zero.

On the first block board, go up from the start and left by one tile. Hold down and fire. The cherry below has a clear space to slide into. The delay routine at $2C90 runs until the timer reaches zero. Movement starts on the next tick.

Secret: Four apples in the corners clear the board

The four Bad Apples in each block orchard can finish the round by themselves. The check at $2C78 combines the four corner tile values. When they all carry the apple block value, the round ends and the game adds 3,000 points on top of the remaining bonus.

Inspect the corner tiles in the level viewer.

Secret: Shift pauses the orchard tune

Hold Shift, or latch Shift Lock on the computer, to quiet the melody while playing. The code at $1780 switches off voice 1 and leaves its note counter where it was. Voice 2 still handles effects.
