Flowers of Crystal

Super Jim's whole map of Crystal, and every place on it, down to each square of Grubble's gum forest.

01 · The map

Super Jim's whole map of Crystal

In the game the map is 42 rows tall. The screen shows 16 rows at a time, and the arrow keys scroll it (the map). Here is the whole map at once, north at the top: the Danger Area, the Mountains, the Desert, the Lake, the Forest, the Hills, the Factory, the City and the Centre.

Point at a row to see how it is stored.

Each row printed the way the game's PROCq prints it: a new-background code, then the row that line 100 builds from the DATA at $39DA, read from the listing and drawn with BBC.drawTeletext.

03 · The gum forest

Grubble's gum forest, square by square

The forest is 8 squares across and 7 down, and the player walks it with N, S, E and W. What a square holds depends only on its two coordinates multiplied together.

The page's copy of the game's FNForest ($499E, $4A58, $4CBA). Each square is checked in the game's order: a Zap Gate, Grubble's workers, the Grabbers nearby, a capture, the gold pot.

Each square has two coordinates: g% across, 0 to 7 from the west, and h% down, 0 to 6 from the north. The game multiplies them, w% = g% × h%, and that one number decides the square. A Zap Gate is where w% is a multiple of 4 and g% is 1 to 3, and it sends the player to a place picked at random. Grubble's workers are where w% is a multiple of 5 and g% is 1 to 5, and the Grabbers are near where w% is 17 to 19 or 31 to 41; both are only warnings. The player is captured, and taken to a random square, where w% is a multiple of 3 and h% is 1 or 2. The gold pot is at g% 2, h% 1, and finding it takes the metal detector. A row or column numbered 0 makes w% 0, which counts as a multiple of every number, so the north edge has three Zap Gates and the west edge two captures.

Where the player starts depends on where they came from: the Hills, the Lake and the Factory each have a fixed square, and anywhere else gives a random one. After a capture the game checks only for the gold pot on the new square, so the player can be captured straight onto the gold. The loop has no other way out (the forest's loop).

04 · Part 2

The Danger Area, place by place

Part 2's places are numbered 1 to 10. Each visit ends with RUN, which starts the program again, and the variable Z% holds the number of the place the next visit goes to. Seven of the places offer a spell against a danger.

A lucky dip (16-20) turns into a random spell from 1 to 15, except at the castle, where it is always a flower spell. The next place is read from the DATA at $6CD5, $6CEC and $6D04, but the Globs, the flight, the three paths and the castle then set it again themselves. The stones use only a 0 from the DATA, for the whirlwind, which picks a place from 1 to 8 at random. A flower spell in the flight reads a 1 from the first list, and the flight's later dangers then treat a vanishing spell as a moving one (line 10050).
