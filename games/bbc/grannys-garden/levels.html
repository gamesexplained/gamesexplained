1983 · 4Mation Educational Resources

Granny's Garden

Every place in both programs, drawn from the screens the game showed on one play-through, and the ways between them.

01 · Part 1

The magic trees, the woodcutter's house and the Giant's garden

Part 1 is two searches. Without a password the kingdom leads to the cave, the raven and the woodcutter's house, where Esther is hidden. With her password, snow, it leads to the toadstool and across the Giant's garden to Tom.

02 · Part 2

The City of the Dragons

With Tom's password, sky, Part 2 starts in the kingdom again, and the raven takes the player to the city, where Clare is held in the baby dragons' cave and Anna is found at the gates.

03 · The Land of Mystery

The Land of Mystery, on the game's own map

With Clare and Anna's password, river, the raven brings the player to the Land of Mystery. This is the map the game draws before every journey, with the red cross where the player starts, and each of its five places can be chosen from it.

The map, as the page draws it from the 1,000 bytes jsbeeb showed on arriving. Choose a place on it, or from the buttons.

A journey is chosen by where the player is and where they go. The game adds the two places' numbers, forest 1, cottage 2, lake 3, castle 4 and hill 5, and the sum decides what happens on the road, so two different roads with the same sum behave alike (every road is known by the sum of its two ends).
