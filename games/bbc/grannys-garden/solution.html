1983 · 4Mation Educational Resources

Granny's Garden

Every answer that finds the six children, taken from one play-through of both programs in an emulator. Press the space bar wherever the screen says (space bar).

01 · The answers

Every answer, in order

There are four searches, two in each program. Each ends with a password, and the next search starts by asking for it.

Esther

T Y a1 N Y Y Y Y Y Y Fig Y N Stairs Apple N Cupboard N N Cupboard Y Green S

Tom

T Y a1 Y Y Snow Butterfly Snail Worm Bee Spider

Clare and Anna

Y Sky Y Chips B L O C S

Jessica and Daniel

Y River Cottage G Y Forest Cottage K Hill Y Y Lake Pompom N N Castle Y Lake Y Y Y Forest Y

Most answers are recognised by a few letters inside them, so a misspelt answer is often taken (typing an answer). The magic tree is chosen at random unless T is typed first, and a wrong guess is only asked again.

On this disc, Part 1 started with CHAIN"GRAN1" runs out of memory before Tom can be found (the bug). Typing LOAD"GRAN1" and then GOTO3280 runs the program's own move down to &0E00, where there is room, and the play-through below was made that way. Part 2 runs to the end from CHAIN"GRAN2".
