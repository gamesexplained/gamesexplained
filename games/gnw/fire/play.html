1981 · Nintendo

Fire: Play

The whole unit, clock and alarm included, written again in JavaScript routine by routine, and run in lockstep with the chip's own program in the tests.

01 · Play

Bounce the people to the ambulance

The unit starts as a fresh battery leaves it, every segment lit. Press Time for the clock, or Game A or Game B to play: the game starts when you let go.

Keys: ← → move the firemen; A Game A, B Game B, T Time, L Alarm. Watch starts a game of Game A or B, chosen at random, for the computer to play; Computer plays hands the game in progress to it. At each step the computer tries pressing nothing, left or right, then again at the next step, on copies of this port run a few seconds on, and keeps the plan that loses no one and scores most. A press of left or right takes the game back.

Each press of left or right moves the firemen one place, under one of the three nets. A person is caught if the firemen are under him when he comes down above a net, and a press made just after he lands, before the next part of the path moves, still catches him. Hold Time in the clock to see the alarm's time, and hold a game's button to see its best score.

02 · The port

The chip's program, routine by routine

This page does not run the game's own code. Each routine of the listing is a JavaScript function of its own, on the game's own 65 cells of memory, with the chip's registers kept where one routine hands them to the next. The port charges each instruction its length in cycles, as the chip runs a byte a cycle, so its clock matches the chip's to the cycle. That matters because the jumps take their chance from the chip's divider and the clock's seconds.

The test beside this page, test_play.js, runs the port in lockstep with the game's code on the kit's model of the chip, which is held to MAME instruction for instruction. At every pass, every pass of the wait before a step, every return from drawing the screen and every reading of the buttons, the two must agree on all 65 cells and on the cycles since power on, through games played by this page's computer player and through the states play seldom reaches: the bonus, the top speed, 999 going on to 000, the alarm, the test pads and the time being set.
