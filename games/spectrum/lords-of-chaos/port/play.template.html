The whole game, playable in the browser from where its loader hands over: SELECT CONTROLS, the wizard designer, the three scenarios loaded from the cassette, one to four wizards, the computer's own wizard and creatures, every spell, battle and treasure, the portal and the end of turn menu, with saving and loading to a tape file. Every routine is ported from the game's own Z80 code and was run beside it until the two agreed.

  • Tap a key in this list to press it in the game.

  • HOW MANY PLAYERS? takes 1 2 3 or 4; scenario 3 is for one player and does not ask

  • Loading: the cassette holds DESIGNER., 1.GAM, 2.GAM and 3.GAM, then whatever is saved or added, and the game loads the first file whose name starts with the letter typed. It winds back to the start after the last file; a search that finds nothing stops at the end of the tape. Hold Space to stop a search
  • Saving (SAVE GAME at the end of a turn, SAVE WIZARD in the designer) puts the files on the cassette and offers them as a .tap file to keep
  • SELECT CONTROLS, and every menu: Q A move the bar, Space chooses
  • In play: Q A O P move the cursor, Space opens the menu for the square under it
  • The arrow keys and Ctrl are the controls chosen at the start: the keys above, a Kempston joystick or a Sinclair one

The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the Spectrum would show it, at the Spectrum's own pace: the interrupt animates the creatures and slides the bars every frame, and the computer's wizard takes as long to think as it does on the machine. The sound is the port's writes to the beeper, each edge at the T-state the game's delay loops make it.
