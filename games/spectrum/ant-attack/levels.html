The whole city and every starting place for the ten rescues.

Antescher

Every rescue uses this city. Rotate the view to expose walls, or switch to Plan view and inspect any column. The marker shows the selected person’s starting position.

In Plan view, click a column to read its six occupancy bits.

Positions come from BASIC DATA lines 1001–1091, beginning at $6DC3. After a successful rescue, the previous remaining time modulo four chooses the next location variant. A retry keeps that choice.
