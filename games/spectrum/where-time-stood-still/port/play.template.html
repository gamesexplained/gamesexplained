The whole game, playable in the browser: the title and the control selection, the plateau's sixteen areas, the party of four, the creatures, the menus and the music. Every routine is ported from the game's own Z80 code and was run beside it until the two agreed.

Loading the game's data…
  • 2, 0, Y on the control selection: the arrow keys as a Kempston joystick
  • ← → ↑ ↓ walk the leader; with fire held, run
  • Right Ctrl or Right Alt: fire, which shoots the way the leader faces
  • Space the menus: the arrows move the pointer and fire picks
  • 1, 3 or 4 on the control selection: keys of your own, Interface 2 or cursor, played on the Spectrum's keys
  • Shift is CAPS SHIFT, Left Ctrl SYMBOL SHIFT and Enter ENTER

The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the Spectrum 128 would show it, at the 128's own pace: the game draws a new view six to eight times a second, into the screen it is not showing, and then shows it. The sound is the port's writes to the AY chip and the beeper, played by the site's model of them as each frame is shown.
