# What the port corrected in the listing

Porting the game routine by routine for the Play tab (`play.html`), with each routine checked in
step with the game's own code (`test/lockstep.js`), showed 11 of the listing's comments wrong. They
are corrected in `parts/main/symbols.json` and `parts/bank-03/symbols.json`, and the listings
rebuilt. game.json's `accuracy` counts them in its `play` row.

| Address | Part | Was | Is |

Channel A's note is taken from $729C by the step's low nibble, B's and C's from $71B2 by its low three bits. |
