1988 · Ocean

Where Time Stood Still

The plateau is sixteen areas in a four by four grid, each 2048 pixels across and 1024 down, and the game holds one of them at a time.

01 · The plateau

The whole plateau, one character to a dot

Each area here is drawn from its map at an eighth of its size: one dot for each character, as bright as the character has ink. Press an area to draw it whole below.

 

Area 0 is at the top left and the numbers run along each row, so walking off the east edge of an area goes to the next number and walking off the south edge goes four on (bank 3 $DB0B). Play starts in area 13. Areas 12 and 15 are blank because the game holds no scenery record for them: bank 7's words for them at $DB00 point into the ROM.

02 · One area

Each area, drawn as the game draws the view

The view on the play screen is a window 28 characters across and 18 down onto one of these. Point at the picture to read the block and the character under the pointer.

· ·

 

Each map is what the game's own area loader ($87CF) leaves in bank 0, run on the kit's Z80 from the listings. The loader unpacks the area's grid of blocks from bank 7 ($8A9B) and stamps the scenery pieces from bank 6 over it ($899B). This page then draws every character as $9C10 and $9C4D do. test_maps.js runs the game's own view drawing ($A012) at the start of play and at a place in every area, and holds this drawing to it byte for byte. The loader's map for area 13 is the same as the one in memory at the start of play in the emulator.
