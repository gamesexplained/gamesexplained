The picture that comes up while the tape loads, drawn from the tape's own bytes at the tape's own pace.

01 · The loading picture

VALHALLA is loading

Before the picture, the tape's BASIC program clears the screen to black and prints "VALHALLA is loading." in white across the middle. The picture's bitmap comes in over it unseen, and as it does the message disappears a line of pixels at a time, because the picture has nothing in the cells it is printed in. After 31 seconds the colours sweep down the screen a row of cells at a time: a Viking helmet in yellow, the Legend and Movisoft logos, and VALHALLA in tall white letters.

The tape's own bytes, at its own pace from the picture's first byte, 21 seconds into the tape. The blue and yellow stripes are the Spectrum's own loader at work: it turns the border over at every edge of the tape's signal, so each stripe is one pulse, a thin one a 0 and a thick one a 1.

The picture takes 35.1 seconds. There is no title screen: the picture stays up while the machine code and the game load after it, and the game clears it when it starts (How it works).
