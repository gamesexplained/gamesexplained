01 · The loading picture

Freescape, introduced in the dark

The tape's first fast block is a picture: DRILLER in yellow and white, the words INTRODUCING FREESCAPE, and a green tower on a green plain under a blue planet, with a laser beam striking a red block. The tape's BASIC program has made ink, paper and border black, so for 22 seconds the bitmap comes in unseen. Then the colours sweep down the screen a row of cells at a time, in under three seconds.

The tape's own bytes, at its own pace from the picture's first byte, 28 seconds into the tape. The blue and yellow stripes are the loader's, in the colours the Spectrum's own loader uses: it turns the border over at every edge of the tape's signal, so each stripe is one pulse, a thin one a 0 and a thick one a 1.

The picture takes 24.6 seconds, about 280 bytes a second, half as fast again as the Spectrum's own loader. It stays up while the game loads behind it. It is not the screen the game plays on: the tape's last block is another 6,912 bytes, the cockpit, which loads over the picture just before the game starts.
