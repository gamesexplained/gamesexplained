1985 · Nintendo

Balloon Fight

What the code does that the booklet never says: a reset that always wipes the scores, a sound driver living inside the ranking table, and a thunder that makes no sound.

01 · The reset button

Bug: the reset button never keeps the top scores

The code to keep them is there. The sound driver defeats it on the title screen's first frame.

At reset the game compares three bytes at $07FA with the letters HAL ($C027). When they match, RAM is left alone: the three top scores and Balloon Trip's table of fifty survive the Reset button, and only a power cycle clears them. When they do not match, everything is cleared, the tables are filled afresh, and the three letters are written ($C06C) so that the next reset will find them.

The next reset never finds them. The sound driver keeps a flag of its own at $07FA, and the routine that silences every channel zeroes it ($F7B8). That routine runs whenever a tune starts, and a tune starts on the title screen's very first frame (the next discovery says which and why). So by the time anyone can press Reset the first letter of HAL is a 0, the comparison fails, and the scores go. Tested in the emulator on 19 October 2026: a top score poked to 99,990 came back as 10,000 after a reset from the title screen and after one from play, and the three bytes read 00 41 4C in every snapshot taken after power-on.

02 · The ranking table

Bug: the sound driver keeps its counters inside the Balloon Trip ranking table

Ranks 46 to 50 hold the effects' timers, and the table starts the bonus tune on the title screen.

Balloon Trip's table of fifty scores runs from $0700 to $07F9, five decimal digits each. The sound driver's working bytes run from $07E0 to $07FE: the spark effect's length at $07E1, the music's loop flag at $07E8, the fall counter at $07FB, the fish effect's periods at $07FD. Those addresses are the digits of ranks 46 to 50. After a game the bottom of the table holds frame counts and APU periods where digits should be; a fresh table's rank 47 is 2,000 points, and after the first frame of the title screen it is 0.

The loop flag is the reason the title screen starts a tune. A fresh table puts the digit 2 at $07E8, the thousands of rank 47's 2,000. The music driver reads that byte as "the bonus tune reached its end, start it again" ($FB25), starts the bonus round's tune, and clears the byte. The APU is switched off on the title screen, so nobody hears it; the start also runs the silencing routine, which is what zeroes the HAL signature above. The table's last entry spills the same way: inserting a score moves every entry below it down by five bytes, and the fiftieth moves into $07FA to $07FE, the signature and the driver's bytes ($C5A1).

The last six entries of the ranking table and the signature as the emulator held them: filled at power-on ($C043), after the title screen's first frame, and after a Balloon Trip that ended on 200 points. Orange marks bytes that are not digits; blue the two that the driver and the ranking share on the title screen.

03 · The thunder

Bug: the lightning flashes, and the thunder makes no sound

A request the game sets and the sound driver never reads.

Half a second before a cloud throws a spark it flashes through four colours, and at the flash the game sets bit 3 of the second sound-request byte ($C852): the thunder. The driver's dispatch for that byte tests bits 0, 1, 2 and 4 in turn ($FA4E) and shifts bit 3 past without a branch ($FA59). Nothing else reads it. Every other request bit the game sets has a routine; this one has a flash and silence. Recorded from a paused game with the bit poked in, the driver wrote nothing to the APU for 90 frames.

04 · Balloon Trip's quiet stretch

Secret: one of Balloon Trip's generators makes nothing at all

The eighth difficulty step's generator is a single RTS.

Balloon Trip picks the generator that fills the sky from a table of five by a difficulty step that rises every 32 runs ($C3BF). The fifth generator's address is $C45E, which is the RTS at the end of the generator before it ($C3B5). The table puts it at step 7, so after about 55 seconds the sky empties for 32 runs, 256 pixels of scrolling, before sparks alone and then balloons with sparks come at step 8 and 9. Whether the breather was meant or the table was meant to name another routine, the bytes do not say.

05 · The flap limit

Secret: holding B flaps exactly as fast as the game allows, every nine frames

Tapping A faster than that gains nothing, and a tap shorter than three frames can be lost.

The controls are read every third frame, and after a flap the next one is not accepted until the actor's animation has counted its frame back round to 0, three readings later ($EC75). B held flaps on every reading it is allowed to, one flap every nine frames; A flaps once per press and is subject to the same limit. Counted in the emulator with the player in the air: B held for 120 frames gave 14 flaps, A tapped every 12 frames 10 of 10, and 60 two-frame taps only 10. The How it works page runs the same arithmetic.

06 · The new player's shield

Secret: a new player is untouchable until he presses anything

The shield lasts ten seconds, or one press of A, B, left or right.

A player placed at the start of a phase or after a life lost is marked invulnerable ($F393): birds pass through him, sparks miss him, and he is drawn flashing. The mark lasts 200 counts of three frames, ten seconds ($E6C6), but the controls clear it on the first reading that finds A, B, left or right pressed ($EC45). Standing still at the start of a hard phase is therefore safe for ten seconds, and a bird that lands on the player during them does nothing.

07 · The phases that repeat

Secret: phase 9 is phase 4 with two propellers, and phase 13 is phase 4 again

Sixteen layout records, twelve of them distinct, and a wrap to the fourth.

The layout for phase 9 ($DBCA) names the same bird list and platform tables as phase 4 ($DB77), with two propellers added; phase 12's record is phase 6's ($DBE1) by the pointer table itself ($DB2A). After the sixteenth record the index goes back to 4 rather than 0 ($F358), so from phase 13 the game cycles phases 4 to 12 and their bonus rounds. The birds keep getting faster through the difficulty tables, which are indexed by the phase number and top out at phase 33.
