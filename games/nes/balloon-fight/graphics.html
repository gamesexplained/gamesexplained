1985 · Nintendo

Balloon Fight

Every picture the players, the birds, the fish and the sky are drawn with, played at the game's own speed by its drawing routines. A player in the middle of a flap and a bird with its parachute half open are the same six sprites, laid out from a different line of one table.

01 · The balloonists

The balloonist with two balloons, with one, and with none

Each player is six sprites, the console's small pictures that move over the background, each 8 by 8 pixels. What the player is doing and how many balloons it has pick which six.

Speed

Two balloons

A balloon bursting

One balloon

No balloons

Each card runs the game's routines for one player, frame by frame, as the game's loop runs them, and draws what they leave in the sprite table: actor_animate ($EA18) steps the animation, actor_think ($EBC4) reads the controller where a card needs one, and draw_actor ($E3A4) lays out the six sprites. An address such as $E3A4 is a numbered place in the console's memory, written in hexadecimal (base 16). The routines are the Play tab's, written again in JavaScript and checked against the game's own code. The player stays in one place: the routines that move it are left out. Player 2 is the same pictures in the second of the four sprite palettes, the sets of three colours a sprite can use.

The animation has four steps, and it moves on only when the controls are read, every third frame ($E6B8). A flap starts at step 1 and runs to step 3, and with B held the next flap starts as soon as the last one ends, so the arms go round every 9 frames. Gliding and standing step only when the frame counter is a multiple of 8 at the same time, which happens once every 24 frames. Running steps every 3. Skidding and turning have four steps, but the four are the same picture.

A balloon that bursts is drawn for one turn of the controls, three frames, in the pose the player had when it was hit (player_burst_actions, $F053). Then the player carries on with one balloon. The flashing at the start of a life swaps the player for a pale copy. It ends when its timer runs out, or sooner, at the first press of a button ($EC45). A spark makes the player flicker between its own picture and a pale, shocked one, for 64 frames, about a second.

02 · The birds

The birds: one set of drawings in pink, green and white

A bird's type picks its palette, and nothing else about the picture. The pink, green and white birds share every drawing.

Speed

The same routines as the players', run for a bird. The flapping bird flies low on the screen, where its steering, steer_actor ($E6E9), holds B down. The pumping card starts as phase 3 starts, with the waits of that phase (enemy_inflate_rates and enemy_launch_delays, $F3BA and $F3C3), and plays until the bird has taken off. The bird keeps its colour when it takes off, as a bird does that has not been hit.

A bird on the ground at the start of a phase stands for 88 frames, then pumps its balloon up in four steps, 48 frames apart, and takes off 280 frames, under five seconds, after play starts. In phases 1 and 2 the waits are shorter, and it is in the air after 147 frames. A popped bird is drawn with no balloon for three frames, then with its parachute half open for three, then under the open parachute until it lands. A bird that has lost its parachute falls with its arms and legs out.

03 · The water

The fish, the splash, and the bubble a sunk bird leaves

The fish is drawn as a ninth actor, by the same routine as the players and the birds, from its own table of layouts.

Speed

The fish card runs fish_update ($C6F9) every frame beside a player gliding low over the water. The splash is draw_splash ($E587), and the bubble is draw_actor_bubble ($E4D5), both run every frame. The bubble stays in one place here; in the game it drifts up and sways.

The fish rises a pixel a frame, through three pictures. On its 24th frame it bites, if the player is still within reach. From then on fish_draw ($C5C3) draws the player at the fish's mouth, and on the 32nd frame takes the player away. The fish sinks through three more pictures and is gone. The splash is four pictures, four frames each. A bird that falls into the water comes back up as a bubble, which shifts down a pixel for 32 frames out of every 128 and right a pixel for another 32. A player who touches it bursts it for 500 points.

04 · The sky

Lightning, the bonus balloons, the propellers and the scores

The rest of what moves: the lightning a cloud throws, the balloons of the bonus round and Balloon Trip, the propellers, and the points that pop up.

Speed

Balloons

Scores

The lightning card runs sparks_update ($C8B7) every frame for a spark thrown down and to the right, held still once it is free. The balloon is balloon_draw ($CE2F), the propeller propellers_update ($CB74), and the scores show_popup ($D871). The propeller is drawn with background tiles, which the game sends to the picture chip through its queue at $0300. The balloons' colours are the round's, from bonus_palette_ptrs ($D46A).

The bolt grows out of the cloud in five steps, 8 frames apart, and its tiles change every 2 frames to make it flicker. The spark comes free in the bolt's last step and turns through its tiles a frame at a time. The other three directions use the same tiles, flipped. Balloon Trip's sparks use the first four entries of the same list of tiles, a new one every other frame.

A balloon is three sprites. The bottom one, the string, swings from side to side in a cycle of 128 frames. Balloon Trip's balloons are green, the first bonus round's colours, and the pointer on the title screen is the same balloon. A spinning propeller turns an eighth of a turn every other frame; its two blades look the same after half a turn, so four pictures make the whole spin. A score that pops up is two sprites, above the player who scored it, in that player's colours.
