The key sequence that opens the game’s own room browser.

Secret: ESPILCE opens a room browser

During play, enter ESPILCE, pressing and releasing each key in that order. Numbers beginning with $ are hexadecimal. The seven key codes at memory location $3AE9 are $0E $0D $29 $21 $2A $14 $0E. They spell ECLIPSE backwards.

The successful path hides sprites (the small pictures drawn over the background), restores two spare lives, and enters the room browser at $3B23. Joystick directions change the room number by the positive or negative steps stored at $3AF0. Fire returns to the game. The range check accepts room IDs from $15 through $A7, although the room drawing directory ends at $64.

Real keyboard events, delivered through the C64’s rows and columns of key switches, passed the comparison loop at $3B0A and $3B11. Executing one processor instruction at a time followed the spare-life store at $3B1D. The larger browser range is traced in the code; its out-of-directory effects are left open. The Maps viewer stays within the stored directory.
