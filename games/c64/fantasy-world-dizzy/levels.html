01 · Rooms

The complete room directory

Each room has a stored number, its ID. Moving horizontally into the next room adds or subtracts one from that number. A vertical transition changes it by sixteen, with special cases in the movement code. The selector includes 101 stored drawing streams: lists of pictures to place. They include fragments and title art. Objects use the five initial tables at $C400 through $C69D, followed by the adjustments at $29E4. A $ introduces a hexadecimal number; these numbers locate the data and instructions in memory.

With objects hidden, the viewer runs the scenery-drawing routine at $2E79. With objects shown, it uses the room-entry drawing path at $2F22. The view does not claim a route through every selected stream.
