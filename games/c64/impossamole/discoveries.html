1990 · Gremlin Graphics · C64

Impossamole

A shopkeeper who loses count when you wiggle, and a high-score table that reaches into the music.

Secret: waggle the joystick and the shopkeeper takes less

Every level has a shop. Monty walks up to an item, its price appears, and fire buys it if he has that much money. The item is his at once. The money leaves afterwards, one coin a frame, fifty times a second, so a 125-coin shatterbomb takes two and a half seconds to pay for.

On each of those frames the game reads the joystick. If it is pushed left or right, and not the same way as the last push, that frame's coin stays in Monty's pocket. Waggle the stick and some of the price is never taken. Change direction on every frame and the item is free. The payment always lasts as many frames as the price, so a fast hand saves money, not time. The instructions don’t mention it.

Pick an item to buy it.

Pick an item, then press Left and Right in turn, or the arrow keys. The purse starts full each time: 250 coins is the most Monty can carry.

Bug: a cheat name knocks the tune out of key

When a new name goes into the high-score table, the game checks it against six cheat names (below). The check counts matching letters, and it keeps that count in a byte that belongs to the music: the first step of one of the tune’s instruments, the number of semitones that step adds to each note. The byte is 0 when the game loads, and the check leaves its count behind.

A cheat name matches all eight letters and leaves 8. From then on that instrument plays every note eight semitones high, while the other two voices stay in key. It is a part on the third voice that first comes in 53 seconds into the tune, which plays on the title screen and, with music chosen, during play. It stays wrong until the next high-score name is checked, or the machine is switched off.

Both players start 50 seconds into the tune. Mute voices 1 and 2 to hear the third on its own.

Ordinary names can do it too. The last cheat name checked is JUGGLERS, and when nothing matches, the count left behind is how many letters the new name shares with JUGGLERS from the start. JIM leaves 1, JUDY 2, JUGGLER 7. Any name that begins with J pushes that part up.

Six cheat names hide in the high-score table

Typed into the high-score table, six names change the next game. Each must fill all eight places, so the spaces around HEINZ. count. The name entry starts as eight full stops, which a short name keeps.

The joystick can pick a full stop, A to Z, a space and a question mark.

NameThe next game
LUMBAJAKA longer energy bar: its size is 17 instead of 9.
 HEINZ. Monty starts at full strength, 3 instead of 1.
COMMANDOA weapon from a shop or the floor never wears off.
ANNFRANKOne spare energy bar, used when the first runs out.
OOCHOUCHGround that hurts, such as the sea, still bounces Monty up, but no longer harms him.
JUGGLERSFood restores twice as much energy.

Only the newest cheat counts. Each one first puts the energy bar, strength and spare back to normal, then makes its own change, so two cheats never add up. An ordinary name leaves the last cheat in force. In memory the names are kept with every letter moved 69 places along, and moved back one at a time as they are compared.

Cheat lists for the PC version give some of these names, and they work the same way here.
