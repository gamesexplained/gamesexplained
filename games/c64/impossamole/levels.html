1990 · Gremlin Graphics · C64

Impossamole

Each world is one long strip of scenery, cut into areas that Monty moves between by leaving the top or bottom of the screen.

Five worlds, end to end

A world is stored as one strip 1,696 characters long and 24 high. It is built from blocks, each a square of four by four characters, stacked six to a column, so the same block can appear in many places. The strip holds several areas side by side. In some places Monty can walk off the top or the bottom of the screen, and the game jumps the view to another stretch of the strip and brings him in at the other edge. The shop is the last screen of every world.

Click an exit arrow to follow it.

Scroll sideways along the world. Creatures and items are drawn where they appear, and most creatures then move. An arrow marks an exit, pointing the way Monty leaves the screen; click it to jump to where it leads.
