1990 · Gremlin Graphics · C64

Impossamole

Every world loads its own creatures, pickups and scenery. Monty and the shop’s goods are the only faces that travel with him.

The cast of each world

Each world brings its own set of 160 sprite shapes, and short lists of shape numbers make them move. Here is every creature, pickup and shot a world can put on the screen, moving as it does in the game.

Placed in the world

Made during play: shots, sparks and the like

Monty

Monty’s animations come from a list of his own, picked by what he is doing and which way he faces. Carrying a gun swaps in a second set of shapes with the weapon in his paws.

Shapes the code picks itself

The rest of a world’s sprite shapes are not in any animation list. The game’s code picks them directly: Monty’s death, his shots, the shatterbomb’s blast and some creatures’ attacks, along with any spares. Each takes its colour from the code that uses it, so they are drawn here in white.

The scenery’s characters

The scenery is built from a world’s 256 characters, each eight by eight pixels, shown here for the world chosen at the top of the page. A character’s colour decides whether it is drawn in two colours at full width, or in four colours with pixels twice as wide.
