03 · The addresses

  
Where each map, table, and list sits in memory

  
Every byte the page draws from is in the listing of the level's own part. A test holds the page's copy against that listing.

  

    

    
The listing of level 2 holds 192 of the 2,048 bytes of its character shapes and 64 of its 256 colours, and the listing of level 3 holds neither, so the drawings of those two levels rest on the first-screen check alone for shapes and colours. The tile tables, the maps, and the object lists are held byte for byte against the listings. The game sets bit 3 of every character colour when a level starts, and level 3 copies eight of its map cells to others; the page holds the bytes as they stand in play.
