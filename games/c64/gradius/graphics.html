1987 · Konami

Gradius

Every sprite shape the game stores, 96 of them, from the Vic Viper to the last boss, drawn from the listing.

01 · The sprites

Ninety-six shapes in six kilobytes

The sprite shapes fill $2000-$37FF, 64 bytes each, numbered $80 to $DF by where they lie.

Each group of shapes is drawn from the listing's bytes, under the name the listing gives it. A group of more than one shape steps through them on the page, one shape every eight frames, to show them in turn; the game's own order and rate vary with the mover. Each mover chooses its sprite's colour, size and whether it is multicolour (spr_colour, spr_big, spr_multi), so the buttons set those for every shape at once. Multicolour uses the two shared colours stage_setup sets, grey ($D025 = 12) and white ($D026 = 1).

The video chip finds a sprite's shape from a pointer byte at the end of the screen, $07F8 to $07FF: shape n lies at n × 64 in the chip's 16 KB bank, so shapes $80-$DF are $2000-$37FF. The game keeps a table of eight sprites of its own at $4074 and copies it to the chip each frame (draw_sprites, $40C4), each slot's shape being its base shape plus an animation step (spr_frame, spr_anim). Slot 0 is always the Vic Viper.

Most of the moving things are sprites, but not all. The fliers in formation, the rushers, the shots, the lasers, the missiles, the capsules and the explosions are characters, drawn into the screen and moved a cell at a time. Only eight sprites can be on a line, so the game saves them for the ship, its Options and the larger enemies.

02 · The last boss

A boss drawn one dot at a time

The last boss's four shapes are emptied as the fight starts and drawn in again from a picture stored elsewhere, 25 dots a frame.

The picture at brain_picture ($9C49), 256 bytes laid out as the four shapes $BC-$BF, drawn double size in brown (colour 9) as place_brain sets them. The slider shows the picture as it would stand after that many frames if the dots went in byte by byte, 25 a frame (grow_brain, $9985); the order add_dot picks its dots in is not modelled here.

clear_brain ($993B) empties the shapes at $2F00, so the boss starts invisible. Each frame, grow_brain calls add_dot 25 times, and each call copies one bit of the picture into the shapes. After 2,048 dots, 82 frames, the boss is whole and the fight begins.
