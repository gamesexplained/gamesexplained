About this page

The copy studied

The pages describe Domark's PAL disk of the game. Its main file is a machine saved with Trilogic's Expert freezer cartridge and packed by the cartridge's own restart routine: the disk was mastered that way. The listing on the Source tab is built from that memory, resumed at the game's first instruction, $0A03. A second listing, the overlay, holds the 2 KB of code the game swaps in at $4000 for the shop, the messages, and the high scores, over sprite shapes that share those addresses.

No game image is hosted here.

Original game credits

The game's credits screen names Neil Harding for the code and Tony West for the graphics, under the logo of The Kremlin, and Domark as the publisher. The music is by Matt Furniss, who is not named on the screen.23
