Commodore 64 · 1990 · Domark

The Spy Who Loved Me

Every stretch of road, water and sea bed the Lotus drives over, drawn from the game's own maps and building blocks.

01 · The maps

Four maps, read from the bottom up

The scenery scrolls down the screen and the car drives up it. Each map is drawn here as one long strip with its start at the bottom, where the stage opens. Pick a stage; the strip scrolls in its box.

A map is a list of rows, two bytes to a row. Each byte names a block of 16 by 4 characters, one for the left half of the play area and one for the right (map_blocks, 64 bytes a block). The game reads the list backwards, from the stage's last row towards its first, and builds the next character row of the blocks above the top of the screen each time the scenery has moved down by eight pixels (scroll_step). Every character takes its colour from a table of 240 (char_colours); the sea bed instead paints every character the same colour.

The same counter that steps through the map also drives the enemies: each stage has a list of what appears at which distance (spawn_from_list), and some entries in it are orders that send the map back to an earlier row.
