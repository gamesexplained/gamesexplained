Commodore 64 · 1990 · Domark

The Spy Who Loved Me

A joystick in port 2 drives, steers and fires; two keys pause the game and choose between music and sound effects.

01 · The joystick

Port 2: drive, steer and fire

The game reads the joystick in port 2 (joystick_car reads $DC00). The manual gives no keys for the 8-bit versions and says only "follow on screen instructions".1

StageUp and downLeft and rightFire
The roads and the waterMove the car up or down the screen, which sets the speed; up gathers pace a step every other pass, down slows at once (stick_up, stick_down)Steer, building up sideways speed, which dies away when the stick is let go (stick_left, stick_right)Fires every weapon that has ammunition, and lays smoke while held
The sea bed and the open seaMove the car up and down at up to 4 pixels a frameMove the car sideways at up to 4 pixels a frameAs above

How sharply the car can turn depends on how fast it is going: at each speed the sideways speed has a limit, so a slow car only drifts across the road (x_speed_limits). On the sea bed and the open sea the speed is fixed and the stick moves the car freely in every direction, easing back to a stop when it is let go (joystick_free). A shot cannot be fired again for four passes, so holding fire gives a steady stream (fire).

While the car is being taken into the Q-truck, while it spins, and while it flies over a ramp, the stick does nothing: the game locks the controls until the move is over (controls_on).

02 · The keyboard

SPACE pauses, C= picks the sound

The credits screen names two keys, and the game reads both once a pass (read_keyboard). SPACE pauses: the game waits for the key to be let go, pressed again and let go again before it carries on. C= switches between the James Bond theme and the sound effects (music or effects). RESTORE does nothing: the game points the interrupt it causes at a bare return (nmi_ignore).

03 · The high scores

Three letters with the stick

After "game over" the game shows a table of nine scores (high_scores). If the score beats one of them, the lower entries move down a place and the new one goes in with the name AAA (game_over_scores). Up and down step the letter under the cursor through A to Z, round from Z to A, and right fixes it and moves to the next of the three. The table lives only in memory: it is lost when the machine is switched off.
