Discoveries

Secret: a switch that would stop Broadway’s clock

The walk down Broadway can be lost two ways: the clock reaches twelve, or the slime runs out. One byte turns both off, and the game never sets it.

Before the game ends the walk at twelve o’clock, it looks at that byte. It looks again before a fresh fireball, or a ghost reaching the statue, takes a row from the slime supply. With the byte set, the clock carries on past twelve and the fireballs and ghosts take nothing. The byte is zero when Broadway loads, on the original disk as on the cracked copies, and Broadway’s code only ever reads it. A switch like this was probably for the team’s own testing, so they could ride the whole street without losing.

Broadway’s panel, with the clock moved on by the game’s own clock routine, faster than in play. Turn the switch on and run it again.

Secret: a third word for the panel, DAZED

In the museum, the equipment panel writes OK or DEAD under each Ghostbuster. The game also keeps a third word, DAZED, ready to go beside them.

Each word is drawn from two sprites, and DAZED is stored right next to the other two, in the same style. The code that chooses the word for each Ghostbuster only ever picks OK or DEAD, so DAZED never reaches the screen. Perhaps a Ghostbuster was once meant to be knocked out for a while before getting back up.

The three words as the museum stores them, drawn from their sprites: the two the panel shows, and DAZED.
