# Broadway alternate disk

`work/entry.vsf` is the Jondi/Censor disk handover at `$8009`, reached from the forced second-story route in `../../orientation.md`. The decompressor output matches all 56,695 stable bytes in that snapshot. Compared with the Censor handover, the ranges `$2B00–$CEFF`, `$E000–$EF76` and `$F000–$F7FF` match exactly and are counted in the Broadway part. The alternate backdrop and coordinate data at `$0900–$2AFF` differ and are accounted for here.

After 32 neutral frames from entry, the machine was waiting for fire at `$CA03`. Two fire frames reached the game-loop wait at `$82A2`; that state is `work/play.vsf`. The `$0900–$1F7F` backdrop and `$2000–$2AFF` coordinate data are unchanged between these two snapshots. The Levels tab can show the alternate backdrop using the shared character set; the coordinate data includes the lookup views described below.

The code at `$AD6B/$ADEE` and its selector tables match the Censor handover. Their views beginning in `$2000–$2AFF` are described at their addresses in this part; they match between the alternate entry and play snapshots. `test_broadway_curve.js` checks three indices of each such view through the shared processor code. The eight 8-byte gaps between those views each contain a selector byte followed by the ASCII text `newline`; they are separators outside the coordinate lookup views. Exhaustive read traces over all 32 selectors and 256 phases, for 8,192 calls per dataset, confirmed that the coordinate reader does not consume these tags.

The alternate load produces 56,695 stable bytes. Its `$0900–$2AFF` output is in this listing; `$2B00–$CEFF`, `$E000–$EF76` and `$F000–$F7FF` match the Censor load-time snapshot and are recorded in the Broadway listing. That later play listing differs from the alternate load-time state at 1,793 of these shared addresses. The 128 bytes at `$1F80–$1FFF` are zero in the captured alternate load.
