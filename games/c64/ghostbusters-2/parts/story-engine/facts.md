# Restored story engine

The snapshot `work/entry.vsf` stops at `$1000` just after the forced airshaft-success route copied `$D000–$DBFF` into `$1000–$1BFF`. At that stop the two ranges match byte for byte. The same transition copied `$DC00–$DFFF` into `$0400–$07FF`; that lower range holds the loader and is not included in this engine part. The route is recorded in `../../orientation.md`.

Each of the second-story, third-story and ending snapshots differs from this copied engine in exactly 88 positions: `$1394`, `$145E`, `$1903`, `$1905–$1906`, `$19A3`, `$1A1B–$1A1C`, and all 80 bytes of the two text buffers at `$1A67–$1AB6`. `$1394` is the high byte of a self-modified store; `$1A1B/$1A1C` are the text-reader pointer operand. The later parts count the shared engine here once and exclude their restored copies from their own coverage.

The entry snapshot's `$1000–$1BFF` matches the stash source at `$D000–$DBFF` byte for byte. Existing descriptions of the copied routines and data were therefore carried to their executing addresses. The listing marks the display routines' I/O context separately from the routines that select RAM under I/O. The final copied bytes at `$1B80–$1BFF` are a retained 128-byte fragment described below; a separate gameplay consumer remains unresolved.

The isolated byte at `$11DD` is `$12`, immediately after an unconditional jump to `$11F3`. The bytes at `$1A37–$1A66` form 24 little-endian addresses from `$0400` to `$0798`, 40 bytes apart. A consumer of that table has not been established.

The 128 bytes at `$1B80–$1BFF` match RAM beneath `$FCD7–$FD56` in both the title and opening-story snapshots. In the captured airshaft play state, 31 of the corresponding high-memory bytes differ while the stash copy remains unchanged. The same bytes also occur at `$2DA0–$2E1F` in the earlier Censor-intro snapshot. The Censor boot unpacker emits this retained tail into the resident stash along with the engine. The first engine entry and later restoration have identical bytes there; its inclusion is therefore a property of the packed boot image, rather than a subsequent gameplay write.

A direct-control-flow closure from `$1000`, the six installed engine IRQ entries and the loader’s `$07D8` handler visits 1,220 instruction starts. It follows both arms of every conditional branch, treats external calls as returning, and contains no indirect JMP. This closure does not reach `$16A4–$16CA`, `$1AE0–$1B06`, `$0767–$07AC`, either skipped `SIDE FLAG` check, or `$11DD`. It describes the fixed captured engine; arbitrary code edits and external jumps into another entry are outside this method.

Scoped VICE traces cover ten neutral frames and the normal input-to-next-load transition from each of the title, opening-story, second-story, third-story and ending captures. Each case enters the tick wait ten times and the next decompressor once. The retained RNG, self-copy and filename helpers stay unentered. The first skipped-check watch includes the live continuation `$11B7/$11BA`; those are its only hits, with none in the skipped `$11A2–$11B6` block. Neither the 24-word row table at `$1A37–$1A66`, test text at `$1AB7–$1ADE`, nor retained tail `$1B80–$1BFF` is read in these intervals. These bounded runtime observations agree with the control-flow result without asserting that every possible external entry has been tested.
