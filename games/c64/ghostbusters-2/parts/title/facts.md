# Title screen facts

The game loads `1.THE INTRO` before the title prompt. The prompt state is captured in `work/entry.vsf`; the opening story follows a further load. The packed file expands to the ranges in `part.json` when the captured decompressor is run over RAM filled with either zeroes or ones. Its 16,098 output bytes differ from the later title snapshot in 99 places, all in the sound-driver area `$E0C2–$E14A`, `$E43B–$E43C` and `$EB58–$EB5B`. These are runtime voice state, self-modified decoder and rate operands, and the advancing four-byte effect-noise generator, all written by the sound driver. See `../../unpack-map.json`.

In the title-prompt snapshot, all 5,120 bytes at `$6400–$77FF` match `$4000–$53FF`, and all 640 bytes at `$7800–$7A7F` match `$6000–$627F`. The title sequence at `$1049` calls the transfer routine at `$14DB`. It copies sixteen 320-byte bitmap rows and sixteen 40-byte screen-matrix rows. The raster handler at `$17D7` selects VIC bank 1, bitmap address `$4000`, matrix address `$6000` and multicolour mode for the picture band. Each bitmap cell takes eight bytes; the corresponding matrix byte supplies two colours. The routine also fills colour RAM using the value at `$7A80`.

The title file contains 36 space-padded 40-byte rows at `$C200–$C79F`, followed by zero at `$C7A0`. They give staff and music credits, the developer and publisher, the title and a key prompt. The resident sequence at copied address `$1049` passes `$C200` to the story text renderer at `$1924`.

The title snapshot has sprite pointers `$A0–$A7` at `$07F8–$07FF`. The stored setup writes those pointers, places sprites 0–3 in four positions and sprites 4–7 at the same positions, and sets their colours to 1 and 2. The raster handler selects VIC bank 0 for the sprite portion of the display, so the pointers select eight 64-byte slots at `$2800–$29FF`; each slot contains 63 bitmap bytes and a padding byte.

The title logo uses its own character artwork at `$2000–$27FF`. Setup at copied `$13D8` writes the consecutive values `$00–$EF` into 240 cells at `$0568–$0657`; the raster handler selects VIC bank 0 and `$D018=$19` for that band. The measured title-frame renderer fetches all 240 patterns at `$2000–$277F`. Codes `$F0–$FF` are zero-filled and outside the setup’s sequence. These logo patterns are separate from the story alphabet. The title frame reconstruction matches 104,445 of 104,448 VICE pixels; its three differing pixels lie on raster lines 32, 122 and 124, around the splits. This does not claim a perfect frame match.

The title’s sound-update instructions at `$E13A–$E225` match the airshaft code except for the mutable rate and accumulator operands at `$E147/$E14A`. The update advances three voice streams on accumulator carry, calculates their pitch values, writes enabled voice state to the SID and writes the volume byte.

The story alphabet is at `$3000–$37FF`. The opening-story frame selects it with VIC bank 0 and `$D018=$1D`. Renderer `$1A12` maps each input byte into two character codes in successive screen-row buffers: space gives `$00/$01`, digits give `$20/$21` through `$32/$33`, and either case of A–Z gives `$42/$43` through `$74/$75`. The 122 stored title/story/ending rows (4,880 bytes) were all run through the actual routine. They also select punctuation pairs: exclamation `$02/$03`, ampersand `$0C/$0D`, apostrophe `$0E/$0F`, parentheses `$10/$11` and `$12/$13`, comma `$18/$19`, hyphen `$1A/$1B`, period `$1C/$1D` and question mark `$3E/$3F`. `test_story_text.js` checks those mappings from the listing. The corpus selects no code above `$75`; the measured title frame separately fetches `$76–$7F` around its raster transition, so absence from the text corpus does not imply absence of VIC fetches. A separate text use for the higher patterns is not established.

The shared effect selectors at `$E6AD`, `$E70C` and `$E76B` each use an index in A to fetch a 12-byte record through the pointer table at `$E7CA`. They copy the record into per-voice state and initialize the corresponding SID voice. The selector bytes are identical in the captured title, airshaft, Broadway and museum states.

The 95 bytes after the credits terminator at `$C7A0` are zero in both decompressor-fill runs and in the title snapshot. They are included in the listing; they are padding after the terminating zero, outside the text renderer’s row reads. All 16,098 stable output bytes from `1.THE INTRO` now have listing records, with the 99 snapshot differences described above.

The 127 bytes following the title picture colour at `$7A80` all hold `$2A` in the captured title snapshot. The picture transfer reads the single colour byte at `$7A80`; the remaining bytes pad the loaded segment and are outside that transfer’s source range.
