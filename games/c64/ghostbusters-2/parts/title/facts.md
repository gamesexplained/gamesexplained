# Title screen facts

The game loads `1.THE INTRO` before the title prompt. The prompt state is captured in `work/entry.vsf`; the opening story follows a further load. The packed file expands to the ranges in `part.json` when the captured decompressor is run over RAM filled with either zeroes or ones. Its 16,098 output bytes differ from the later title snapshot in 99 places, all in the sound-driver area `$E0C2–$E14A`, `$E43B–$E43C` and `$EB58–$EB5B`. These include mutable voice state and self-modified operands; the cause of each difference remains open. See `../../unpack-map.json`.

In the title-prompt snapshot, all 5,120 bytes at `$6400–$77FF` match `$4000–$53FF`, and all 640 bytes at `$7800–$7A7F` match `$6000–$627F`. The title sequence at `$1049` calls the transfer routine at `$14DB`. It copies sixteen 320-byte bitmap rows and sixteen 40-byte screen-matrix rows. The raster handler at `$17D7` selects VIC bank 1, bitmap address `$4000`, matrix address `$6000` and multicolour mode for the picture band. Each bitmap cell takes eight bytes; the corresponding matrix byte supplies two colours. The routine also fills colour RAM using the value at `$7A80`.

The title file contains 36 space-padded 40-byte rows at `$C200–$C79F`, followed by zero at `$C7A0`. They give staff and music credits, the developer and publisher, the title and a key prompt. The resident sequence at copied address `$1049` passes `$C200` to the story text renderer at `$1924`.

The title snapshot has sprite pointers `$A0–$A7` at `$07F8–$07FF`. The stored setup writes those pointers, places sprites 0–3 in four positions and sprites 4–7 at the same positions, and sets their colours to 1 and 2. The raster handler selects VIC bank 0 for the sprite portion of the display, so the pointers select eight 64-byte slots at `$2800–$29FF`; each slot contains 63 bitmap bytes and a padding byte.

In the saved title state `$145D=$01`, `$1905=$3B` and `$1906=$1B`. The raster handler at copied address `$1805` writes `$1906` to `$D011`, `$07` to `$DD00` and `$19` to `$D018`; in that character-mode band the character set begins at `$2000` in VIC bank 0. The renderer at `$1A12` maps each text byte to two screen codes: space becomes `$00/$01`, digits 0–9 become `$20/$21` through `$32/$33`, and either case of A–Z becomes `$42/$43` through `$74/$75`. It writes the even and odd codes into separate 40-byte buffers for successive screen rows. The rest of the set, codes `$76–$FF` at `$23B0–$27FF`, contains 138 eight-byte patterns; their display use remains open.

The title’s sound-update instructions at `$E13A–$E225` match the airshaft code except for the mutable rate and accumulator operands at `$E147/$E14A`. The update advances three voice streams on accumulator carry, calculates their pitch values, writes enabled voice state to the SID and writes the volume byte.

The opening-story frame writes `$07` to `$DD00`, `$1D` to `$D018` and `$08` to `$D016` at raster line 171, then `$17` to `$D011` at line 184. In the resulting standard-text band, VIC bank 0 and `$D018=$1D` select the character set at `$3000`. Those 2,048 bytes match the title snapshot. Codes `$00/$01` are blank; `$20/$21` through `$32/$33` form the renderer’s digit pairs, and `$42/$43` through `$74/$75` its letter pairs. The remaining ranges contain 30 patterns at codes `$02–$1F`, 14 at `$34–$41`, and 138 at `$76–$FF`; their display use remains open.

The shared effect selectors at `$E6AD`, `$E70C` and `$E76B` each use an index in A to fetch a 12-byte record through the pointer table at `$E7CA`. They copy the record into per-voice state and initialize the corresponding SID voice. The selector bytes are identical in the captured title, airshaft, Broadway and museum states.

The 95 bytes after the credits terminator at `$C7A0` are zero in both decompressor-fill runs and in the title snapshot. They are included in the listing; their use beyond the preceding terminator remains open. All 16,098 stable output bytes from `1.THE INTRO` now have listing records, with the 99 snapshot differences described above.

The 127 bytes following the title picture colour at `$7A80` all hold `$2A` in the captured title snapshot. The transfer routine reads `$7A80`; any other use of the following bytes remains open.
