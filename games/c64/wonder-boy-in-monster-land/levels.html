Maps drawn from the game's tile and character data.

Explore a captured map

The 31 captures were obtained by invoking the game's own loader. Their numbers describe the capture sequence; those captures do not demonstrate routes through ordinary play. The maps show the background terrain and omit moving objects. The page uses the colours in each captured state.

Each map byte selects four character codes from the tables at $81CF, $82CF, $83CF and $84CF. The drawing loop at $6D4D–$6D63 places them in a two-by-two block. A lookup at $7FCF supplies the colour for each character. The horizontal and vertical bounds come from $7F4B and $7F4C; the stored row stride is at $7F4A.
