The two primary display slots take the frame and the adjacent frame at $1CAB–$1CEC. A 120-frame VICE observation found a six-frame interval between subsequent changes in this state. The monochrome colour was $00; the multicolour sprite used $01, with shared colours $02 and $05. The two layers are separated here so their individual pixels remain visible. This viewer covers this observed state; it does not establish the behavior of other sprites.

Each slot is 64 bytes apart in the VIC bank. The first 63 bytes make a 24-by-21 bitmap; the game chooses the bank through CIA2. The slot bytes for this example are at $F1C0, $F200, $F240 and $F280.
