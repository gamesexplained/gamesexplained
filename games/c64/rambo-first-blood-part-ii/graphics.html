1985 · Ocean Software

Rambo: First Blood Part II

Every sprite the game shows, moving at the game's pace: Rambo, his six weapons, the soldiers, the prisoners, the items, the helicopter and the gunship, each drawn from the shapes and tables in the game's memory.

01 · Rambo

Rambo is two sprites, one on top of the other

A black high-resolution outline sits on a multicolour body four shapes further on. On some frames the game shows the outline a step behind the body, because it draws the outline from a copy made once a pass. The outline is the hardware's sprite 0, which the interrupt sets apart from the rest; the body is one of the objects the multiplexer shares out among sprites 1-7.

From rambo_animate ($1281): a list of four frames for each way the stick points ($12CD, $12D8), the outline from the list and the body four on ($12B1), a step every fourth frame. Pushed diagonally Rambo uses the north or south list; when the stick lets go he stops on his last frame, and a new direction starts its list from the first.

The copy of the outline's picture is made by the main loop (objects_snapshot, $1D61); the cards here keep outline and body together. The body's colour, 10, is set by game_init ($1778), and the multicolours every sprite shares are white and black ($175D, $1765).

02 · The weapons

Six weapons, each with its own shot

The panel shows an icon for each weapon Rambo owns, and the space bar steps between them. Each weapon fires a shot of its own type, in its own colour, in the direction Rambo last moved.

From fire_check ($19F7): each weapon's type ($1AA0), colour ($1AA6) and shapes by direction ($1AAC, $1AB7, $1AC2). The knife spins a shape a frame from $39 to $40 (shot_step, $1831). Types 5 and 8, the explosive arrows and the rocket, explode on a hit; the ordinary arrows stop. The grenade is thrown: shot_arc ($183D) runs here on the site's 6502 a call a frame, lifting it along a table of 28 heights ($8000) and drawing it larger near the top, until it comes down and explodes. Each icon is the one the panel shows for the weapon (panel_weapons, $193D) in its colour there.

An explosion: five shapes, a step every third frame (explosion_animate, $1920), in green (explosion_start, $18EC). Grenades, purple arrows and rockets end in one, and the helicopter stage's shots use the same shapes ($316A).

03 · The soldiers

Soldiers walk, kneel, climb out of the ground and fall

A soldier walks one of eight ways, set by the zone it enters from and changed now and then at random, and comes in one of four colours picked at random. Diagonal walkers use the north and south frames, as Rambo does.

From enemy_animate ($1070): a list of frames for each type ($10B1, $10BC), a step every third frame, in a colour from enemy_colours ($11E1). Only the soldier placed at the map's marker stands still, as type 0 (marker_scan, $0E31): it kneels in grey, a step every 21 frames. A soldier coming out of a hole in the ground (hole_spawn, $0DB7) is brown, one of two shapes by the character below the hole, and is run here through the game's own enemy_rise ($0D33) on the site's 6502. A soldier that is hit turns white and falls through five shapes, a step every fifth frame (shot_hit, $1539; enemy_step, $0F68).

04 · Prisoners and items

The prisoners walk out of the hut

Once the hut is opened, ten prisoners come out one at a time, walk south, then turn east and walk to the edge of the screen. Four items lie in the jungle; the last calls in the helicopter.

The prisoners from pow_spawn and pow_walk ($0803, $084B): grey, four frames each way, a step every third frame (pow_anim_tick, $0897). The items from item_place ($0B01) and item_frame ($0BCB): the first three flash through eight colours, a step every third frame (flash_counter, $1B00); the helicopter is two sprites in light blue, which it keeps for the flight.

05 · The helicopter and the gunship

Twelve frames of helicopter, forty-eight of gunship

The helicopter has four headings, and for each a frame flying straight and two turning towards either side, three to a heading. Its rotor is a single sprite that moves round the hub as it turns. The gunship is four sprites for each of the same twelve frames.

The helicopter from heli_objects_place ($3775): two multicolour sprites, the second at an offset for the frame ($3793, $379F, $37AB). The frame is the heading's first ($3751) plus 1 or 2 when the stick also points along the other axis (steer_heading, $36CE). The rotor is sprite 0, white, in four steps that each move it round the hub (heli_sprites_place, $3827; $388A-$3892), a step every second frame. The same sprite draws the gunship's rotor, so each rotor shows on every other frame only, as it does here.

The gunship from gunship_place_sprites ($35C3): four sprites from gunship_frames ($3630), light grey, at offsets for the frame ($363C-$369B), with the rotor on the frames the helicopter's is not shown. Its 48 shapes run from $83 to $B2.

06 · The panel

The panel is eight sprites below the jungle

The interrupt at the bottom of the screen reuses the sprites for the panel: the words ENERGY and SCORE stretched to double width, and an icon for each weapon. The selected weapon's icon flashes.

From irq_panel ($217C): sprites 0 and 1 doubled in width ($10, $11), yellow while the energy is 10 or more and flashing below that, and six icons at panel_sprite_x ($2258) in their colours ($226D). The selected weapon's icon takes the flashing colour (panel_flash, $1989); weapons 4 and 5 share a pair of icons, which shows the machine gun, the rocket launcher or both. The words and the energy bar are as the game's memory holds them at the start of a game.

The name wheel in the front end points at letters with one more sprite, its colour cycling (wheel_sprite, $C5C7), below. Nine shapes are never shown, as nothing in the code points a sprite at them: $21, $25 and $D8-$DE, below it.
