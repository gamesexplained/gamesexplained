Commodore 64 · 1987 · System 3

Gameplay

The island is built from reusable scenery. Each new chapter replaces its rooms, artwork and music while the engine stays in memory.

01 · Fighting

A direction changes the blow

The same joystick direction can select a guard, a kick or a weapon strike. The weapon and Armakuni’s facing choose the pose script. A strike checks for contact at a particular point in that script; the wind-up does not wound the opponent.

A guard works only when its direction opposes the incoming attack. Somersaults reject contact before the range checks. The guard test also reads the opponent’s close-action counter: at four or more it permits contact through an otherwise valid guard. The chapter handler separately prevents repeated wounds while the opponent is reacting.

02 · Jumping and hazards

One pixel can miss the next safe area

The three forward jump inputs share a somersault animation but move Armakuni along different projected paths. Compare them against a room’s stored safe areas. A landing passes when its tested coordinate lies inside any rectangle. The right and bottom edges are excluded.

Blue marks the movement path. Yellow shows accepted player coordinates; the dot is the coordinate used by the landing test. The preview isolates movement and the landing test. It omits collisions on the way and the chapter’s other triggers.

03 · The opponent

A guard waits before choosing its approach

The opponent does not choose another blow on every update. Its control states separate waiting, approaching, selecting a close attack and finishing that attack. Change the positions, then step the decision to see what it asks for next.

State: Idle

This steps the shared wait, approach-target and close-choice rules. Positions are inputs: placing the guard changes that input directly. Walls, walking animation, special opponent types and damage are outside this decision viewer.

The wait takes the low five bits of a random byte. The nonzero variants add 16 ticks. Once close, moving Armakuni at least eight pixels across or four in Y restarts the approach. The close-action count can also force another approach after three actions.

04 · The controls

Choosing what Armakuni carries

The status panel keeps weapons and puzzle objects separate. Space changes the equipped weapon. F3 and F5 cycle through the objects you have collected. The Using and Holding displays show the selections.

KeyDuring play
SpaceCycle through available weapons.
F3 / F5Cycle through collected objects.
F1Mute or resume the tune at its saved position.
F7Pause or resume play.
Shift + plus / minusSpeed up or slow down the game ticks.

The joystick uses port 2. Attacks depend on direction, facing and the selected weapon. The original manual gives the direction diagrams for fighting and jumping. The speed keys also let you change the pace of play.

05 · Drawing the island

The Wastelands

The opening room from Side 1 of the C64 cassette.

The game draws the fork and its rocks from a shape script. The room’s record gives the renderer a pointer-page choice, an offset in that page, and a background colour. The same renderer draws the loading picture after a different tape file is loaded into the same space. Browse the room pictures and their records.

06 · Loading a chapter

What the next chapter replaces

After the Wastelands, each chapter loads eight named files. The chapter letter changes in the filename and the loader uses the same destinations. Three files take turns at $0800 while the loading picture is drawn and other bytes are copied elsewhere.

Side 1

FileFirst destinationWhat happens to it
0B$B000Plays the loading tune
1B$0800Draws the loading picture
2B$0800Copies to $DF00 and $FF40
3B$0800Copies to RAM beneath the I/O registers at $D800
4B$CC00Supplies the room records
5B$9E00Replaces upper chapter data
6B$0600Replaces the low chapter window
7B$B000Installs the gameplay tune and chapter startup

The engine at $5400–$9DFF stays in place. Once the last file lands, the loader jumps to the new chapter’s entry at $BC60. See the chapter loader at $CE80.
