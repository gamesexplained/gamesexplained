01 · The inventory

What the objects are for

Weapons appear under Using. Other collected objects appear under Holding, where F3 and F5 select them. The pictures below are drawn by the game’s inventory painter. Location links open the connected map at the recorded room.

Hidden effects

Hidden effects have their own record codes. Their full behaviour remains open; they are not labelled as extra inventory objects.

02 · Picking up

The hand must reach a small rectangle

Moving close to a visible object is not enough. The pickup reader uses Armakuni’s position as a proxy for the reach of his hand and tests it against a stored rectangle. Facing left shifts the tested X coordinate by 36 pixels. The right and bottom boundaries are excluded.

Yellow marks accepted coordinates, rather than the visible outline of the object. The blue dot is the coordinate the code tests. The object sprites are a separate layer from this room background.

03 · Using an object

The right object can still be the wrong selection

Some puzzle checks read the selected Holding object, not simply whether it is somewhere in the inventory. Several also require empty hands and an up-right facing. Try the selections below against the original gate conditions.
