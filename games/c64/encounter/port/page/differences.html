The game plays as it does on a C64, at the C64's pace. These are the places where the page cannot be the machine, and what it does instead.

On a C64On this page
The program loads from disk, unpacks itself and starts the game at $9C3E, with the screen as BASIC left it.There is no loading. The page starts at $9C3E with the memory as the unpacking leaves it, and the title comes up at once.
The game times the screen as the title comes up, and on a machine whose frame is not a PAL one's it jumps into colour memory and stops: it runs only on a PAL C64. The same jump comes on a PAL machine after a game over when the check's first read lands on line 0 or 256, two of the frame's 312 lines.The page is a PAL C64, and goes on to the title every time.
Each instruction takes its cycles, and the video chip takes some of the processor's on the lines where it fetches characters and sprites.The port executes no instructions. Each routine costs the cycles it costs the game, and one whose work varies costs it by the work: a shape by the bytes it draws, the view by the objects in it. The video chip takes its cycles as the beam passes those lines.
The raster's seven interrupts a frame change the screen's colours and character sets where the beam is, which draws the sky, the horizon's bands, the ground and the console.Each handler runs whole when its interrupt comes, and each of its writes to the video chip lands on the cycle of its line that the C64's lands on.
The SID plays the engine, the hum and the effects, written by the game as it runs.The site's model of the SID, with the filter of the C64's original 6581, plays what the port writes as each frame is shown.
The KERNAL reads the keyboard for the title.The page has no ROM: the port reads the keyboard as the KERNAL does and finds the same key.
The game reads a joystick in control port 2, and the keys F1, F5, F7, Space and 1 to 8.The arrows and X (or Right Ctrl) are the joystick, or the pad on a touch screen, and the other keys are the C64's of the same names. A tap shorter than a pass of the game is held until the game has read it.
