The Far view switch draws more of the track ahead, as the draw distance cheat on the C64-Wiki does: eight pieces of track a pass in place of four.

Each pass, the game draws the piece the car is on and the one after it, then as many more as the 2 in LDA #$02 at $173D says (DRAW_VIEW, $167A). The cheat pokes 6 into $173E, and the switch puts 6 in the same place; the game's own code does the rest. While the crane lowers the car the game draws five more pieces whatever the count says, so the cheat changes nothing until the car is let go.

On a C64 each extra piece costs time. A pass on Little Ramp takes about 8.8 frames in place of 7.1, so the picture changes about 5.7 times a second in place of 7, and the race's clocks, which add 0.14 seconds a pass, fall further behind real time. This page's machine pays the same cost. With Fast CPU on as well, a pass still takes one video frame and the clocks keep real time.
