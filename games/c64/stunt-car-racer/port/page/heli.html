The Heli cam button films the race as you drive it, from a helicopter flying with your car: behind it and to one side, high up, looking ahead along the road, so that you can steer by it.

The helicopter

The camera hangs 560 units from your car, 126 degrees round from its nose, and 210 above it. It looks at a point 250 ahead of the car, so the car sits low and to one side of the picture and the road it is driving into fills the rest. While the opponent's car is ahead of you and less than 1,600 units away, the camera films the point midway between the two cars and stands further off, by 0.8 of the gap, so that both are in the picture. The game keeps which car is ahead in bit 7 of $C36A (CAR_GAP, $F585). An opponent behind you is not followed, so the road ahead stays in sight.

When the track has hidden your car from the helicopter for six video frames, and the car would be in sight from the other side, it flies round to that side. It goes over the back of the car, higher as it passes behind, and stays on its new side for at least a second and a half. It never comes lower than 270 units above the road under it.

It follows the car with a lag, as a helicopter would. Across the ground it closes 12 % of the gap each video frame and turns with the car at the same rate; up and down it closes 30 %, so that a car falling off the track stays in the picture. Its lens is flat and sees 57 degrees across, about the game's own 56.

The track, the cars, the dust and the colours are Chase cam's, drawn by the same code, and the opponent's car is drawn behind and beside yours here too. The sound is the game's own.
