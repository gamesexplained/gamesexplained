The Heli Replay button stops the race and plays it again from its start, as a helicopter beside the cars would film it. Pressed again, or once the replay reaches the moment it was pressed, the race goes on from where it stood.

The recording

Each time the game draws a picture of the race (DRAW_VIEW), the page keeps what Chase View reads from the game's memory: where each car is, which way it points and the heights of its wheels, and the crane's chains. A new race empties the recording (CLEAR_RACE), so the replay starts with the crane lowering the car. The game draws about seven pictures a second on a C64, and fifty with Fast CPU; the replay moves the cars in a straight line between two pictures, so that they glide where the game's own picture steps, except where a car is put somewhere new.

The helicopter

The camera hangs 750 units to the side of the cars and 160 above them, a little behind, and swings slowly, 34 degrees towards their front, then as far towards their back, and round again every twenty seconds. When the opponent is less than 1,200 units away it films the point midway between the cars. It follows them with a lag, as a helicopter would, closing 15 % of the gap each video frame, and turns with the cars at half that rate. Its lens is flat and sees 57 degrees across, about the game's own.

The track, the cars and the colours are Chase View's, drawn by the same code. The dashboard stays as it was when the replay began.
