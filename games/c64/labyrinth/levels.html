The city as a workflow

The opening connects places with commands and gates some actions on what you carry. Select a place or an arrow to see its description, condition and result. Solid blue arrows move between locations. Dashed amber arrows show actions at the counter. The gray connection follows the coin choice into the later theater encounter. ($90F4, $9327, $95B7)

A route through the cinema, with optional change collection. Details appear below the diagram; on narrow screens, scroll the diagram sideways. The counter actions can be taken in either order. This is a guide to verified decisions, rather than a complete playable opening.

02 · Inside the maze

Captured scenes

The disk guide describes a journey through Jareth’s maze, with objects to collect and puzzles to solve before the clock reaches thirteen.1 The gallery distinguishes ordinary arrivals from controlled scene captures. The gate, first corridor, and Brick Hallway were reached through ordinary play. The Wall of Hands and Stone Corridor arrivals continue from the controlled Alph and Ralph setup; the Stone Corridor route also uses explicitly supplied shears. Other captures show a scene selected in memory and loaded by the game.4 For movement within the first corridor, use the corridor movement strip on Gameplay.
