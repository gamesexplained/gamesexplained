Sources

The numbered notes on the reading pages point here. Memory-address links open the matching part’s listing. Emulator captures establish the recorded state; examples that start from a prepared room state say so in their “Sources and checks” notes.

  1. Labyrinth: The Computer Game — Player’s Guide, disk edition, read 17 October 2026. Printed pages 3–13 cover the game’s premise, controls, and saving. Pages 7–9 describe the word wheels and command input. The documented features and their verification status are listed below.
  2. Labyrinth — C64-Wiki, read 17 October 2026. Secondary reference for the text opening, character choices, and independently sourced reference pictures. Its claims are distinguished from the observations made with the supplied disks.
  3. Labyrinth — cassette Player’s Guide, read 17 October 2026. Used for the documented encounters. Its saving restriction applies to that edition; it does not establish the disk edition’s behavior.
  4. The two supplied Activision disk images, run in VICE between 17 and 20 October 2026. Captures and command observations support the text opening, gate entry, corridor and Brick Hallway routes. Alph and Ralph examples begin in a controlled room; the door-count comparison changes the counter explicitly. The checked continuation reaches The Wall of Hands from that setup. The methods and limits appear in each example’s “Sources and checks” notes and in the analysed-state description below.
