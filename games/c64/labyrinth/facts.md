# Labyrinth — established facts

This Bronze contribution describes the playable text opening. The graphical continuation is named in `parts/labyrinth/part.json` and is unanalysed. It has no listing; the opening’s listing is in `parts/opening/listing.json`.

Live screenshots establish the slot menu, character-setup questions, verb/object commands, several city locations, and the transition to a graphical gate scene. They do not establish the maze’s mechanics or the timing of its clock. See `features.md` for the distinction between documented and observed behavior.

The opening’s code, alphabet, hardware census, and literal string sweep are documented in `parts/opening/facts.md`. Its nine comments have no independent sample or audit. A later Silver contribution needs complete coverage and verification.
