1986 · Activision · Controls

Labyrinth

Two word wheels give the commands and joystick port 1 moves the character. This page explains both, and the area guides show how the inputs affect play.

01 · Command wheels

Typing chooses a word from the wheel

The two wheels hold actions and objects. Typing narrows the active wheel’s choice; Space changes wheels, and Return executes the command. A letter that matches nothing is discarded. ($5F2C, $612C, $61F7)1

A mistyped letter disappears

Typing G selects give. Adding Z matches nothing, so the game drops that letter and keeps G. Adding O then selects go. Delete clears the whole prefix while leaving the selected word in place. A rejected letter also flashes the border red ($612C); the length of the flash has not been measured. ($5F2C, $612C, $61F7)

02 · Movement

Walking and approaching

The graphical adventure uses joystick port 1. Left and right move along a wall; up approaches a doorway and down retreats. The movement map decides how close the character can get.4 ($4171)
