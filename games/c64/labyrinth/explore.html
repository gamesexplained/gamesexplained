Area 1 · The text adventure

The text adventure

Labyrinth begins with a text adventure. You name your character, answer the setup questions, and explore the streets outside a movie theater. Two word wheels hold the available actions and objects. Entering go home takes you from the sidewalk to a room with a television and a camcorder.2 ($9114)

Words that change the scene

Typing selects words on the active wheel. Space changes wheels, and Return carries out the selected command. The opening route visits a beggar and a popcorn counter before the movie begins. ($D000)1

  • The slot menu offers F1, F3, and F5.
  • The setup accepts a name, sex, and favorite color.
  • Commands such as take camcorder and give nickel produce different responses as the location changes.
  • The cinema sequence leads from text descriptions to a picture of the character at a gate.

A mistyped letter disappears

Typing G selects give. Adding Z matches nothing, so the game drops that letter and keeps G. Adding O then selects go. Delete clears the whole prefix while leaving the selected word in place. ($5F2C, $612C, $61F7)
