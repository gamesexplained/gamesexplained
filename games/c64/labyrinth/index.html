01 · The game

From a rainy street to Jareth’s maze

You begin on a city block, enter a cinema, and emerge in Jareth’s maze. The opening describes its places in text. Beyond the cinema, the game shows your character moving through corridors and rooms.

02 · Commands

Typing chooses a word from the wheel

The two wheels hold actions and objects. Typing narrows the active wheel’s choice; Space changes wheels, and Return executes the command. A letter that matches nothing is discarded.

03 · Movement and interaction

The same command can do something different a step away

In the Brick Hallway, OPEN DOOR does nothing at the arrival position. Moving to the neighboring door makes the command open it. The handler reads the map cell under the player and changes a closed-door cell into an open-door cell.

This example reproduces the command decision and map-cell change. The pictures are live captures. Follow the checked route through this door.
