Commodore 64 · 1986 · Lucasfilm Games

Labyrinth

A trip to the cinema becomes a journey through Jareth’s maze. Words choose your actions; movement brings you face to face with its puzzles.

01 · The adventure

From a rainy street to Jareth’s maze

You begin on a city block outside a movie theater. You name your character, explore the streets, and buy a ticket. The opening describes its places in text, with two word wheels for choosing actions and objects. Entering the cinema leads into the graphical adventure.2

Beyond the cinema, your character walks through corridors and rooms. Commands let you open doors and talk to the maze’s inhabitants. The Player’s Guide sets the goal: reach Jareth before thirteen hours expire.1 The entrance gate leads to the first corridor and the Brick Hallway, where choosing a passage changes where you arrive.

02 · The release

Lucasfilm Games’ adventure

Lucasfilm Games made Labyrinth, based on Jim Henson’s film and published by Activision in 1986. The credits name David Fox as production manager and programmer, Charlie Kellner as technical director and lead programmer, and Gary Winnick among the artists. David M. Martin Jr. is credited with the music and Commodore sound.1
