Commodore 64 · 1987 · Imagine

Arkanoid: graphics

The picture the cassette builds on the screen while the game loads, which the game then keeps as its title picture.

01 · The loading picture

The title picture, drawn on the screen as the tape reads it

The cassette loads the title picture first and lets you watch it arrive. On a white screen it fills in a cell at a time, left to right along each row of cells and down the screen, over 38 seconds, while Martin Galway's Ocean loading tune plays (Music).

The game's own bytes, from the listing: the bitmap at $4000-$5F3F, its screen colours at $6000-$63E7 and the colour RAM that the title copies from $6400, all three the same byte for byte as the 1987 cassette loads them. The bitmap appears at the tape's own pace, where it had got to every half second, measured in VICE.

About a minute into the tape, with the screen switched off, the loader reads the picture's screen colours and its colour RAM. Then it turns the screen on, white, and reads the bitmap in the order it lies in memory, at about 210 bytes a second. The bitmap keeps the eight rows of each 8 by 8 cell together, eight bytes in a row, so the picture arrives in whole cells: forty across, then the next row of cells down, twenty-five rows in all.

The game keeps these bytes as they loaded: they are its title picture. show_title_picture ($B480) points the video chip at them again for the title screen, and once more after Doh falls at the end.
