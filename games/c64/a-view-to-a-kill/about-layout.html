The copies studied

Two copies were studied, and every address and fact on these pages comes from one of them: Domark's own PAL disk, labelled THRILLER PACK. Its menu loads each of the five programs from a file of its own (A, B, C, D and F), which unpacks itself over memory and jumps into the game. The disk's loaders and menu are not described.

The other copy is a crack, titled "BOMBJACK LTD. presents A VIEW TO A KILL / Originally cracked by MAGIC... levelcrunched and modified by BJK". It was studied first, and on 7 and 8 October 2026 it was compared with Domark's disk byte by byte, each program unpacked and saved in play on both. Of the bytes the crack's listings call code, the intro differs in 231 of 3,015, Paris in 185 of 5,741, City Hall in 168 of 6,078 and the mine in 51 of 7,927. The finale is the same in every byte its listing covers.

What the crackers changed:

  • Going back to the menu. The crack has no screen for ABORT and RESTORE to open, and restarts the section at once. Its intro stops and waits at the end instead of loading the menu.
  • The credits. The crack's credits read "speech by B.-Jones" where Domark's read "Speech synthesis by David Aubrey-Jones", and drop the thanks to Malcolm.
  • Leftovers of other cracks. The crack's intro holds the title and menu of another group's crack ("THE DYNAMIC-DUO PRESENTS", with SEE OPENING, PARIS-CHASE, CITY ESCAPE, SILICON MINE and FINALE), and its Paris an earlier packer signed "MAGIC".

What plays differently. A crack would not normally change how a game plays, so the crack may have been made from another release of the game; which came first is not known.

  • The music. In every program the crack's tunes step after three frames and then two, 20 steps a second, against every third frame on Domark's disk.
  • Paris. The crack's locator lamp flashes and beeps; on Domark's disk it never runs. The crack ends the chase at 40 damage from roadblocks as well as crashes, counts the altimeter down every 20 of May Day's steps instead of 21, lands her on the map's small ovals, and lets a police car that touched the car move again. One cell of its town map differs.
  • City Hall. The crack draws Bond with two sprites and uses the other two for energy gauges, outline figures of Bond and Stacey that empty over time but end nothing. Domark's disk has no gauge, and draws Bond with four hi-res sprites. The crack blinks the edge of the open menu window red and yellow, where Domark's stays purple. In its room data, room 38 has no second piece of furniture, and item 45 weighs 2 instead of 6.
  • The mine. The crack moves the hazards and conveyor belts every frame from the interrupt, with shorter periods, and waits half as long in its main loop, so it plays faster. Six tiles of its map differ.
