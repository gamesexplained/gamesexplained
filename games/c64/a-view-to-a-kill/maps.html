Level 1 · Paris

Paris: the town, and where May Day can land

May Day comes down at one of eight points around the edge of the town, picked at random at the start. How the chase works is in The five parts.

Paris map

The circles are May Day's eight landing points. At the start the game picks one of them with a random number from 0 to 7 ($4F00) and reads its position from a table ($4FC0). Three lie along the top, two at the middle of the sides and three across the lower part of the town, about two thirds of the way down. The map has seven small ovals of its own near the edges, a cell below the top and side points and far below the lower ones, and no oval for the lower middle point; why they do not match is not known.

Level 2 · City Hall

City Hall: 75 rooms, a fire that spreads, and two ways out

The building room by room, with the keys and doors the route uses and the time each room catches fire. How the section works is in The five parts.

City Hall map

Room numbers count along the floors from the top left, 1 to 15 on the top floor, down to 61 to 75 at the bottom. A thick line is a wall. K marks a door that opens with that key, T one the tool forces with two uses, and ★ the door to room 61, which opens when items 56 and 57 are used in room 62. Numbers inside a room are the items the solution below uses: door keys in a circle , cupboard keys in a square , kit pieces in a diamond , the tool (43), the item Stacey waits for (21), and, as plain grey numbers, the items the tool uses up. A violet ▲ or ▼ is a lift and the way it goes. The figure in the corner is when the room catches fire, in minutes.6

Watch the route

Press play and Bond walks the whole solution below on the map. The fire spreads on the same clock, doors open as he reaches them, and the list of what he carries updates with every pickup. The route takes 28.9 minutes, and the animation spreads that evenly over the rooms Bond crosses, so the clock between rooms is approximate.7 The two deadlines hold with room to spare.

Drag to see which rooms are burning. Rooms 60 and 71 to 75 had not caught by 90 minutes. The items 84 to 89 and 36, 57 are shown in the room where a cupboard key puts them.

City Hall solution 1: let the fire take you

With CCPHJ typed, the burning-room bug (see City Hall in The five parts) is a way to win. Standing still in room 8 wins when the fire reaches the room, at 21.6 minutes. Walking towards the fire is faster: room 4 catches at 7.3 minutes.

City Hall solution 2: take Stacey out through room 76

This route reaches the end screen carrying 83 of the 101 the weight limit allows, with nothing dropped. It has two deadlines. Cupboard key 37 has to be used in room 5 before 10.9 minutes, and item 21 in room 8, where Stacey waits, before 21.6 minutes.

  • Fire opens the item list. Choose an item, press fire, choose a word, press fire. To pick something up, choose the blank first item and SEARCH, then the item.
  • A lift works from the back wall in front of it. Those in rooms 4 and 12 only go down, and those in 64 and 72 only go up; for these, stand at the lift's left side. In the others, stand at the left side to go down and the right side to go up.
  • Each use of the tool, item 43, spends one of the tall black-and-blue items, and the route collects more than the 18 its nine forced doors use.

1. Top floor (start in room 8)

  1. Room 9: take key 30. Back in room 8, use it to open the door to room 7.
  2. Room 7: take the tool, 43. Room 6: take key 24.
  3. Walk back to room 9 and use key 24 to open the door to room 10.
  4. Room 11: take 44, 45 and 46. Use the tool twice on the door to room 12.
  5. Room 13: take 60. Room 14: take key 32.

2. Fetch cupboard key 37 (second floor, east)

  1. Take the lift in room 12 down to 27. Room 28: take 47 and 48.
  2. From room 26, use the tool twice on the door to room 25.
  3. Room 25: take key 31. Room 24: take 56. Room 22: take 49 and cupboard key 37.
  4. Still in room 22, use key 32 to open the door to room 21. This opens the way along the second floor that section 5 walks.

3. Room 5 before 10.9 minutes

  1. Walk back to 27 and take the lift up to 12. Walk left to room 6.
  2. Use the tool twice on the door to room 5.
  3. Room 5: use cupboard key 37. Take kit piece 89 and item 57.

4. Stacey before 21.6 minutes

  1. Walk to room 12 and take the lift down past 27 to 42. Room 43: take 81.
  2. Go back up to 27 and walk to room 28. Use key 31 to open the door to room 29.
  3. From room 29, use the tool twice on the door to room 30.
  4. Room 30: take item 21 (it is heavy, 40), then 58 and 59.
  5. Walk back to room 8 (lift 27 up to 12, then left). Use item 21: Stacey follows you from here on.

5. West side

  1. Walk to 12, take the lift down to 27, and walk left along the second floor to room 19. The short way, through room 4, is closed: room 4 caught fire at 7.3 minutes. Room 20 catches at 24 minutes, so do this first.
  2. Take the lift in 19 down to 34. Room 35: take 62 and 63. Room 37: take key 25.
  3. Go back to 34, take the lift to 49 and walk to room 48. Use key 25 to open the door to room 47.
  4. Room 46: take cupboard key 38. Walk back to the lift in 49, go up to 34, and walk left to room 33. Use cupboard key 38 there, and take kit piece 84 (2 of 6).
  5. Take the lift in 34 down to 49 and walk right to room 50. Use the tool twice on the door to room 51. Room 51: take key 33.
  6. Walk back to 49 and take the lift down to 64. Room 65: take key 29. Room 66: take 69.

6. East side

  1. From room 66 walk left to 64 and take the lifts up through 49 and 34 to 19. Walk right along the second floor to 27. Take the lift down through 42 to 57. Walk left to room 56.
  2. Use key 33 to open the door to room 55. Room 55: take kit piece 86 (3 of 6) and cupboard key 39.
  3. Take the lift from 57 down to 72. Use the tool twice on the door to room 71.
  4. Room 70: take 78, 79 and 80.
  5. Take the lift from 72 up to 57, then 57 up to 42. Walk left to room 38.
  6. Use the tool twice on the door to room 37. This joins the two lift shafts for good.

7. Bottom floor, west

  1. Walk to 34 and take the lift down through 49 to 64. Room 65: use cupboard key 39. Take kit piece 85 (4 of 6) and key 36.
  2. From room 63, use the tool twice on the door to room 62.
  3. In room 62, use 56 and then 57. That opens the door to room 61. Walk through.
  4. In room 61, take kit piece 88 (5 of 6), and 72, 73 and 91.

8. Out

  1. Walk back up the west shaft to 34, right along the third floor to 42, and take the lift down to 57.
  2. Use key 29 to open the door to room 58.
  3. From room 58, use the tool twice on the door to room 59. Room 60: take cupboard key 40.
  4. Walk back to 57 and take the lift down to 72. Use key 36 to open the door to room 73.
  5. Room 73: use cupboard key 40. Take kit piece 87 (6 of 6).
  6. Room 75: use the kit, 54. Walk through into room 76 with Stacey: CONGRATULATIONS 007, code DB4CT.

Level 3 · The mine

The mine: four winch parts, five digits and the detonator

The whole mine with every object placed, and the order the code needs them in. How the bomb is checked is in The five parts.

Mine map

Mine solution

The stops in order, with the place of each in tiles from the top left. The player below draws the walk between them on the map.

Walk through the mine

Step through the solution on the map, one stop at a time, or press play to watch Bond walk it. Every stop was checked in VICE, and so was the end, where the game shows CONGRATULATIONS 007 and the code ILVCT. The walks for the first five stops were played in VICE too. The later walks were planned with a model of Bond's moves, measured in VICE, and may need retries where a jump or a fall lands short.8

  1. Take the grapnel gun (24, 9). On the top gallery, a short walk right from the start. USE it where there is open air above, and it fires a rope Bond can climb. He climbs it from one step to its left.
  2. Take a stick of dynamite (51, 9). Further along the top gallery. One stick clears one rock fall.
  3. Take a second stick of dynamite (109, 9). Along the top gallery, jumping the gaps. The second stick is needed for the pit around the number 7.
  4. Take the lighter (116, 9). At the right end of the top gallery, short of the gap. The lighter sets off a planted stick.
  5. Take the plank (39, 29). Back left along the gallery and down. Falling rocks drop in three columns around the plank. They only hurt, but each hurt costs about 29 seconds, so wait for a gap.
  6. Take the winch rope (39, 40). Down and left of the plank.
  7. Lay the plank over the gap in the top gallery (121, 10). Back up to the top gallery, left of the gap. USE the plank there and it fills the gap, which the way home needs later.
  8. Take the number 6 (17, 56). Far down on the left.
  9. Take the number 3 (49, 69).
  10. Take the winch drum (40, 84).
  11. Free May Day: dynamite, then the lighter (45, 95). Stand near the rock fall at the bottom left, USE a stick of dynamite, then the lighter. May Day is free, and the winch rope can now be fitted.
  12. Take the number 1 (92, 97). Along the bottom of the mine.
  13. Take the hook (146, 72). On the right side, low down.
  14. Take the winch (138, 49).
  15. Fit the four winch parts, then WINCH DOWN (128, 6). At the winch, top right: USE the hook, the winch drum, the winch, and the winch rope. Then WINCH DOWN lowers Bond down the shaft.
  16. Take the number 4 (121, 31).
  17. Take the number 7 (106, 29). The number 7 lies in a pit Bond cannot jump out of.
  18. Blast the floor of the pit: dynamite, then the lighter. The floor of the pit is a rock fall. USE the second stick of dynamite, then the lighter, and Bond drops into the pit below.
  19. Enter 6, 7, 1, 3, 4. Fire a rope in the lower pit and climb out, then go right. In the right-hand part of the mine, USE the numbers in the order 6, 7, 1, 3, 4. Exactly five: there is no feedback, and a sixth digit ruins the mine.
  20. Take the detonator, then WINCH UP (136, 29). The game lets Bond take it only with the winch fitted and five digits entered. Carrying it, he cannot climb on the right, so WINCH UP lifts him to the top.
  21. Walk left to the start: CONGRATULATIONS 007. Along the top gallery, over the plank, and on to the left edge. The game checks the digits there and shows CONGRATULATIONS 007 and the code ILVCT.
