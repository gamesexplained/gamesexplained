The picture Ocean's cassette shows while it loads, from the tape's own bytes.

01 · The loading picture

Auryn's two snakes, before the story starts

Ocean's 1985 cassette keeps the screen blank while it loads its picture, then puts it up whole, 100 seconds into the tape: THE NEVERENDING STORY in pale green capitals, Auryn's two snakes, one gold and one silver, each biting the other's tail, Ocean's logo, and © 1985 in the corner. It stays up, in silence, for a minute and a half while the first part of the game loads.

The cassette's own bytes, recorded in VICE with the picture up and drawn as the video chip draws them, with the border as the loader painted it in that one frame.

The disk copy these pages describe is a crack, and it shows the same picture, the same in every pixel, after the crackers' own introduction (Discoveries).
