Who is in the game, where it happens, what stands in the way, and how the world is saved from stupidity, all read from the game's own scripts. The answers are folded away: open only the ones you want.

01 · The cast

A reporter, an archaeologist and two students on Mars against the aliens who run the phone company

The player moves four people, and switches between them once they have met. The rest of the cast stays where it is met, and each wants something.

From the names the scripts give the actors (script 108 names the four kids), the news on Zak's television (script 102), the Caponians' talk in their secret room (script 140), the Skolarians' recording (script 47), the Guru's lesson (script 42), the Shaman's dance and lesson (scripts 70 and 45), and the epilogue (script 149).

The game opens on Zak asleep and dreaming (script 1), and then on the morning news: the world is getting stupider, a hum comes from every phone line, and a spokesperson for The Phone Company refuses to comment. The same broadcast shows Annie asking for artifacts and Melissa on Mars, waiting for instructions. Zak's boss wants one last story from him, on a two-headed squirrel that has been attacking campers near Seattle (script 127).

The player moves one kid at a time and changes kid with the Switch verb. No kid finishes alone: the kids on Earth bring the maps, the crystals and the glowing object, and the two on Mars open the way into the Face and its pyramid.

02 · The places

Two airport pictures serve nine cities, and the game's rooms serve more places than there are rooms

The game has 58 rooms, but a room can be several places: the scripts give it more numbers, and each number has its own ways in and out. That is how one airport picture is San Francisco, Seattle, Miami, London and Mexico City.

The rooms each place uses are on the Maps tab. The airports at London, Mexico City, Miami, Seattle and San Francisco are room 8; those at Cairo, Kinshasa, Katmandu and Lima are room 9 (the game's pseudoRoom instructions give them the room numbers 141 to 149). Each flight is chosen on the airport's reservations terminal (script 30).

A flight costs money from the kid's own CashCard, and a kid can carry one ticket at a time (script 58). Every flight out of the United States also asks for an exit visa: the code is printed in the book that came with the game, not in the game (What came in the box).

Two mazes are fixed, the same in every game: the Sphinx's, under Cairo, where the Guardian of the Sphinx sleeps in a dead end, and the corridors of doorways in the Martian Face. The jungles at Kinshasa, Lima and Mexico City change as the kid walks: which paths a clearing shows is picked again on each visit, so a route cannot be learned by heart.

03 · The puzzles

What stands in the way, in the order the game lets it be cleared

Each step needs something from the steps before it. The game ends when the Skolarians' device is built in the pyramid at Cairo: the glowing object, a candelabra and three crystals, one from Seattle, one from Stonehenge and one from Mars.

Show the puzzles
  1. Money. Zak's CashCard is under his desk, out of reach until he pushes it out with the phone bill. A butter knife bent prying at the loose boards sells at Lou's Loans, and Lou sells the golf club, the tool kit, the wet suit, the hat, the guitar and the nose glasses.
  2. An empty fish bowl. Sushi has to go somewhere else first: the wrench opens the sink's pipe, the stale bread from the bakery goes in the sink, the garbage disposal turns it into bread crumbs, and Sushi goes into the sink once the pipe is closed again.
  3. A disguise. The nose glasses and the hat make a kid look like a Caponian, so the one at The Phone Company's counter lets him behind it.
  4. The plane's lighter and oxygen. Toilet paper in the bathroom sink, the tap on and the call button send the stewardess to the flood, and an egg in the galley's microwave blows up and keeps her busy cleaning. With her out of the way, the seat cushion hides a lighter, and the sixth bin opened holds an oxygen tank.
  5. The Blue Crystal. At Mt. Rainier, the peanuts get the squirrel out of the way, a tree branch digs into a cave, a fire of the bird's nest and the branch lights it, and the yellow crayon drawn on the strange markings makes a door. The crystal behind it is locked into its pedestal, and comes off once the television's remote control has been used there.
  6. Annie. The Blue Crystal in the drop slot on 14th Avenue is the artifact that brings her in. Her own CashCard is under the blotter in her study.
  7. The Shaman's dance. The golf club, a Golden Boar Sand Wedge, is what the Shaman wants for his set. He dances the order in which the Face's buttons open, which differs from game to game.
  8. The Guru. The Guru's book, which the devotee at San Francisco's airport hands over for a kid's CashCard, persuades the guard that the kid follows the Guru. The Guru then teaches the Blue Crystal: it puts a mind into an animal's, as long as the body touches the ground. The lighter on the hay sends the jailer out to the fire, which leaves the jail key; the flagpole is outside.
  9. The scroll. Bread crumbs in Lima's bird feeder bring the bird down, and the Blue Crystal puts Zak's mind into it. The bird flies into the carving's eye and takes the scroll.
  10. Mars: in. Melissa takes the fuse, the CashCards and the tape from the Shuttle Bug, and the monolith takes a CashCard and gives a token. Inside the hostel, the token, a new fuse and the buttons by the doors get Leslie to the lockers, which hold a vinyl tape, a flashlight, a ladder and the broom alien. The broom alien sweeps the sand from the solar panels, the ladder reaches the Face's door, and its buttons pressed in the Shaman's order open it.
  11. Mars: the doors. Melissa records the crystal sphere's note on her tape, which the vinyl tape makes writable, and playing it opens each massive door. Leslie takes the flashlight through the doorways to the large switches, which put air in the tunnels.
  12. The crystal shards. One is in Annie's study, and comes to Zak when Annie joins (script 62); the other is in Mexico's temple, behind a symbol drawn with the yellow crayon.
  13. The Yellow Crystal. In London, the whiskey from Miami's bum makes the sentry drunk, the switch in his hut turns the fence off, and the wire cutters open it. On Stonehenge's altar, the flagpole and both shards, then the scroll read aloud, fuse the shards into the Yellow Crystal.
  14. The glowing object and the Lotto. On the biplane tour Zak is taken to the Caponians' ship. The King lets him go if he is a fan, and the Lott-O-Dictor shows tomorrow's numbers. The pilot's tune drops him with the parachute; the kazoo calls a dolphin, the Blue Crystal swaps them, and the dolphin finds the glowing object under the seaweed. The Lotto ticket with the Dictor's numbers wins $10,000, which pays for the flights to come.
  15. The maps. Drawing on the Sphinx's leg opens a way in. Through the maze, Annie reads the hieroglyphics and presses the three buttons, and the map of the Martian Face shows; Zak draws it on the torn wallpaper.
  16. Teleporting. The Shaman teaches the Yellow Crystal, which only Zak can use: it takes him to any teleport pad drawn on his map. He goes into the Face, through its doorways, and takes the ankh.
  17. The golden key. The ankh switches the force field off, and the smaller key is the one that does not crumble. The red button plays the Skolarians' message.
  18. The white crystal. Zak needs air on Mars: the wet suit, the oxygen tank, and the fish bowl last, sealed with duct tape. In the Martian pyramid, the broom alien clears the sand, the bobby pin sign opens the keyhole, and the sarcophagus's feet open the stairs while one kid stands on them. The golden key goes in the containment device, and its button lets the white crystal go for a moment.
  19. The device. The candelabra is in the cave behind the Peruvian carving's eyes. In the Cairo pyramid: the glowing object on the base, the candelabra on it, the three crystals in the candelabra, and the two big switches pulled by two kids.

Each step is played on the game's own scripts, from the first morning to THE END, in the order given in the walkthrough below.

04 · The solution

The whole game, played from the first morning to THE END

The steps that finish the game, in the order they were played on the game's own scripts. Some puzzles are picked at random at the start; the steps say which, and what to look for.

San Francisco, the first morning
  1. In the bedroom: open the desk drawer and take the kazoo; open the dresser drawer and take the phone bill. Use the phone bill on the plastic card under the desk: it is Zak's CashCard. Take Sushi in the fish bowl and the torn wallpaper.
  2. In the living room: take the seat cushion by the television, plug the power cord into the outlet, take the other cushion and the remote control under it. Use the remote to watch the news, then turn it off.
  3. In the kitchen: the small key by the front door, the egg from the refrigerator, the crayons and the butter knife from the cabinet under the sink.
  4. Back in the bedroom, pull the rug's corner and use the butter knife on the loose boards: it bends.
  5. Outside, push the bakery's doorbell three times, and take the stale bread the baker throws out.
  6. At Lou's Loans, sell the bent knife, and buy the golf club, the tool kit, the wet suit, the hat, the guitar and the nose glasses. Open the tool kit, and cut the bobby pin sign off with the wire cutters.
  7. In the kitchen, open the sink's pipe with the wrench, put the stale bread in the sink, and turn on the garbage disposal: take the bread crumbs. Close the pipe, and put Sushi in the sink.
  8. At The Phone Company, read the phone bill, take an application, put on the nose glasses and the hat, open the counter door and use the terminal. Fill the application in with the yellow crayon, and post it from Zak's mailbox with the small key.
  9. Wake the bus driver with the kazoo, pay with the CashCard, and ride to the airport.
Seattle, and the Blue Crystal
  1. At the airport, give the CashCard to the devotee, who hands over the Guru's book. Buy a ticket to Seattle at the terminal, and board.
  2. On the plane: put the toilet paper in the bathroom sink, turn the tap on and press the call button. Put the egg in the galley's microwave and go back to the seat. Once the egg has blown up and the stewardess is cleaning, take the cushion: the lighter is under it. Open the bins until the oxygen tank turns up.
  3. In the forest: give the peanuts to the two-headed squirrel, take the branch, and dig the loose dirt with it. In the cave, knock down the bird's nest, put it in the fire pit with the branch, and light it.
  4. Draw on the strange markings with the yellow crayon, and go through the door. Use the remote control in the crystal chamber, and take the Blue Crystal.
  5. Fly back to San Francisco. Read the sign on Annie's red door, and put the Blue Crystal through the drop slot.
  6. By Zak's mailbox, a letter arrives once he has been away from it: the King's fan club card.
  7. As Annie, take the blotter, and her CashCard under it. Both kids take the bus, each paying with their own card.
Miami, Kinshasa, Katmandu and Lima
  1. Miami: give the Guru's book to the bum. He reads it, gives it back, and gives up his whiskey and "all my earthly possessions".
  2. Kinshasa: through the jungle to the Shaman's village (the paths change; never take the one just walked in by). Give him the golf club, and watch the dance: note the order in which the dancers crouch.
  3. Katmandu: give the guard the Guru's book, read the certificate, and walk up to the Guru, who teaches the Blue Crystal. Light the hay by the jail with the lighter, take the jail key and the flagpole, and pay the yak to get back to the airport.
  4. Lima, by London and San Francisco: in the jungle, read the sign, put the bread crumbs in the bird feeder, and use the Blue Crystal on the bird when it lands. As the bird, fly into the carving's left eye, take the scroll, fly out and back over the valley, and give it to Zak. Leave the jungle before the aliens come.
Mars: the hostel, the Face and the doors
  1. As Melissa: in the Shuttle Bug, take the boom box, the fuse and the CashCards from the glove compartment, and the tape. Fill the helmet at the oxygen valve and put it on. Give Leslie her CashCard and the fuse.
  2. As Leslie: fill her oxygen, put her CashCard in the monolith's slot for a token, and use the token on the hostel's metal plate. Replace the burnt fuse, and press the buttons by the door. From the lockers, take the vinyl tape, the flashlight, the broom alien and the ladder. Give the vinyl tape to Melissa.
  3. Outside, use the broom alien on the sand. At the huge face, lean the ladder on the door and press its buttons in the Shaman's order.
  4. In the great hall, put the vinyl tape on the tape, the tape in the boom box, choose Record, and press the crystal sphere. Playing the recording opens a massive door.
  5. Leslie, with the flashlight on, takes the doorways purple, blue, purple to the two large switches, turns both on, and waits for the gauges to go green: the tunnels have air, and the helmets can come off.
Mexico, London and the Yellow Crystal
  1. Zak in Mexico City: through the jungle to the temple, light the torches, read the markings on the second statue, draw the symbol on the markings with the yellow crayon, and take the crystal shard.
  2. Annie and Zak fly to London, where Zak gives her the whiskey, the flagpole, the scroll, the wire cutters and both shards.
  3. Annie gives the whiskey to the sentry, pulls the switch in his hut, cuts the fence and goes to Stonehenge. On the altar stone: the flagpole, then each shard. Reading the scroll fuses them into the Yellow Crystal.
The Bermuda Triangle, the dolphin and the Lotto
  1. Zak flies from Miami on the biplane tour of the Bermuda Triangle, and is taken aboard the Caponians' ship.
  2. Push the small button, and once the pilot has gone give the fan club card to The King. Read the Lott-O-Dictor: tomorrow's Lotto numbers.
  3. Play the pilot's tune on the coloured buttons (it is picked at random at the start) and step over the line on the left: Zak falls. Put on the wet suit, and open the parachute.
  4. In the sea, play the kazoo for the dolphin and use the Blue Crystal on it. As the dolphin, swim down, move the seaweed on the right, take the glowing object, swim up and give it to Zak. Then switch back.
  5. The Caponians catch Zak and put him through the stupidity machine. Once his words come back, take off the wet suit, put the hat and nose glasses back on, and get his things back from the cabinet in The Phone Company's back room.
  6. At Lou's, buy a Lotto ticket and enter the Lott-O-Dictor's numbers on its keypad. Once the numbers are drawn, collect $10,000.
Cairo, the maps and the teleport
  1. Zak in Cairo: walk to one of the Sphinx's legs and back, then to the other: the markings are always on the leg visited second. Draw on them with the yellow crayon and go in.
  2. Through the maze to the chamber with three buttons; read the hieroglyphics (Zak can't).
  3. Annie follows, reads the hieroglyphics, and presses the buttons in the order they say (picked at random at the start). Read the map of the Martian Face, and give the Yellow Crystal to Zak.
  4. Zak draws the map on the torn wallpaper with the yellow crayon.
  5. Annie, back through the maze, goes to the pyramid's door. Zak flies to Kinshasa and gives the Yellow Crystal to the Shaman, who teaches it.
  6. Zak uses the Yellow Crystal, picks the Egyptian pyramid, pulls the lever, and Annie climbs the stairs.
Mars again: the ankh, the golden key and the white crystal
  1. Zak teleports to the Mars face chamber, draws on its markings, goes through the right doorway with the lighter on, and takes the green doorway on. Melissa plays her recording at each massive door that blocks him.
  2. Zak takes the ankh, puts it in the panel to switch off the force field, takes the smaller golden key, and presses the red button for the Skolarians' message. He gives the key to Melissa.
  3. Zak tapes the fish bowl with duct tape and puts on the wet suit, the oxygen tank, then the bowl. Outside, he fills it at the Shuttle Bug's valve and gets a token from the monolith. Leslie and Melissa put their helmets on, and get tokens.
  4. All three ride the tram. Leslie sweeps the sand pile with the broom alien; Zak opens the keyhole with the bobby pin sign and goes in through the first doorway on the right.
  5. Leslie, with the flashlight, pushes the sarcophagus's feet: stairs open on the left. Melissa climbs them; Leslie steps off and they close.
  6. Melissa puts the golden key in the box. Zak stands by the containment device; Melissa presses the button, and Zak takes the white crystal before the field closes again.
The device
  1. Zak teleports to the Peruvian eye cave, takes off the fish bowl, the tank and the wet suit, and takes the candelabra.
  2. He teleports to the Egyptian pyramid. On the base: the glowing object, then the candelabra on it, then the blue, white and yellow crystals.
  3. Zak pulls the right big switch, and Annie the left one.

Played on the game's own scripts in ScummVM's SCUMM engine, from the first morning to THE END, in about two hours of the game's clock. The random picks in that game were the pilot's tune blue, green, purple and red, the Lotto numbers 7 0 3 0 after the Lott-O-Dictor was read, and the Sphinx's buttons left, right and center.

05 · Ways to lose

Every death has its own last line, and a bad visa ends in jail

Each death prints its own last line. Before Annie has joined, Zak's death ends the game. After that, the kid who died is out of it, with everything they carried, and play passes to a kid who is left; the game is over only when none is.

The death script, 78, takes from the kid every object from 14 to 83 they carry except the helmet, prints the kid's name and one of six endings, chosen by the number the script before it left in Var[166], and then looks for another kid to play. It ends the game when the drop slot on 14th Avenue has not yet taken an artifact, or when no kid is left. In Katmandu, script 113 chooses between the life sentence and the release by Var[49]; what sets it there is not known.

06 · What came in the box

The newspaper in the box is a book of hints, and the code book is the copy protection

The game came with a manual, an exit visa code book and The National Inquisitor, the tabloid Zak writes for, dated March 29, 1997. Nearly every story in it is a hint for a puzzle in the game.

Read from the scans of the newspaper and the manual kept at the Internet Archive: The National Inquisitor and the manual. The manual credits the newspaper to Victor Cross.

The manual warns the player not to lose the code book: without it, no flight leaves the United States. A ticket from San Francisco, Seattle or Miami to anywhere outside them shows a travel key, such as "Section 4 (C 22)", and the player finds the four symbols at that section, column and row of the book (script 30). The key is picked anew for each such ticket: a section from 1 to 7, a column from A to G and a row from 1 to 30 (script 110).

The code book's symbols are not stored in the game. Script 110 works out the four symbols for the key with a few lines of arithmetic, from the section, the column and the row, so the game needs only the rule the book was printed from. A right entry gets "Successful entry. Thank you for flying Air Airlines." (script 15). Each wrong one is counted on the screen, and the fifth sends the kid to jail, where a police officer explains that it is high time the player bought a legitimate copy, because only they can stop worldwide stupidity, before THE GAME IS OVER and the game starts again (script 135).

The manual's own hint for running out of money is to "figure out how to win the lottery!", which is the Lott-O-Dictor on the Caponians' ship.
