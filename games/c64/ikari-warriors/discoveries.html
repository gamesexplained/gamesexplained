Commodore 64 · 1988 · Elite Systems

Ikari Warriors: discoveries

A token nobody is given, three places where player two is handed player one's power-ups, and a typo in the last words of the game.

01 · Secret

Secret: an eighth token, worth 800 points

pick_up_item knows eight kinds of token. Kind 3 adds 800 points to the 200 every token scores, so picking it up would be worth 1,000. The token's colour in item_colours is 12, the same value as the bullets token's, which multicolour shows as purple.

No soldier ever carries one. Tokens are handed out in turn from the list at item_cycle, and the list gives kinds 0, 7, 5, 1, 2, 6, and 4, then starts again. Kind 3 is not in it, and nothing else in the game makes a token. Why it was left out is not known.

02 · Bug

Bug: power-ups read for the wrong player

Each player has their own power-ups, kept side by side in memory and read with the player's number as an index. Two places in the code forget the index and read player one's, and a third looks up a tank shell's range by the wrong number. In a two-player game, let player one take the yellow and cyan tokens: on player two they help player one.

03 · Text

Rescved: a typo in the last screen

The message for reaching the end of the road, at ending_text, reads "YOU HAVE RESCVED COLONEL COOK".

04 · Bug

Bug: the drums' pitch jump never sounds

In drum mode, voice_update plays every other frame as noise and then raises the frequency's high byte by $31, so the noise would sound higher. The raise comes after the frequency has already been written to the sound chip, and only changes the driver's own copy, which is worked out afresh next frame. The drums play their noise at the note's own pitch. The Music page plays them as the game does.

05 · Unused

Left on the disk

  • 25 characters of shapes at unused_tile_shapes and a list of their colours at unused_tile_colours, between the tile sets. No line of the road's script loads them, and no pointer anywhere in memory names them.
  • A frame-skip switch in the music driver, skip_frames, for playing music at the same speed on American machines. Nothing turns it on.
  • A debugging sign left in the road's script runner: when all six object slots are full, map_script_step steps the background colour, a flash a programmer would see.
  • Music command $8D stores a value that nothing reads (cmd_unused).
