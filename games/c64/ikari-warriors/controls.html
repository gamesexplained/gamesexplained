Commodore 64 · 1988 · Elite Systems

Ikari Warriors: controls

Two joysticks, one fire button that does four jobs, and a lock key that stands in for the arcade's turning stick.

01 · Joysticks

Either port for one player, one each for two

The joysticks are read once a frame in the interrupt at the foot of the screen (lower_panel_irq). In a two-player game player 1 uses port 2 and player 2 port 1. In a one-player game the two ports are read together, so a stick in either port works. A player walks in any of eight directions, one pixel a frame (move_player), and turns to face the way they walk.

02 · The lock

A key that keeps your aim

The arcade game's joysticks also turned, to aim one way while walking another. Here each player has a lock key instead (check_lock_keys). With the lock on, the player walks without turning, so they can back away from soldiers while still shooting at them. Each press turns the lock on or off.
