The whole game is one street, 28,800 pixels long, walked from right to left: Chinatown, the sewers, Lo Pan's halls and the temple at the end.

01 · The map

One street from Chinatown to the temple

The game has no separate levels. The street map (street_map, $2B66) is 1,800 columns of 16 pixels, each one a tile number, and the walk starts at its right-hand end and ends at its left. Click the strip to jump the street below to that place, or scroll it.

The street drawn from the map and the 29 tiles (street_tiles, $326E), each column in the colours of the part of the street it lies in. Above the street, numbered ticks mark where each of the 12 sections begins, a cross marks each of the 16 sewer monsters' places, and the label B marks where the boss's stretch begins. In the game the whole view takes the colours of the part its left edge is in, so the colours change all at once as a boundary scrolls past.

02 · The four parts

Four parts, told apart only by their colours and their tiles

The game knows which part the view is in from the scroll position alone ($0A23), and the only thing it changes there is the play area's colours (zone_colours, $01E9 and $01ED).
