Paradroid

Every robot on a deck is the same black sprite, with its three-digit number written into the shape and a symbol that turns faster the more energy it has. The shots, the explosions, the console's pictures and the menu all come from one bank of shapes.

01 · Everything that moves

Every robot, shot and explosion, drawn by the game's own code

The robots' numbers and turning symbols are written into their shapes by the game's own routines, run on the kit's 6502 in this page, a pass of the game at a time; a pass is about three frames. The shots flicker and the explosions step as the game steps them.

The robots are drawn by the game's own code in the built site.

The robots

A robot that comes into view takes a free sprite from 1 to 6, drawn in black, and sprite_number writes its number into the sprite's shape. Each pass energy_symbols counts down a timer, and when it runs out turns the symbol above and below the number one frame of eight and starts the timer again at 64 less the robot's energy, divided by 8. A robot at full energy turns it every pass.

The player

The player is sprite 7, the 001's shape numbered as the host's, and its symbol turns by the host's energy the same way. It is white, or below 8 energy flashes through greys; in transfer mode it takes the deck's own colour, or below 8 energy flashes through hot colours.

Shots

A shot's shape comes from shot_shapes by the weapon and the direction: the 001's twin laser and the two guns, four directions each. Each pass a shot takes a colour from flash_hot picked by the noise of the sound chip's third voice, and a gun's shot flips between its two shapes. The robots fire the guns' shots.

Explosions

A robot's shot that reaches a wall becomes the small explosion, white; a robot or a robot's shot blown up, and the player's end, become the big one, yellow and multicolour. Each pass shows the next shape, and the explosion goes after its last.

The console's pictures

The robot enquiry draws each type with all eight sprites, four rows of two, from the shapes its record names; a right half the record leaves at 0 is the left half mirrored by mirror_shape into shapes $44-$47. The console's menu has a picture for each of its four items on the window's orange: the player's own numbered shape, a question mark, and the deck plan's and the side view's at double width. The chosen item's is white.

Characters that move

On the deck the energisers swirl and the marker on the deck plan changes, each frame written into the character set by animate_tiles from a list of entries with a delay for each. Two groups of four characters turn round one place every other pass (animate_chars).

Shapes nothing shows

Of shapes $30 to $A3, from $4C00 in the video bank at $4000, all but two are drawn above. $48 would be sprite 0's numbered shape but is never written, because sprite 0 is the player's shot, and $A3 is a striped block in no table or record.

The robots' and the player's shapes are made by sprite_number ($3C77) and turned by energy_symbols ($3CFB) on the kit's 6502 over the listing's memory, one memory for each robot, and the deck's characters by animate_tiles ($2605) and rotate_chars ($38A5) the same way. The console's pictures are droid_picture ($3629) as the page's port of it builds them, held against the game's own code by test_droids.js. The robots' sprite settings are droid_step's ($18CA); the shots' shot_step's ($1849) and player_shot's ($32D9); the explosions' explosion_step's ($17F4) and explode's ($1BCA). The two shared sprite colours in play, black and orange, are the ones cold_start and ship_start set.
