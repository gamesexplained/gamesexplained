06 · How a room is stored

32 tiles a room, 16 cells a tile

Each room is 32 bytes at $0C00 plus 32 times its number: 8 tile numbers across and 4 down. A tile is 32 bytes at $8000 plus 32 times its number, 4 cells across and 4 down, each a two-byte number of an 8-byte multicolour cell at $1180. draw_room lays the tiles over a background: a plain grey field outdoors, and in a building the same perspective picture of walls and floor for every room (a 16 by 32 map of patterns at $0400), recoloured from two bytes per room at $AA5E. A cell's %00 pixel pairs let that background show through, so the furniture stands on the floor.

In a building, a room on the building's outer edge gets its doorway on that side painted over by draw_area_edges: the side walls' doors on the left and right edges, and a block of the back wall on the first row.
