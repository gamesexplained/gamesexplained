Commodore 64 · 1986 · Domark

Friday the 13th: graphics

Every sprite the game draws people and weapons with, the eight heads of the fear meter, and the two pictures a body can bring up, all drawn from the game's own bytes.

01 · People

Four looks for eleven people, and Jason's own

Everyone in the camp is one of four sets of sprites, recoloured. The men's set starts at block $6D and the women's at $88; the ten others use those and two more, and Jason without his disguise has a set of his own.

‹›

Sprites are multicolour, with the shared colours 6 and 10 that start_play sets and each person's own colour: the counsellors' from counsellor_colour, the others' from the high nibble of actor_looks. Jason unmasked is drawn here in white. The walk steps through walk_frames, 0, 1, 2, 1, with 5 added for the other direction (walk_frame_offset).

A person's first block is at actor_sprite_base for the look, and the body at actor_sprite_dead. The blocks after each set's body hold the frames for being hit and for swinging a weapon; the whole run is below.

02 · Every sprite

Sprite blocks $6D to $FF

All 147 blocks from $5B40 to the end of video bank 1, in one colour. Pick a colour to see them as each person.

‹›

Block numbers are the sprite pointers the game writes; block n is 64 bytes at $4000 plus 64 times n.

03 · Weapons

Eleven objects, two directions each

The weapons lie in eleven rooms at the start of a round. Each object type has a pair of blocks at object_sprite: the first lying on the floor or flying one way, the second flying the other.

Every object is white (object_colour). Types 7 and 9 share the axe. The swing frames of the long weapons are the blocks after them on the sheet above.

04 · Fear

The eight heads of the fear meter

The panel's head is 16 characters, 4 across and 4 down, copied from one of eight 128-byte pictures at fear_heads by draw_fear_head.

Head n shows fear from 10 n to 10 n + 9. Fear 80, the most the game allows, shows head 0 again (Discoveries).

05 · Bodies

The two pictures of a body

The first time you walk into a room with a body in it, one time in five scare_check fills the play area with one of two pictures and plays the scream. Your fear rises by twice your counsellor's fear value.

Drawn by the game's own show_gore on a copy of the C64's processor, with its random choice set each way. Each picture is a 16 by 32 map of cells, its cells, and its screen colours; colour RAM is filled with one colour, 9 for the first and 1 for the second. Both lie under the KERNAL ROM.
