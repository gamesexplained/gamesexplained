About this page

The copy studied

The pages describe Data East's North American release, from two G64 disk images of it. Every snapshot and every page comes from the first. The game loads three times: LOAD1 is level one (stages one to three) together with what stays in memory for the whole game, A, B, and C are level two (stages four to seven), and D, E, and F are level three (stages eight and nine). Each load has its own listing on the Source tab, and so does the part LOAD1 leaves for the others, built from memory saved at the first instruction of each level.

The second image holds the same files, but five differ in content and two have their names swapped; its stage seven cannot be drawn (Discoveries). Neither disk shows a cracker's intro or trainer.

No game image is hosted here.

Original game credits

The title screen credits Jonathan Dunn (music), Steve Thomson (graphics), and Johnny Meegan ("the rest"), and reads "COPYRIGHT DATA EAST 1988" and "LICENCED FROM OCEAN SOFTWARE".

Memory footprint

Every byte of the C64's 64 KB, one pixel each, 512 to a row. Lit pixels are code, graphics, sound, text, tables and variables; dim areas are screen and working memory.
