Commodore 64 · 1988 · Data East

RoboCop: controls

A joystick in port 2, the space bar, and RUN/STOP. Nothing else on the keyboard does anything, apart from two words typed on the title screens.

01 · The joystick

Walking, aiming, and jumping

Once a frame the game reads joystick port 2 and uses its five switches (up, down, left, right, and fire) as a number from 0 to 31. That number picks a routine from a move table, one address for each value ($8B3C in level one). The stage decides which table is used: the walking stages have one, the crosshair stages another, and stage six, where RoboCop punches, a third.

JoystickOn the walking stages
Left or rightWalk that way, aiming straight ahead; on the stairs, keep climbing
UpAim up
Up and left, up and rightClimb stairs going up that way, or else walk aiming up on the diagonal
DownDuck
Down and left, down and rightGo down stairs that way, or else duck facing that way
FireStop and fire the way he aims; against a door, punch it
Fire and upJump
Fire and downDuck and fire, or fire along the stairs

From the move table of level one at $8B3C; levels two and three have tables of their own that do the same. Each shot needs a new press of fire.

On stage six RoboCop cannot fire at ED-209 or Dick Jones; fire and down throws a punch instead. On a hostage stage the joystick moves a crosshair and fire shoots at it. On the photofit the joystick steps through the choices for a feature, and fire accepts it when it matches the suspect's ($8DDD).

02 · The keyboard

Space and RUN/STOP

During play the space bar pauses the game and a second press goes on. RUN/STOP gives up the level and goes back to its title screen. The game reads the keyboard by its own routine ($E834), which checks one key at a time and ignores a key that is really a joystick in port 1 pulling the keyboard lines low, so a stick in the wrong port types nothing.

On level one's title screen the space bar switches the SOUNDTRACK SETTING between AURAL AMBIENCE and ATMOSPHERICS ($84EA). The first plays music during play; the second plays sound effects instead. The game has one or the other in play, never both, though the title and start-of-stage tunes play with either setting.

Fire starts the game. The title screen also listens for the letters of a word, SUEDEHEAD on level one's title and DISAPPOINTED on level two's, which skip to the next level.
