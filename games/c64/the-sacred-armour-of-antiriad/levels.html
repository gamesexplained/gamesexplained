1986 · Palace Software

The Sacred Armour of Antiriad

All 69 rooms, drawn from the game's own maps, tiles and character lists, with the creatures each one starts with.

01 · The world

The whole world on one map

Every room laid out where its links put it, on the C64-Wiki's grid of columns A to O and rows 1 to 14. Click a room to see it below.

Each room's own 32 columns of the screen, columns 4 to 35, drawn as in How it works and placed by a walk of room_links ($F25A) from room $00 at H1. The rows of rooms $06-$09, $0D-$12, $1A-$23 and $29-$37 wrap round, so the walk would put $06 at G5; the wiki draws it at K5, at the right of its row, and so does this map.

The power centre, room $00, sits at H1 on top of a column one or two rooms wide. Below row 10 the world spreads out: row 13 has fifteen rooms and row 14 has thirteen. Radiation falls a level with each band of rows, from 3 in rows 1 to 5 down to 0 from row 11 to the bottom (How it works). In rows 5, 7, 11 and 13 the last room's exit to the right leads back into the first.

02 · Room by room

Every room, with its exits and creatures

Each room as a new game finds it, with the strips of its neighbours at the sides, the creatures and items of its list, and the tables the game reads for it.

Drawn from the listing by the same port of build_room ($852C), place_room_sprites, hold_back_group and place_bomber as the How it works page, which test_rooms.js checks against the game's routines for all 69 rooms. Each sprite shows the first picture of its animation. Add-ons and recharge cells show as in a new game, before any is taken.
