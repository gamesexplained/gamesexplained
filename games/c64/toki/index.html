Commodore 64 · 1991 · Ocean Software

Toki

Ocean's conversion of TAD Corporation's 1989 arcade platformer, in which a warrior turned into an ape spits fireballs to rescue his captured love. On the C64 it shipped as a 128 KB cartridge, and the game treats that cartridge as a library it copies from rather than a program it runs.

01 · The cartridge

Sixteen banks behind one window

The cartridge holds 128 KB in sixteen 8 KB banks, and the C64 sees one bank at a time at $8000. Switching on starts bank 0, and by the time the game's first routine in RAM runs $51FB, most of the program has been copied out of the cartridge. During play the game keeps the cartridge out of sight and runs entirely from RAM, including a copy of bank 3 at $8000.

When it needs something from the cartridge, a short routine in the stack page brings bank 0 back, looks the item up in a directory of eight-byte entries, switches to the bank that holds it, and copies it into place $011F. The stage names, GAME OVER, and STAGE CLEAR never reach RAM: they stay in bank 0.

02 · Timing

A frame driven by an NMI

A CIA timer set to one PAL frame raises an NMI every frame $4D1D, and two raster interrupts split the screen between the play area $837E and the status panel $BCE1. The main loop spends its spare time waiting for the next frame $819B.

03 · Screens

From title to stage 1

Title. Fire on joystick port 2 moves on.

Stage card. Each stage has a name, and the player picks sound effects or music. The game stores five names: Labyrinth of Caves, Lake Neptune, Caverns of Fire, Ice Palace, and Dark Jungle.

Stage 1. The timer starts at 4:30.
