Commodore 64 · 1987 · Activision

Galactic Games: controls

Two joysticks or the keyboard, chosen by pressing fire. What each event does with UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT, and FIRE, and the keys the manual gets wrong.

01 · Choosing a controller

Whoever presses fire first

There is no options screen. When an event asks a player to press fire, it watches three buttons at once: the up-arrow key on the keyboard, joystick one's fire and joystick two's fire ($0D2C, $2388, $3E8C, $295F). The one that answers is that player's controller for the event, so either player can take either joystick or the keyboard, with one exception: 100m Slither, Space Hockey, and Psychic Judo refuse the keyboard together with joystick two, with "KEYBOARD AND JOYSTICK TWO NOT ALLOWED", and ask again (race_restart, $24BE, $3D40). Joystick two is read through the same port of the input chip that picks which row of the keyboard to read, so a stick pushed while the keys are read would garble them. When the same button answers both players' prompts, the computer plays player one (Gameplay). The Marathon's two players take turns, and the menu itself takes any of the three.

The keyboard keys as the code reads them ($35DE, $2468, $3F0C, $2BB3). The manual swaps two of them, giving K as left and H as right (Discoveries).

RUN/STOP leaves an event at once and goes back to the menu. The manual promises a choice between playing the event again and returning to the menu, but no event offers one (Discoveries). RESTORE does nothing: every part points the machine's NMI at an instruction that simply returns. In Space Hockey the Commodore key pauses the game ($1176).

From the events' control routines: slither st_bunched and st_wait_down, hockey read_controls, judo player_one and shield_zones, head $1AC4, marathon pick_runner and its neighbours. Within a form the stick does more: UP keeps the Flyer's power building, RIGHT keeps the Burrower digging, and DOWN crouches the Jumper and builds its spring, which springs when DOWN is let go, with LEFT and RIGHT steering it in the air (st_flyer, st_burrower, st_jumper).

03 · Typing a name

The champion's name

The only typing in the game is the Galactic Champion's name, after a championship that beats the record. Letters, digits, and space go in, up to eight; the left-arrow key rubs out the last one and RETURN finishes. The resident reads the keyboard itself, through its own scan ($CF5F) and its own table of key codes (key_table), because the machine's ROM is switched out while it runs.
