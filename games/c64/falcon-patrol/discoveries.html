Commodore 64 · 1983 · Virgin Games

Falcon Patrol

What the manual leaves out of Falcon Patrol: a score of ten thousand puts the waves back to two aircraft, and the guns stop working near the ground and while the jet hovers.

01 · Ten thousand

Bug: scoring ten thousand makes the game easier

The wave size reads the thousands and hundreds digits of the score and never the ten-thousands one (How it works). Score ten thousand and the difficulty resets to the opening wave, because the digit that rolled over is the one nobody tests.

02 · More from the code

Things the manual does not mention

  • The guns stop near the ground. Below sprite Y $7C the fire bit is forced high. A player reads this as the aircraft refusing to shoot while low, and nothing on screen says why.
  • A stationary aircraft cannot fire. $5790 returns immediately when horizontal velocity is zero, so hovering leaves the missiles unavailable.
  • Running out of fuel uses the same three instructions as being shot down. One gate at $573D opens for either, forces the byte to down-held, and flies the aircraft into the ground.
  • The AAM counter stops dead at 100. Refuelling tops up fuel and missiles together, but the missile count is only refilled while its hundreds digit is zero.
  • The radar is drawn inside the font. Blips are pixels set directly in the character bitmaps at $3680, so the whole 256-column world fits in sixteen characters at four columns to the pixel.
  • Sixteen identical solid glyphs sit at $F0 to $FF. A colour table at $4000 gives each cell its colour from its glyph, so sixteen copies of one solid block is sixteen colours of fill for the price of one bitmap.
