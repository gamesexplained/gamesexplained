1988 · SSI

Pool of Radiance

An item is sixteen bytes, and a few of them disagree with their names: the trolls' RING OF PROTECTION +2 protects nothing, BRACERS AC2 give AC 4, and a pile of 26 HUGE TAPESTRIES weighs about as much as one.

01 · The trolls' ring

Bug: the trolls' RING OF PROTECTION +2 protects nothing

Every troll in the hedge maze of Valjevo Castle carries a RING OF PROTECTION +2 and leaves it in the treasure when it dies. Readied, the ring leaves the armour class where it was. Try it on the sheet, beside a real one.

Try
    Add

    The sheet runs the game's own code, with the site's shared scripts that the built site serves.

    Each change runs the game's own routines on the kit's 6502 over the listing's bytes: compute_combat_stats ($3729) for the armour class, THAC0, damage and weight, max_load ($472E) for the most PENDRAGON can carry, and COMBAT's can_strike ($1648) for whether a bow or crossbow has what it shoots. The character is PENDRAGON, a level-1 fighter with strength 17 and dexterity 17, whom a player saved onto their copy of game disk 3 (Rules).

    Whether an item protects is set by its type, not by its name. Each of the 128 item types has a byte whose top bit marks an item that protects, and whose other bits hold a base armour class (armour_bonus, $37E3). A ring of that kind adds its plus, and only the best ring counts. Every other RING OF PROTECTION in the game, on a THEURGIST, the LEVEL 6 MUs, an 8TH LVL FIGHTER and Tyranithraxus, or in a treasure in the slums, is type 93, which has the bit. The trolls' ring is type 69, the type of the plain RING and of the RING OF FIRE RESISTANCE, whose byte is 0, so its +2 is never added.

    The ring is in one troll file, MON1F on disk 3 side A, which the hedge maze loads; the trolls elsewhere carry nothing. Its worth is a real ring's, 15,000 gold, so a shop pays 7,500 for each one, like a working ring (sale_price, $1A27).

    02 · Bracers

    Bug: the shaman's BRACERS AC2 give AC 4

    Bracers work like armour: armour class 10, less their plus. Their name gives the armour class they should make, and one pair's plus is two short of its name.

    NameWorn byPlusArmour classWorth

    The armour class before dexterity: the type's base of 10 less the plus, the figure armour_bonus ($37E3) works with. The worths are in gold.

    The pair named AC2 has the plus of the AC4 pair, so it makes AC 4. It is worse than the AC3 pair and worth more than any of them. The shaman in the nomad camp wears it. Random treasure makes bracers too, always as BRACERS AC4 with a plus of 6 (bracers_ac, $1F13).

    03 · Piles

    Bug: a pile of heavy things weighs almost nothing

    An item with a count, such as a bundle of arrows, weighs its weight times its count. The multiplication uses only the weight's low byte, so a piece heavier than 255 loses 256 for each 256 it weighs, and the loss is multiplied by the count.

    PileWhereCountEach weighsShould weighWeighs

    Every item with a count whose pieces weigh 256 or more, from the item lists on the disks. Weights are in the game's units, of which a character carries 1,500 and more with strength.

    The routine that adds up what a character carries (add_item_weight, $4093) loads the weight's low byte into one register and its high byte into another, then loads the count into the second register over the high byte and multiplies the two registers, eight bits by eight. The check made when a character takes an item does the same (item_fits, $47A1). The 26 HUGE TAPESTRIES in Kovel Mansion, 800 each and worth 300 each, weigh 832 in all, so one fighter can carry 7,800 gold's worth of tapestry, which a shop buys for 3,900. The price uses the worth's full bytes (item_price, $13B4).

    04 · The heavy crossbow

    Bug: the HEAVY CROSSBOW shoots arrows, and the 25,000-gold bow is one

    The item type named HEAVY CROSSBOW behaves as a bow: it shoots arrows, twice a round, and adds the archer's strength to the roll to hit and to the damage. No other bow or crossbow that an item uses adds strength.

    TypeShootsShots a roundStrength addsRange

    From each type's bytes: its flags (bit 0 arrows, bit 7 quarrels, bit 2 strength), the shots it makes in two rounds and its range.

    A weapon's flags decide what it needs: with bit 7 set it needs quarrels readied, and otherwise with bit 0 set it needs arrows (can_strike, $1648). The light crossbow has bit 7. Type 45, the heavy crossbow, has bit 0 and not bit 7, so it needs arrows, and it has bit 2, which adds strength's adjustments to the roll and to the damage (weapon_bonuses, $368F).

    The FINE COMPOSITE LONG BOW in New Phlan's silver shop costs 25,000 gold, and its type is 45. The HEAVY CROSSBOW in Valjevo Castle's armoury, worth 20, is the same weapon. The 3RD LVL FIGHTER and 5TH LVL FIGHTER at the buccaneers' base each carry a HEAVY CROSSBOW and QUARREL(S), so the party that beats them takes a crossbow and the one thing it cannot shoot. Three item types that no item uses, 94 to 96, are bows with the heavy crossbow's flags.

    05 · Restoration

    Bug: the CLERICAL SCROLL OF RESTORATION vanishes with a spell still on it

    A treasure in Valhingen Graveyard holds a CLERICAL SCROLL OF RESTORATION with RESTORATION on it twice. Casting the first one in camp takes the scroll away, and the second goes with it.

    A scroll counts its spells in its name. The middle word is WITH 3 SPELLS, WITH 2 SPELLS or WITH 1 SPELL, words 212, 211 and 210, and casting a spell from it takes one off the word. In camp, a word that falls below WITH 1 SPELL means the scroll is used up and gone (use_scroll_word, $1ED9). The scroll of Restoration's middle word is OF, word 167. The first spell cast from it takes that to 166, below 210, and the scroll is gone.

    Every other scroll with RESTORATION twice, in the graveyard, the city hall's reward and Mendor's library, is named WITH 2 SPELLS and lasts for both. In combat a scroll is used up only when its word reaches exactly one below WITH 1 SPELL (gone_if_zero, $2832).

    06 · Kovel Mansion's scroll

    Bug: a CLERICAL SCROLL that only a magic-user can ready

    In Kovel Mansion one of the scrolls is named CLERICAL SCROLL WITH 3 SPELLS and holds three cleric's spells: PRAYER, REMOVE CURSE and ANIMATE DEAD. Its type is 61, the magic-user's scroll.

    Readying an item checks the classes its type allows (ready_item, $4630). Type 61 allows the magic-user and type 62, the clerical scroll, allows the cleric, so a cleric gets WRONG CLASS and a magic-user can ready it. In combat a cleric is shown the spells on a clerical scroll before it is identified, by a test of the type's class byte, which this scroll does not pass (item_spells, $270D). Once it is identified, its spells show to anyone.

    07 · Aligned swords

    Three long swords that hurt a wearer of the wrong alignment

    A LONG SWORD +2 in Valhingen Graveyard and two LONG SWORD +3 in the bailey of Valjevo Castle each accept one alignment, and their names do not say so. Readied by anyone else, the sword comes off and deals its wearer damage with no saving throw.

    SwordWhereAcceptsDamage to anyone else
    LONG SWORD +2Valhingen Graveyardlawful good15
    LONG SWORD +3 (two)Valjevo Castle, the baileylawful evil5

    An item can carry a power that comes on when it is readied, in its last byte with the top bit set. These swords' power is kind 132, and the byte before it holds the rest: the low half is the alignment the sword accepts (0 lawful good, 2 lawful evil), and the high half is the damage. The routine is in the combat's effects file and again in the camp's spell file (kind_item_aligned, $AE94; aligned_item, $AE5F). No other item has the power.

    The graveyard's sword is also in an earlier list left on disk 2 side A, with no power at all (section 10).

    08 · Random treasure

    Secret: the random treasure has a cloak that never comes up

    A treasure can ask for random magic items instead of a list. Each one is picked from a table of 36 kinds by a roll of 1 to 188, and one kind, a CLOAK, has no share of the roll.

    KindRolls in 188Chance

    The table (item_chance, $1F7C) in its own order. The last kind takes the rolls the others leave.

    The roll is taken away from each kind's share in turn, and the kind that brings it to 0 or below is the one made (random_base, $1E98). The cloak's share is 0, so the roll is still above 0 after it, and the cloak is never made. Its type, 67, adds nothing to the armour class. The one item of that type, a LEVEL 6 MU's CLOAK OF DISPLACEMENT with a plus of 8, adds nothing either, while the CLOAK OF DISPLACEMENT +2 in Mendor's library is type 92 and adds its plus; both give their wearer the power of displacement. The clerical scroll comes last and takes rolls 184 to 188, 5 in 188, where the magic-user's scroll has 50.

    Each random item gets +1 on a d100 roll of 1 to 64, +2 on 65 to 95 and +3 on 96 to 100, and its worth is multiplied by the plus (random_plus, $1EE4). A scroll gets one spell of its own kind, rolled from 1 to 55 (scroll_spell, $1F2F), so RESTORATION, spell 56, is never on one. Arrows and quarrels come in piles of 2d6 (missile_count, $1F66).

    09 · Shops and the armoury

    The castle armoury fills up again, and a shop never runs out

    The barracks of Valjevo Castle have an armoury of 58 weapons and armours, free to take. The script offers the whole list every time the party stands there.

    The armoury is the arms and armour shop's list with a HEAVY CROSSBOW added, and its QUARREL(S) weigh 20 each where the shop's weigh 3. Taking an item from a treasure removes it from the list in memory (remove_item, $11BB), but the script loads the list from the disk afresh each time, asking DO YOU WANT TO TAKE ANYTHING? with no flag to stop it. A shop's list is never changed at all, so a shop sells the same DIAMOND NECKLACE as often as the party can pay for it.

    New Phlan's four shops, for general goods, jewelry, arms and armour, and silver items, run the same code as a treasure, with a flag that makes it a shop. A price is the worth times the count, times a factor the script sets, divided by 16 (item_price, $13B4). All four shops set 16, the factor that leaves the price as it is. A shop buys an item for half its worth (sale_price, $1A27) and names an unidentified one for 200 gold (identify, $1D82).

    When a battle is won, every item in its treasure with a plus adds 400 experience points for each point of it to the party's share, before anyone takes anything (magic_exp, $15A7). The plus is a byte of its own, not the name: a scroll from the random table keeps the +1 to +3 it was rolled although its name drops it (scroll_spell, $1F2F).

    10 · Left on the disks

    Item lists no script gives, item types no item uses, and names never given

    Five item lists are on a disk side whose scripts never ask for them. Two are earlier forms of treasures in Valhingen Graveyard, left on the other side of the graveyard's disk.

    FileItemsWhat it is

    A script's TREASURE loads the list of that number from the disk side in the drive, so a list is given only on a side whose scripts name its number. The scripts that choose a list from a variable are counted too.

    The table of item types has entries that no item uses, in a list, on a monster or in the random table.

    TypeWhat its bytes say

    Types 1 to 47 are named by the name word of the same number. A type above 47 has no name of its own.

    The table of name words has these words on no item, in a list, on a monster or in the random table, and the code never writes them into a name. Many name items of the AD&D rule books that the game does not have.

    11 · Every treasure

    Every item list, and where the party finds it

    A treasure names its list by number, and each disk side has its own files of that number. These are all of them, with the squares the scripts give them on.

    ListWhereItems

    The table is drawn by the page's script.

    A square is given as the map, then across and down from the north-west corner. A list met on many squares shows the first few. Three scripts choose their list from a variable, and those give no square here.

    13 · Item types

    Every kind of item the game knows

    An item's first byte is its type, an entry of 16 bytes in a table of 128 ($7B00). The type holds what the item is: where it is worn, the hands it takes, its damage, its range, its armour class and the classes that may ready it.

    Show every type with an entry
    TypeName, or the items of that typeHandsDamage, small or man-sizedLargeShots and rangeProtectionClasses

    Classes as the first four bits of the type's class byte allow them: M magic-user, C cleric, T thief, F fighter. The four higher bits are for classes the game does not let a player make.
