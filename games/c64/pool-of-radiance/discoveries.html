1988 · SSI

Pool of Radiance

Bugs and secrets in the code and its scripts: password slips that spell the real passwords in runes, a clean river that cancels the council's meeting, thieves who pay the party each time it lets them reseal a box, and a change of marching order that moves spells onto the wrong people.

01 · The runes

Secret: the runes on the password slips spell the real passwords, one rune to a letter

Wherever the game shows writing in runes, it is a word the party will be asked for later. The giants' list in the castle barracks, the slips from a kitchen slave and from Porphyrys Cadorna, the sign over a door in Yarash's pyramid and the scroll on a dead elf at Sokal Keep all spell their words one rune to a letter.

Drawn from the game's own rune font, SECSET10 on every disk, and the character numbers the scripts print with their PRINT GLYPHS command. The scripts check the answers as plain words, so the party types HARASH, RHODIA, NOTNOW, LUX, SHESTNI or SAMOSUD.

There are two alphabets. The sign in the pyramid calls its runes dwarvish, and each one is a single character wide. The others are two characters wide and look like a flowing script. In the font both alphabets run in alphabetical order, and a letter has the same rune wherever it appears. The giants' list mixes the two: today's password in the wide runes, the gate's in the dwarvish ones.

The pyramid's sign is the one to read with care. Any answer at that door but NOTNOW brings a monster whose scream costs every member 201 hit points, with no saving throw. The Solution tab lists every password and where each is asked for.

02 · Cadorna

Secret: the party's return from the Zhentil outpost is what gives Cadorna away

Lord Cadorna sends the party to the Zhentil outpost as envoys, with a sealed packet. The commandant skims the papers, asks over dinner how strong New Phlan's defences are, and that night four guards attack the party in its sleep. When the party is back, the clerk at the city hall pays for the mission with one line: "We were pleasantly surprised that you completed your mission so swiftly. Cadorna was wagering your return would be much delayed."

How the council finds Cadorna outWhere

Each of the three marks Cadorna a traitor in the same byte of the saved game. The clerk's line is the same however long the trip took: the script pays for the mission and marks him in one step.

From then on the clerk calls him the traitor Cadorna, who fled. The list moves on to Lord Urslingen and Stojanow Gate, and Porphyrys Cadorna turns up beaten and chained in a cell in the castle. Freed, he says he only thought the pool was there.

03 · The council's meeting

Bug: killing Yarash before the council's special meeting means the meeting never takes place

Once the party has taken Stojanow Gate, the clerk summons it to a special meeting, and the whole council asks it to lead the attack on Valjevo Castle. The clerk offers the meeting only while one byte of the saved game, $4A9A, is below 254. The scene that shows the land recovering, after Yarash is dead, uses the same byte for a count of its own and leaves 255 in it.

What happens$4A9A

The city hall's script offers the meeting while the byte is below 254 and holds it when it is 254, then sets 255. The eastern wilderness's script runs the scene the first time the party leaves the pyramid's area with the river clean: 1 while it redraws the land, 255 when it is done.

Only the scene is lost. The gate's reward still ends with "return there to lead the attack", and nothing in the castle reads the byte, so the party can walk in and fight Tyranthraxus all the same.

04 · Cadorna's box

Bug: Restal's thieves pay the party for resealing Cadorna's box, on every visit

Cadorna wants his family's iron box back with its seal unbroken. Restal, the old thief whose guild is reached through a well by the textile house, makes an offer: "We can duplicate the seal on that chest, for half the treasure. No one will ever know. Interested?" A yes takes nothing from the party. It gives the party half the treasure again, and marks the box as unopened.

From the treasure commands in the textile house's script. Both list the same set of items, the last of the eight amounts each command takes.

The offer comes on every visit to Restal once the box has been found, opened or not, until the council has paid for it. The script remembers the yes only among the 32 flags it keeps for the area, which the game clears when the party leaves, so a party can go out, come back and be paid again. A party that never opened the box is paid all the same. The city watch takes the box at New Phlan's gate, and the council's reward comes on top.

05 · The fortune teller

Bug: the murdered fortune teller is back at her table on the party's next visit to the slums

A ragged old woman in the slums offers to tell the party's fortune for a few coppers. Attack her instead and the game says YOU EASILY MURDER THE OLD WOMAN, and THE GODS HAVE NOTED YOUR ACTIONS. The gods' note is real: the script forgets every fight the party has won in the slums. Her death is not, because the script keeps it in a flag the game clears as soon as the party leaves.

Fights won in the slums, $4A80The fortune teller, $4A0B

What it decidesThe patrols stop once it reaches 15, and it picks her fortuneAt 255 she is gone, the patrols come more often and in bigger numbers, and a rest can be broken off

Every area's script has 32 flags of its own, $4A00 to $4A1F, and the game clears them whenever the party enters another area. Bytes from $4A20 on are kept for the whole game.

So on the next visit she is at her table with the same offer, and the patrols that had given up have to be beaten fifteen more times before they stop again.

06 · The vampire

Secret: the vampire is weak and alone in his crypt if his coffin was sanctified first

In Valhingen Graveyard a tall, pale man waits in a pit with 50 wolves. Beaten, he turns into a cloud of vapour and floats away to his crypt of black marble, and the party fights him again there. A party that finds the crypt before the fight in the pit sees a coffin full of dust, broken crosses and spilled holy water, and can sanctify it with whatever holy items it can spare.

The graveyard's script picks the record, and the hit points are in each record, both on the graveyard's disk. A party carrying the efreeti's bottle from the kobold caves has the efreeti on its side for one of the two fights, the first it carries the bottle into.

Once the vampire has fled to the crypt, walking in means the fight at once, so the coffin has to be sanctified before the fight in the pit. The sanctify is kept for the whole game. Coming straight from the pit works too, because the script counts that fight among the area's own flags, but only until the party leaves the graveyard.

07 · The marching order

Bug: Changing the marching order can move a spell onto someone else

A spell that lasts a while is kept in a list of the effects in play, each with the party position of the member it is on. ORDER, in the camp's ALTER menu, moves those effects along when the party changes places, but it moves them one member at a time in the same list. An effect handed to a member who has not been dealt with yet gets handed on again when that member's turn comes.

Who takes position #1?

PositionMemberEffects on them afterwards

The party the game comes with, as its saved game on the first disk has it, each member carrying one effect of its own. Choose who takes each position, as ORDER asks. The effects are then moved as the camp's code moves them ($0AF9, the code's address in memory): from the last position to the first, every effect on a member's old position goes to its new position.

Putting Lara Spellsword first and Arax the Bold second is enough. Lara's effect goes from position 2 to position 1, where Arax still is. Then Arax is moved to position 2 and takes everything on position 1 with him, Lara's effect included. In the game, with an effect put on Lara and the order changed that way, the game's list of effects had it on Arax afterwards.

08 · Treasure

Bug: Jewelry is never worth double

When a treasure holds gems or jewelry, each piece is given a worth as the party picks it up. A gem's worth can double, and double again, with a chance of one in ten each time. Jewelry has the same doubling written for it, and it never happens.

PieceDoubles whileOut of 2,000 pieces
Gema roll of 0 to 9 comes up 01,809 kept their worth, 172 doubled, 17 doubled twice, 2 three times
Jewelry, the four cheaper kindsa roll of 1 to 9 comes up 0all 2,000 kept their worth
Jewelry, the three dearer kindsa roll of 1 to 4 comes up 0all 2,000 kept their worth

The game's own code for each, run 2,000 times from different random numbers: the gem's loop ($1C80) and the jewelry's ($1CE6) for each of its seven kinds.

The gem's loop asks the random number routine for a number from 0 to 9 ($2DBC), and doubles while it gets 0. The jewelry's loop asks a different routine, the one that rolls a die ($2DE0), and doubles while that gives 0. A die starts at 1, so the loop ends on the first roll every time. A gem starts at a worth of 10 to 5,000, a piece of jewelry at 100 to 12,000.

09 · Time

Bug: A month of rest in camp ages the party one day

The party grows older as it plays. After every battle the game adds the days that have gone by to each member's age, and a member past the limit for its race dies of old age: 80 years for a half-orc, 120 for a human. Resting in camp heals each member one hit point a day, so healing can take weeks, and the camp hardly counts them.

Where the time passesDays gone by go upCode
Walking in town, in the dungeons and outdoorseach time the clock's day moves on$7A5F
Resting in campeach time the clock's day goes round from 30 to 0, once a month$125A

The clock keeps minutes, tens of minutes, hours, days and months as figures of their own, each with the number it comes round at ($127D). Both routines watch the day figure, but the adventure's counts when it moves and the camp's only when it comes back round to 0. The ages are added after each battle ($1E1B).

In the game, a rest of one day in the slums moved the clock on a day and left the count of days gone by at 0. One step in New Phlan with the clock at 23:59 put it at 1. A party can rest for thirty days and grow one day older.

10 · New Phlan

Secret: Nobody can rest in New Phlan

Make camp anywhere inside the town and the rest stops after five minutes with YOU ARE ROUSTED BY THE CITY WATCH AND TOLD TO MOVE ALONG. It is not a random encounter that happens to come up every time.

The town's script sets the chance of the rest being interrupted to 101 in 100 whenever the party makes camp, so the camp's roll of 0 to 99 can never beat it, and the script's own fourth entry is the watch. The How it works page shows the script itself (the scripts). In the slums outside the town, a rest of a whole day went through.

11 · The end

Secret: Losing a battle freezes the game for good

There is no screen offering another try. As a rule, when a battle leaves nobody in the party standing and nobody got away, the game prints THE PARTY HAS LOST, waits, and then runs a single instruction that jumps to itself ($0957), forever. The only way out is to reset the computer and load a saved game.

The code word at the start ends a game the same way. Before play the title shows runes and a path and asks for the word the code wheel in the box gives for them. A wrong word brings WRONG !!! and another question; a second wrong word brings WRONG !!! again and the same kind of jump to itself ($12F4).

12 · The scripts

Bug: Three script commands would be skipped wrongly after an IF

The script language has six IF commands. When an IF fails, the next command is skipped, and to skip it the game looks up how many operands it has in a table ($1625). For three commands the table is one short.

CommandOperands its routine readsThe table says
SETUP MONSTER (12)32
ENCOUNTER MENU (41)1413
ADD NPC (54)21

The command's number in brackets. Skipping one of them after a failed IF would stop one operand short, and the game would read that operand as the next command.

It never shows. Decoded command by command, none of the scripts on the game's disks puts any of the three straight after an IF.
