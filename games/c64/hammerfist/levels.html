Every room of the four loads

Each disk load has eight rooms, joined by doorways and by the edges of the screen. A room is built from tiles, squares of four by four characters, ten tiles across and five down. A tile can be drawn mirrored, so one piece of art can face either way. Each room picks its own character set and colours.

These are the rooms as you first find them. Smashing a security system swaps some of the room’s tiles for wrecked ones, and the game remembers which systems are gone when you come back. The docking area also holds two rooms that cannot be reached through any doorway; they are on Discoveries.
