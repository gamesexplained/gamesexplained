Commodore 64 · 1989 · Monarch Software

Controls

A joystick in port 2, the cursor keys, or eight letter keys walk; fire uses the item in hand; the number keys pick from the inventory.

01 · Walking

Eight directions on the keyboard

The stick walks in eight directions. On the keyboard, eight letters around K do the same, laid out as a compass with K at its centre, and K itself uses the item in hand.

U up and leftI upO up and right
J leftK useL right
M down and left, down. down and right

The house reads keys through read_action and the table at action_keys; the street has its own copy, read_command. The joystick and the cursor keys are turned into the same keys first ($625D). Walking into a wall at an angle slides you along it.

02 · Everything else

Using, dropping, and saving

KeyWhat it does
Fire with the stick still, space, RETURN, or KUse the item in hand: strike, shoot, throw, or use (Items). With nothing in hand, the best weapon carried is picked first.
1 to 7Put inventory slot 1 to 7 in hand. Slot 1 is always the special power.
SHIFT and 2 to 7Drop the item in slot 2 to 7.
A and ZStep the hand to the previous or the next item carried.
D or QDrop the item in hand, on an empty floor cell.
/, ?, or ←The save menu: return to the game, save, or load. Not on level 10. The guide, written for the PC too, gives ESC or ?.
HOME or CTRL and SSound on or off. The instruction guide gives CTRL and S, which the keyboard types as the same code as HOME, $13.6
ELeave the vending machine, as the save-menu keys also do.

The number keys and the shifted number keys are handled before the table: keys 1 to 7 give actions 8 to 14 (inventory_select) and shifted 2 to 7 give actions 16 to 21 (inventory_drop). A key held down acts once, since an action that repeats the last becomes "nothing". SHIFT and 1 is not read, so the special power can never be dropped.

In the vending machine the stick moves the cursor round the nine items, fire buys, and A and Z jump to the corners (vending_machine). On the menus the stick moves and fire chooses. Any key skips the title pictures.

03 · In Springwood

One more key on the street

On the street the same keys work, and the street knows one key the house does not: F3 fills your health to the top (street_player_turn). The game never mentions it. Discoveries has more.
