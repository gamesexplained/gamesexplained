About this page

The copy studied

The pages describe Monarch Software's two-sided disk, as G64 images of side 1 and side 2, and three more copies of side 2 that differ only in the saved games a player wrote on them. Nothing on either side looks like a crack: there is no intro and no trainer, and the original fast loader is in place. The game is three parts. The resident code (the boot file FREDDY, the loader, the fast loader, the sound effects, the panel's characters, and the sprite shapes) loads first and stays. The street part, INIT, holds the title, the menu, the character select, and Springwood. The house part, MAIN, on side 2, runs the ten levels.

No game image is hosted here.

Original game credits

The disk names no one: the credits picture names only Westwood Associates and Monarch Software, a division of Sharedata Inc.5 The people below are from the instruction guide, one booklet for the Commodore 64 and the IBM PC, so its credits do not say who worked on which version.6
