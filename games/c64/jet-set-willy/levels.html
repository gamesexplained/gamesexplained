01 · The house

The house, laid out by its exits

Every room as the game draws it on entry. Click one to open it below.

Each room is placed by its exits, starting from The Bathroom, where a game begins: a room's left exit is drawn to its left, its up exit above it. Eleven exits do not lead to the room drawn beside them, and they carry an amber bar on that edge. Most of them join the far ends of the house, so walking off the left of The Forgotten Abbey comes out in The Security Guard. A room's own number on a side (47 rooms have one) and 60 (in rooms 0 to 4) mean there is nothing there; no code checks either before taking an exit.

02 · Room by room

Each room, moving

Step through the rooms, or pick one from the list. The guardians, the arrows, the rope, the conveyors and the items' colours move as the game moves them, with Willy left out.

‹›

Drawn by jswScene in reference/jsw-rooms.js, from the room record at $B000 or $E000, the guardian records at $2443, the items at $22C3 and the arrows at $2403. Its room builder, guardians, arrows, rope swing and conveyors are each checked against the game's own code for every room, by test_rooms.js on the kit's 6502 simulator. The main loop runs about three passes a frame (measured in The Bathroom), and the pace here assumes three in every room.

03 · Exits

Exits that join the far ends of the house

Leaving by a side, Willy goes to the room its exit names, and $2DC6 then places him by which of the old room's exits names the new room, testing up, down, right and left in that order. So an exit back to the same room puts him wherever the first matching side says: leaving Entrance to Hades by any side brings him in at the bottom of Entrance to Hades, as if he had come up through its floor.

04 · Every room

Every room's record

Each room is a 256-byte record: rooms 0 to 29 at $B000 on, rooms 30 to 59 at $E000 on, under the KERNAL ROM, which is why $2E55 copies a room with the ROM banked out. Click a row to open the room above.
