About this page

The copy studied

The pages describe the British disk, published by Domark (disk name DAYLIGHTS 200787, for PAL machines). A boot program loads GM1, a loader that offers a fast loader, shows the loading picture, and loads the game's files. The program itself, TLD1, is stored encrypted, and the loader decrypts it in place with a key that a small routine in TLD8 works out; the loader carries the text "PROTECTION GRAEME ASHTON 87". The listing on the Source tab is built from memory saved during play on the first level, after the game's own start-up has copied its packed screens into place ($3F2E).

A second disk, Melbourne House's for American (NTSC) machines, holds the same game under other file names. Two of its files differ from the British disk's, and its code was not analysed (the American disk).

No game image is hosted here.

Original game credits

The title screen credits David Barbour, who wrote the game for Sculptured Software Inc, Richard Naylor, its designer, and David Whittaker for the music and effects, and says "Copyright Domark 1987", "© Eon Productions Ltd 1987", and "© Glidrose Publications Ltd 1987".
