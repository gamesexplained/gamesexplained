One building to climb per stage, fires that grow and throw debris, and creatures that visit windows. The game does the rest with short tables of objects, each updated once a tick.

A stage

The clock and the four lives

A stage starts on the Get Ready screen, which waits 200 frames (run_get_ready, $1686) while a map picture is drawn. The stage then runs one game tick every four video frames, about 12.5 a second on a PAL machine (run_stage, $1336). The timer starts at 999 and drops one unit every 13 frames, so a stage lasts at most about 260 seconds. The firefighter starts with four lives, and a hit takes one (firefighter_hit, $1CD4).

After a hit the firefighter falls until he is off the bottom of the screen. If lives and time remain, the stage restarts at its beginning; otherwise the game is over (state_1_hit, $1D19).

Fires and debris

A new fire every few frames

A random window catches fire every few frames (start_window_fire, $2195). A burning window grows and throws down pieces of debris, three at a time at most (grow_window_fires, $223A; spawn_debris, $22C8). Debris that touches the firefighter hits him. The game keeps up to sixteen fires in a table at $0964 and three pieces of debris at $09A4.

The firefighter fights back with a spray. Up to six shots can be in the air, kept in a table at $0A78, and the game refuses a third shot in the same direction (fire_spray, $2048). A shot that reaches a burning window puts it out and scores (spray_hits_fire, $242E).

Three pickup states are in the code. One switches on a spray that fires three ways (flag $78; $1A75). A second starts a countdown from 59 in $74 ($1A93). A third is the warp: for 40 ticks the firefighter shoots up the building eight units a tick, spraying as he goes ($1AAA). Which of these the retro blog's suit and parachute pickups are has not been confirmed.1

The top

A last object, then the rescue and the descent

Near the top of the building a special object drawn from four sprites starts to move (update_top_object, $2706). It runs through four behaviours: it rises, swings sideways toward the firefighter, dives past him, and leaves to one side ($27E7). Reaching the top starts the end-of-stage states (stage_end_sequence, $1B34), which play a run of pictures and then let the firefighter float down the building ($1C32). The time left and the fires put out are then added to the score (stage_complete_sequence, $C232).
