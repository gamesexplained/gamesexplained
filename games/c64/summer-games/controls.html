Commodore 64 · 1984 · Epyx

Summer Games: controls

One joystick passed from player to player, or two for the races. What each event does with the stick and the button, and the few keys the game reads.

01 · One joystick or two

Passed round, except in the races

The menu's fourth line switches between one and two joysticks (menu_joysticks). Most events are taken in turns, one player after another, and read only the joystick in port 2: the menus, the pole vault, diving, gymnastics, and skeet (read_stick, stick_dir, move_sight). The races put two players side by side, and with two joysticks the second plays from port 1. In the running events the first fire button pressed at the start of a heat decides which joystick runs in which lane (introduce_heat, irq_frame); a player with no opponent races the computer pacer (Gameplay).

RESTORE with RUN/STOP held leaves an event and goes back to the main menu (nmi_restore, restore_key, sw_nmi). RESTORE on its own does nothing.

From the code: pole vault pv_state3, pv_vault, pv_swing; diving read_position; running read_sticks, dash_move; gymnastics wait_set, on_board, flight; swimming swimming, stroke; skeet move_sight, sk_pressed.

The manual gives each grip a direction of its own: forward for high, left or right for medium, and back for low.1 The code steps the grip one place at a time instead, and each try starts at medium (pv_state3). From medium the manual's directions work, but from high, back gives medium rather than low, and left or right only accept the grip shown. The keys H, M, and L choose a grip directly. For the vault itself the manual and its quick-start card agree with the code once "back" and "forward" are read as the stick's down and up: down plants the pole, up swings the vaulter up (pv_vault).

03 · The keyboard

Names, numbers, and yes or no

The keyboard is used for typing names on the registration screen, up to ten letters, with DEL to rub out and RETURN to finish (type_name); while choosing a flag, S picks it as fire does (frame_flash). On the menus a number key picks its line at once, and Y and N answer the yes-or-no questions (menu_select). The pole vault reads RETURN as fire, Y and N for "TRY THIS JUMP?" and "ANOTHER JUMP?", and H, M, and L for the grip (key_pressed, key_rows).
