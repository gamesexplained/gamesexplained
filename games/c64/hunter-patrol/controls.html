One joystick in port one: up dives, down climbs, left and right turn the ground, and fire has to be let go and pressed again to move on.

01 · The joystick

Up is down: the stick works like a flight controller

Port one only. The game reads the stick from $DC01, the port-one register (read_joystick, $0D14).

StickWhat happens
Upthe plane dives: it moves down the screen, towards the ground
Downthe plane climbs, up to the top of its range
Leftthe plane banks left and the ground slides right, at up to two steps
Rightthe plane banks right and the ground slides left
Firea shot leaves the plane, when the last one has gone

Live test on 13 November 2026, from a snapshot of play with the plane at the top of its range: up held for 40 frames set the climb rate $37 to $FF and moved the plane's y from $51 to $67, and it went on down to $88 in the next 40 frames with the stick let go. Down set the rate back to 1.

steer_plane ($193C) looks at the stick every eighth pass of the main loop. Each push moves a rate one step, and the rate stays where the stick left it, so the plane keeps climbing or diving with the stick let go, and the turn holds. Left and right move the scroll speed $22 between −2 and 2. The plane itself never moves sideways: its x stays at $A0, its picture banks, and everything else slides past it (more). Only one shot flies at a time (fire_shot, $1E40).

02 · Fire

Fire to start, to continue, and to shoot

The same button does three jobs, and two of them wait for it to be released first.

Fire starts a game from the title screen. Before each strafing run, and after the last, the game puts up "PRESS FIRE TO CONTINUE" and waits for the button to be let go, pressed, and let go again ($2B83, $2E80), so a button held down from the last pass does not skip the message.

03 · Keys

RESTORE restarts the game

The cracker's documents name one key, and the game does what they say.1

RESTORE starts the game again from the title screen. The bytes at $8000 hold the signature a cartridge would ("CBM80"), and the KERNAL's RESTORE routine, finding it, jumps to the address they give, restore_restart ($2F30). In a live test on 13 November 2026 a press of RESTORE in play reached $2F30. With the cracker's level-skip trainer on, the C= key ends the level as if the target had been destroyed (Discoveries).
