One joystick in port one: up dives, down climbs, left and right turn the ground, and fire has to be let go and pressed again to move on.

01 · The joystick

Up is down: the stick works like a flight controller

Port one only. The instructions on the disk say so, and the game reads the stick from $DC01, the port-one register.1

StickWhat happensWhere
Upthe plane dives: its height value rises, which puts it lower on the screen$193C sets the climb rate $37 to $FF; $1990 adds it to the plane's sprite position $8061
Downthe plane climbs, up to its highest position, $52$193C sets $37 to 1
Leftthe ground scrolls the other way, at up to two steps$193C takes the scroll speed $22 down to $FE
Rightthe ground scrolls at up to two steps$193C takes $22 up to $02
Firea shot leaves the plane$193C calls $1E40 when the fire bit is clear, and that takes a free shot record

Live test on 13 November 2026: from a snapshot of steady play, each direction held for 24 frames through the emulator's port-one joystick. Up moved the height value from $51 to $5E, left and right set $22 to $FE and $02, and the plane's sideways position $8060 stayed at $A0 in all four tests.

The stick is read once a frame and acted on every eighth frame ($1920, $193C), so the plane answers in steps. Holding right does not make the plane move right. The plane stays where it is and the landscape moves (more).

02 · Fire

Fire to start, to continue, and to shoot

The same button does three jobs, and two of them wait for it to be released first.

Fire starts a game from the title screen. After a strafing run, $2E30 puts up a message and waits for the button to be let go, pressed, and let go again before it continues ($2E80 to $2E9D). A button held down from the last pass does not skip the message.

03 · Keys

RESTORE restarts the game

Only one key is documented, and it is the cracker's.

The cracker's instructions list one in-game key: RESTORE restarts the game. The instructions are the cracker's, not Mastertronic's. The restart was not tried.1
