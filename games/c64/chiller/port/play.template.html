The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the video chip would draw it. Start on begins a new game and takes it to that level the way the game gets there, finishing each level before it in turn with its crosses taken, so the way home finds the five levels as play leaves them. The cheats work on the game's own routines: with Endless energy the routine that takes a step off the energy bar takes nothing, and with Ghosts don't hurt a ghost's touch adds no poison. Skip this level does what taking the last cross does.
