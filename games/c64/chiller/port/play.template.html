The sound chip's three voices

Each bar is a voice's loudness, worked out from the attack, decay, sustain, and release the game sets; the mark is its sustain level. The note is the voice's frequency register on a PAL machine. The tune plays on voices 1 and 2; the game's sound effects use voice 3.

Parallax uses full-colour concept artwork at the game’s pixel size, adding imagined scenery to the forest on the outward and homeward journeys: distant trees, a moon, mist and foreground branches move gently as you walk. The game’s platforms, sprites and rules stay the same. With Parallax selected, Painted scenery adds a full-colour concept skin for the playable trees and ropes. It is an imagined rendition of the level; collisions still use the original map, and characters and collectibles remain live. Poison has a red cap with white spots, food is amber, 100-point bonuses are rotating gold coins and crosses have a blue halo and a moving silver highlight, each with a dark outline. Item ledges align with their live pickup positions. The bridge follows the game’s live erosion tiles, opening holes as its ledges crumble. Other locations keep their original picture.
