02 · Enemy paths

Nine scripts, direction bytes ended by $FF

path_step $CC12 moves one enemy a pixel at a time and, every 8th step, reads the next byte of its script from path_scripts $4A00-$4A8F: 0 up, 1 down, 2 left, 3 right, $FF loops back to the start. Step through a slot's script below; the arrow shows the direction it is about to read.
