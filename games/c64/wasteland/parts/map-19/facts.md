# Map 19, Base Cochise, robot assembly — verified technical facts

A level of Base Cochise where robots are made: its units are the "Robot Auto-assembly station" (message 19) and the "Robot Program Modification station" (message 21), with command consoles behind cage doors (message 16), security monitor panels in the walls (message 13) and a bridge over a radioactive waste pile (message 33). Map 18's exit 0 leads in at (18,1), and map 17's exits 13 and 14 at (1,30) and (19,10). It is 32 x 32 squares, stored on side 4 of the disks as 24 pages from T19/L0 (track 19, logical sector 0), entry 19 of the map directory at T35/L14 (engine `point_at_maps`, `$28CE`). Engine `enter_map` (`$25BD`) loads it to `$3400-$4BFF` and unpacks its tile layer to `$DE00-$E1FF`. It is drawn with tile set 8 (tiles-8). Squares are given as (column, row). Every address here is this map's unless a part is named beside it.

## The map

- The class layer is `$3400-$35FF` and the number layer `$3600-$39FF`. The map record is at `$3A00`, since the map's byte in T35/L8 is not `$40` (engine `load_map_pages`, `$2606-$2616`).
- `$3A2C`, the size: 32 (engine `enter_map`, `$25D3`).
- `$3A30`, the tile set: 8 (engine `$25EE`).
- `$3A33`, the tile drawn off the map: 0 (engine `draw_square`, `$0B5E`).
- `$3A2F`, random encounters: 1 chance in 60 after each step as stored (game `random_encounter`, `$B017`). `$3A31`: monster types 1 to 5 (game `$B047`). `$3A32`: the groups take the first 5 class-15 records (game `$B040`). The map's own code moves all three (below).
- `$3A34`/`$3A35`, the time a step takes: a quarter of a minute (fraction 64, minutes 0; game `advance_clock` `$AF32`).
- `$3A36`, ticks: 1 a step, added to `$D2` and to the elapsed count `$04-$06` (game `$AF58-$AF72`), so game `health_tick` (`$B795`) runs every 16th step (game `$AF75-$AF7B`).
- Squares, 1,024: 418 blocking squares (class 11), 308 of class 0, 190 message squares (1), 60 radiation squares (9), 42 check squares (2), 2 exits (10), 2 question squares (8), 1 encounter (3) and 1 loot bag (5). Message square 0 (`$3A66`), which shows nothing and changes nothing, holds 186 of the 190, the corridors among them.
- Records, named by the class lists of header words 3-18 (`$3A06-$3A25`, engine `action_record` `$09B8`): 21 random-encounter slots (class 15, `$3EC2-$3FBD`), 13 check squares, 10 encounters, 10 remote changes, 7 blocking squares, 6 action squares, 5 tile squares, 5 question squares, 4 message squares, 3 radiation squares, 3 exits and 1 loot bag. Word 1 of the message list and word 4 of the encounter list are $0000. No square or change names blocking square 6 (`$3DBF`, message 0).
- Text: messages 0-78, packed from `$41E2` (header word 0, engine `select_map_text` `$1E4F`); 46-78 are the combat phrases the header's bytes +`$37`-+`$5B` name. The random-encounter slots show message 12, "Robots don't like humans very much (except when using them for target practice)." Message 43 is Vax's, named by his record (below).
- Monsters: the names from `$40C2` (header word 1, game `print_monster_name` `$9E5D`) are 1 Titanium Clawer, 2 Steel Reaver, 3 Killer, 4 Hunter, 5 Silicon Sniper, 6 Silver Strangler, 7 VTOL Auto-fire Robot, 8 Threshing Crawler, 9 Xenon Laser Cannon, 10 Octotron and 11 Vax; name 0 is empty. The 12 monster records start at `$4182` (game `monster_record` `$9EA4`).
- One character can join: header word 20 (`$3A28`) holds the NPC list at `$3FBE`, whose word 1 is Vax's record at `$3FC2` (below).
- The walls answer a bump with "The thick metal walls have lots of scratches on them from robot limbs, but they still seem quite solid.", "Silvery titanium walls surround you and feel as if they are slowly squeezing in on you." and "These walls, though still quite solid, do show some evidence of laser burns and other battle scars." (blocking squares 0, 1 and 2, `$3DB3-$3DB8`, messages 7-9, 392 squares). The assembly stations are blocking squares 3 and 4 (`$3DB9`, `$3DBB`, 12 and 13 squares), "The Base Cochise emblem is stenciled on the side and below it is printed: Robot Auto-assembly station." and "The end of this robot assembly station is hot! It has two doors large enough to disgorge an Octotron!" (messages 19 and 20).

## Its own code

The code list, header word 19 (`$3A26`), is at `$3CE2` and has six entries, `$3CEE`, `$3CFD`, `$3D0C`, `$3D1B`, `$3D2A` and `$3D37`, for action squares 0-5 (`$3CD0-$3CE1`). An action square whose record byte 0 is below `$80` runs entry n (game `run_action` `$8845`: engine `api_map_class_entry` `$0428` with Y = `$10` at game `$885E`, then game `jump_work_ptr` `$AC69`), entered with the carry clear (the ASL at engine `$09D5`). When the routine returns, a set carry has the view redrawn (game `$8872`); then the record's change at +1/+2 is applied to the party's square (game `$8840`, `alter_party_square` `$B105`), and a written change runs the new square at once (game `run_square_action`, `$ACC1`). All six routines return the carry clear, and all six action squares change to tile square 4 (`$3CB4`, tile 72, no message), so each runs once on its square.

Each routine reads one byte of the map record through `$61`/`$62`, which hold `$3A00`, and writes it back only when the new value is in its range:

- `$3CEE` more_encounter_groups, action square 0: `$3A32` + 1, unless that is 21 or more (`$3CF5`).
- `$3CFD` encounters_more_often, action square 1: `$3A2F` − 2, unless the result is 0 or has bit 7 set (`$3D04`, `$3D06`).
- `$3D0C` more_monster_types, action square 2: `$3A31` + 1, unless that is 11 or more (`$3D13`).
- `$3D1B` fewer_encounter_groups, action square 3: `$3A32` − 1, unless the result is 0 or has bit 7 set (`$3D22`, `$3D24`).
- `$3D2A` encounters_less_often, action square 4: `$3A2F` + 2, unless that carries past 255 (`$3D31`).
- `$3D37` fewer_monster_types, action square 5: `$3A31` − 1, unless the result is 0 or has bit 7 set (`$3D3E`, `$3D40`).
- `$3D28` encounter_setting_kept, a CLC and RTS, is where they go when the byte stays as it is.

The three bytes lie in the map record. Engine `enter_map` writes the map back to the disk before it loads another (engine `$25C3`, `save_map` `$2856-$288F`, all 24 pages from `$3400`), so the settings stay with the map on later visits.

### The panels and the consoles

- The security monitor panels are check squares 2, 3 and 4 (`$3ACF`, `$3AF0`, `$3B11`, flags `$80`, which may be walked onto): "A security monitor panel is set into the wall at this point." (message 13). (message 14) and makes the square remote change 0, 1 or 2 (`$3DD5`, `$3DF1`, `$3E0D`); a failure gives message 15.
- Each of those remote changes counts its entries from the party's square (bit 0, relative): it makes five squares plain, without a redraw, the row below the panel for remote change 0, the column east of it for 1 and the row above it for 2, each from two squares on one side to two on the other. It then makes the party's square action square 0, 1 or 2, which runs more_encounter_groups, encounters_more_often or more_monster_types.
- Check square 2 stands on 8 squares, (3,23), (20,23), (25,23), (3,26), (8,26), (13,26), (18,26) and (23,26); check square 3 on 9, (29,10), (26,13), (29,15), (26,18), (29,20), (0,21), (11,21), (16,22) and (29,25); check square 4 on 5, (23,8), (28,8), (25,11), (15,20) and (12,25).
- The 22 panels name 110 different squares. 108 are message square 0, along the corridors beside the panels. - The consoles stand behind cage doors, check squares 1, 6 and 8 (`$3AB0`, `$3B53`, `$3B93`, flags `$00`): "A stylish metal cage door keeps you away from a computer console. Check square 1 is at (16,13), (24,13), (16,17) and (24,19), 6 at (14,13), (20,13), (24,16) and (20,19), and 8 at (18,15), (22,15), (22,17) and (20,21). - Question square 1 asks "Select Intel Mode: A) Hunter B) Killer C) Admin" (message 22), answered with one key. A, B and C give remote changes 3, 4 and 5 (`$3E29`, `$3E31`, `$3E39`). - Vax's record (`$3FC2`) is copied into the roster when a Hire succeeds (game `$A424-$A433`). ### The bridge

- The radioactive waste pile is rows 13-18 of columns 1-11, radiation squares 0, 1 and 2 (`$3D87`, `$3D8A`, `$3D8D`) on 60 squares: rows 13 and 18 cost 4d6 CON ("A wave of heat washes over you and the ground shimmers.", message 28), rows 14 and 17 6d6 ("Your eyes tear and a ringing fills your ears.", message 29) and rows 15 and 16 10d6 ("Your whole body feels ready to combust, and your skin itches mightily.", message 30). Each member not wearing a Rad suit (item 41) takes it and gets radiation poisoning, bit 0 of +`$28` (game `$82E0-$8332`). Messages 28 and 30 are even, so on the 4d6 and 10d6 rows the member's AC, rolled in d6, comes off the damage; the 6d6 rows, message 29, ignore armour (game `$90AF-$90B4`).
- The bridge crosses it in column 6. (6,13), (6,14), (6,17) and (6,18) are message square 0 with tiles 16, 17, 17 and 18, and the middle, (6,15) and (6,16), is radiation square 2 as stored. South of the pile, (2,19)-(2,21) are message square 2 (`$3A68`): "A bridge rises over the radioactive waste pile. The center is retracted and the controls are on the other side of the waste." (message 33).
- The controls at (10,10), question square 3 (`$3D6D`), ask "Bridge Control E)xtend R)etract" (message 32), answered with one key. E gives remote change 6 (`$3E41`), "The bridge slowly extends and locks in position." (message 35): (6,15) and (6,16) become tile square 3 (`$3CB1`, tile 17), (2,19)-(2,21) message square 0, without a redraw, and the controls question square 4 (`$3D77`). R there gives remote change 7 (`$3E5D`), "The bridge parts in the middle and locks in place on each side." (message 36), which puts back radiation square 2, message square 2 and question square 3. R on question square 3, or E on question square 4, makes the square message square 3 (`$3A6A`), "The bridge is in that position already." (message 34), whose change, `$FE`, trades the question back (engine `alter_square`, `$0A28`).
- Encounter 3 (`$3C4A`) at (9,10), beside the controls, is three Xenon Laser Cannons: "Twin barreled Xenon Fusion Lasers orient on you and pulse purple energy bolts at you!" (message 31).
