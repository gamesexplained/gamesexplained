# Map 40, Las Vegas, jail and Fat Freddy's — verified technical facts

The jail, Fat Freddy's Place and three small buildings of Las Vegas share this map. Las Vegas enters them with "Entering the jail." (map 12's message 20, on its exit 1, to (6,1)), "Entering Fat Freddy's Place where even low rollers can have a ball." (map 12's message 40, on its exit 2, to (11,23)) and "Entering a small beat up building." (map 12's message 55, on its exits 3, 4 and 5, to (26,1), (24,30) and (1,17)). The ways out are "Leaving the jail." (message 7, exit 6, to Las Vegas at (61,2)), "Leaving Fat Freddy's." (message 21, exit 1, to Las Vegas at (18,25)) and "Leaving the building." (message 20, exit 5, back to the party's return point). It is a map of 32 by 32 squares. It is stored on side 3 of the disks from track 11, logical sector 2, 35 pages: entry 40 of the map directory, T35/L14. `enter_map` (engine `$25BD`) loads it to `$3400-$56FF` and unpacks its tile layer to `$DE00-$E1FF`. Tile set 4 draws it (part tiles-4). Every address here is this map's unless a part is named beside it.
