# Map 16, Base Cochise, outside — verified technical facts

The ground around Base Cochise and the dome over it: map 0's exit 42 says "Entering Base Cochise." (map 0's message 27) and leads in at (16,30), and the gate bears "an old sign, barely visible, denoting this as Base Cochise." (message 1). It is 32 x 32 squares, stored on side 4 of the disks as 21 pages from T22/L4 (track 22, logical sector 4), entry 16 of the map directory at T35/L14 (engine `point_at_maps`, `$28CE`). Engine `enter_map` (`$25BD`) loads it to `$3400-$48FF` and unpacks its tile layer to `$DE00-$E1FF`. It is drawn with tile set 8 (tiles-8). Squares are given as (column, row). Every address here is this map's unless a part is named beside it.

## The map

- The class layer is `$3400-$35FF` and the number layer `$3600-$39FF`. The map record is at `$3A00`, since the map's byte in T35/L8 is not `$40` (engine `load_map_pages`, `$2606-$2616`).
- `$3A2C`, the size: 32 (engine `enter_map`, `$25D3`).
- `$3A30`, the tile set: 8 (engine `$25EE`).
- `$3A33`, the tile drawn off the map: 63 (engine `draw_square`, `$0B5E`).
- `$3A2F`, random encounters: 1 chance in 75 after each step (game `random_encounter`, `$B017`). `$3A31`: monster types 1 to 8 (game `$B047`). `$3A32`: the groups take the first 3 class-15 records (game `$B040`).
- `$3A34`/`$3A35`, the time a step takes: half a minute (fraction 128, minutes 0; game `advance_clock` `$AF32`).
- `$3A36`, ticks: 2 a step, added to `$D2` and to the elapsed count `$04-$06` (game `$AF58-$AF72`), so game `health_tick` (`$B795`) runs every 8th step (game `$AF75-$AF7B`).
- Squares, 1,024: 728 of class 0, 125 exits (10), 84 blocking squares (11), 66 check squares (2), 16 encounters (3), 2 message squares (1), 2 remote-change squares (12) and 1 loot bag (5).
- Records, named by the class lists of header words 3-18 (`$3A06-$3A25`, engine `action_record` `$09B8`): 20 encounters, 10 check squares, 10 loot bags, 10 exits, 4 tile squares, 4 blocking squares, 3 remote changes, 3 random-encounter slots (class 15) and 2 message squares.
- Text: messages 0-63, packed from `$403F` (header word 0, engine `select_map_text` `$1E4F`); 31-63 are the combat phrases the header's bytes +`$37`-+`$5B` name. Nothing names messages 4 ("Chunks of concrete blown long ago from the base's armored shell block easy access to the base itself.") or 28 ("You have found the hostile base. The territory around it seethes with metal warriors.").
- Monsters: the names from `$3DCC` (header word 1, game `print_monster_name` `$9E5D`) are 1 Titanium Clawer, 2 Vanadium Vulture, 3 Chopter, 4 Steel Reaver, 5 Silicon Sniper, 6 Silver Strangler, 7 VTOL Auto-fire Robot, 8 Threshing Crawler, 9 Laser Pod Blaster, 10 Plasma Constrictor, 11 Laser Hypermortar, 12 Stainless Steel Flechette rifle, 13 Mk 10 Turbo Meson Cannon, 14 7mm Vulcan Cannon, 15 Life-seeking Flamethrower, 16 Sonar-targetted Proton Carbine, 17 Xray Laser Projector, 18 Wire-guided Anti-personel Missile and 19 Gauss Needle Projector; name 0 is empty. The 20 monster records start at `$3F9F` (game `monster_record` `$9EA4`).
- Nobody can join here and the map has no code. Header words 19 and 20 (`$3A26`, `$3A28`), the code list and the NPC list, hold `$3CC7`, the exit list, as do the lists of classes 6 and 8. No square or change is of class 6 or 8, and every encounter record's NPC nibble (+9, high) is 0, so neither list is read (game `run_action` `$885C`, `order_hire` `$A424`).
- The walls answer a bump with messages 6, 7 and 8, the concrete wall "built to withstand a direct hit with a nuclear warhead" among them (blocking squares 0-2, `$3D1D-$3D22`), and the ruins with "Fragments of old buildings dot the landscape and obscure clear lines of sight." (blocking square 3, `$3D23`, message 23, 54 squares).

## The dome and the weapon pods

- The dome's sloped walls are check square 2 (`$3ABA`, flags `$00`) on 42 squares around (11,4)-(22,15): "The sloped concrete walls of the base's dome are steep and are covered with metal barbs." (message 12). A Use of Climb 4 or a Rope passes, "You scramble over this section of wall." (message 13); a failure costs 6d6 CON less the member's armour roll (flag bit 0 clear, game `$90AF-$90B4`), "You slip and catch yourself on one of the barbs." (message 14). Neither changes the square.
- Encounters 0, 1 and 2 (`$3B71`, `$3B7D`, `$3B89`) are the weapon pods: "A weapon pod rises up from the base dome, and its weapons snap out and center on you." (message 20). Each is stationary and holds one of a kind and 1 to 3 of another: a Gauss Needle Projector and Sonar-targetted Proton Carbines; an Xray Laser Projector and Wire-guided Anti-personel Missiles; a Life-seeking Flamethrower and 7mm Vulcan Cannons. Encounter 4 (`$3BA1`) is 1 to 3 Chopters, and leaves loot bag 0 (`$3C89`), which is what (16,13) holds as stored.
- Wiped out, pods 0, 1 and 2 become check squares 3, 5 and 7 (`$3AC9`, `$3AEB`, `$3B0D`), which are what (17,10), (17,12) and (16,11) hold as stored: "The weapons pod cover is jammed on the broken robots, so it cannot retract fully." (message 15). A Use of Plastic explosive, TNT, a LAW rocket, a Sabot rocket or an RPG-7 wastes the cover and leaves "a hole in to the base dome" (message 16): check square 4, 6 or 8 (`$3ADE`, `$3B00`, `$3B22`): "It looks like a long way down. Using a rope would be prudent to facilitate descent." (message 18). A Use of a Rope there, "Your rope drops down into darkness and you descend." (message 19), makes the square exit 0, 2 or 4.
