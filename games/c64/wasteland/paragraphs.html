The booklet in the box holds 162 numbered paragraphs, and the game sends the player to 89 of them. The other 73 are bait, and ten passwords printed in them spring traps the game has ready. Below the traps are the two Bloodstaffs, every paragraph and what cites it, and every word the game takes at the keyboard.

01 · False passwords

Secret: the booklet's ten false passwords each spring a trap in the game

When the screen says "Read paragraph 49", the player turns to the booklet and reads it. The booklet also holds paragraphs that no screen ever names, and ten of them hand over a password. The game's guards know all ten words. A party that gives one is let through, or seems to be, and pays for it.

    Each false paragraph is a copy of a true one with the word changed. Paragraph 49 is a torn note on which THANATOS has been scratched out and KAPUT written under it. Paragraph 161 is the same note with MORS written under it, and paragraph 5 is a third torn note that ends in AZRAEL. A player who was sent to paragraph 49 has the right word. A player who went looking through the booklet has three notes and no way to tell them apart.

    A question square holds a list of words, and each word names what the square turns into when the party types it ($88E3). The game compares the typed line with each word, letter for letter ($8944). At Ugly's hideout the list has four words. KAPUT turns the square into one that sets the gang's guards at peace ($419B). AZRAEL and MORS turn it into a square that first runs a routine of the map's own ($4206), and then does what KAPUT does. The routine goes through every character in use and flips the bit that says whether the Ranger is a man or a woman. The guard says the same line for all three words, and nothing on the screen marks the swap. It shows later, in fight messages that say "her" where they said "him".

    THANATOS, the word the true note shows scratched out, is on the list too. The guard answers "That is the old password." and asks for the party's section leader, and takes STINGER, PIGFACE, MADWOLF, SKULKER, or GRUNT.

    Faran Brygo's door works the same way. KESTREL opens it. PHOENIX and CLOVER open it after a routine ($3C58) has set one bit in every Ranger's record: the bit for the disease the doctor calls Wasteland Herpes. A Ranger with any disease stops getting CON back over time ($B854), and stays that way until a doctor is paid to cure it.

    In the Temple of Blood a prisoner begs for a launch code. MOTEKIM frees him. MORTAR and ATOM print "You've entered the self destruct code by mistake." and take 20 six-sided dice of CON from every Ranger. That is 20 to 120 points each, and a Ranger who falls to −50 is dead.

    The nomads' guard does no harm. For LADYBUG or LIZARD the guard says "Read paragraph 42." Paragraph 42 is a page of the booklet's false story about raiders from Mars, and it is the only page of that story the game ever names. The true word, CATERPILLAR, leads to paragraph 39, which says where to dig, and to a bag with a Shovel in it.

    02 · The Bloodstaff

    Secret: there are two Bloodstaffs, alike in every byte, and only one is real

    The game's table of items lists the Bloodstaff twice, as items 62 and 63. The two have the same name and the same eight bytes. Item 62 is the staff the story is about. Item 63 is a fake, and seven bags hold one.

    The bishop in Needles keeps the staff once the party has brought it back to him. He hands it out again to a party that says the right word. For DIPSTICK, the word in paragraph 129, his square becomes a bag that holds item 62. For BUZZARD or PASTEL, the words in the false paragraphs 18 and 73, it becomes a bag that holds item 63. He says "Please be careful with it." either way, and the two staffs look the same in a Ranger's pack.

    03 · The booklet

    The game sends the player to 89 of the booklet's 162 paragraphs

    Every number below is a paragraph of the booklet. A white one is named somewhere in the game's text. A coloured one is named nowhere: the writers put it there for readers who skip ahead.

    • the game cites
    • false copies of a true paragraph
    • the story about Mars
    • jokes and loose ends

    Press a number. For a paragraph the game cites, the card shows the game's own message and the map it is on. For one it never cites, the card says in a line what the paragraph holds.

    The game's text names a paragraph in 83 messages on 21 maps. They name 82 different paragraphs, because two messages in the Temple of Blood both name paragraph 124.

    Seven more are cited without their number being in the sentence. On level 3 of the Sleeper Base a terminal offers seven personnel files. Each choice shows the message "Read paragraph" and then a second message that is only the number: 52, 40, 100, 63, 70, 91, and 101. That makes 89.

    Of the 73 paragraphs the game never names, 22 are copies of a true paragraph with one thing changed: a password, a number on a bracelet, a direction to dig in. The true bomb code is 11-27-57-04-30 (paragraph 26), and paragraph 159 gives 31-17-54-07-99. The bracelets of Hewey, Dewey, and Louie read 11, 16, and 27 in paragraphs 147, 155, and 107, which is the combination of the safe in Ugly's hideout. In the false paragraphs 143, 9, and 27 they read 13, 27, and 99.

    Another 32 tell one long story in which the robots come from Mars, Finster is a spy raised by aliens, and the Rangers die at the end. The game cites one page of it, paragraph 42, and only to a party that gives the nomads a false word. Two paragraphs speak to the reader directly. Paragraph 22 warns that nobody was told to read it, and paragraph 145 says of itself that it can be reached from no place in the adventure.

    04 · Questions

    Every question the game asks at the keyboard, and every word it takes

    The game asks 67 questions that are answered with a typed line, and between them their lists hold 186 words. Press a word to give it to the game.

    The typed line can hold 16 characters ($1D06), and a word only counts when the whole line matches it. One word on the lists is too long for that. In Finster's mind maze a voice says "Only I am allowed further", and the list takes five forms of his name. IRWIN JOHN FINSTER has 18 characters, so the line stops at IRWIN JOHN FINST and the game turns the party away. The other four forms fit.
