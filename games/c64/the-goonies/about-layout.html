About this page

The copies studied

The pages describe the Datasoft release on disk, the copy that prints V 1 BY SES on the V key ($84E8). The game is one load. Everything except the scenes' pictures is in memory from the moment the loader hands over at $0800, and the addresses on these pages are those of that load. The Source tab's listing is built from memory saved in scene 1, after the start-up check at $0800 has wiped itself.

A second disk, the U.S. Gold release, was run to its own hand-over and compared byte for byte. It is a different build: about 23,000 bytes between $0800 and $FFFF differ, spread over the whole program. Its music driver differs in 71 bytes, and 2,144 of the 2,247 bytes at $C029-$C8EF, where this build keeps its tunes, differ too, so its music is not the music on the Music tab. Nothing else about the U.S. Gold build was analysed, and no claim on these pages is about it except where the C64-Wiki is quoted.

No game image is hosted here.

Original game credits

The game shows no names on screen. That the SES of its version line is Scott Spanburg is an inference, and the music driver names John A. Fitzpatrick in text nobody sees ($BA34). The roles below come from the sources named beside them.
