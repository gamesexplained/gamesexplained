The thirteen levels

Each level is a map of blocks, and each block is four characters across and four down (the How it works tab explains the format). Pick a level to see its whole map as it loads, before anything in it is destroyed, beside the screen as it looks in play. Enemies, symbols and the hero are not part of the map, so the page leaves them out.

Only the first level of each world loads characters, their colours and blocks. The second and third levels load a map of their own, and the game draws them with the characters, colours and blocks that the first level left in memory. The page draws them the same way.
