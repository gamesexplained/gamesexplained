The thirteen levels

Each level is a map of blocks, and each block is four characters across and four down (the How it works tab explains the format). Pick a level to see its whole map as it loads, before anything in it is destroyed, beside the screen as it looks in play. The buttons under a level’s note scroll the map to the places the note names. The enemies and symbols are not part of the scenery: each level keeps them in lists of its own (the How it works tab explains them), and Objects marks every one on the map. A circle is a symbol lying free: red, green, blue or light blue for the four weapon and shield symbols, and white with a letter for the others, 1 for a 1UP. A grey square is a floating container that holds a symbol. A red dot with a number is any other object, by its type, and what each type is differs from level to level. The yellow frame is the screen the level starts on.

Only the first level of each world loads characters, their colours and blocks. The second and third levels load a map of their own, and the game draws them with the characters, colours and blocks that the first level left in memory. The page draws them the same way.
