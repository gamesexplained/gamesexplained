Commodore 64 · 1985 · Epyx

Summer Games II: controls

One or two joysticks, and a different use of them in every event. Every event reads the sticks through CIA 1, $DC00 and $DC01.

01 · Joysticks

Press fire to pick your stick

The menu's fifth option sets the number of joysticks (opt_joysticks), kept at $087D. With two, the events where two players race at once let each player choose a stick by pressing its button at the start, as in Epyx's Pitstop II (Rowing's at_start). In the menu the stick or the number keys choose an option; the names are typed on the keyboard, and on the flag pages S plays the anthem and fire chooses the country. RUN/STOP with RESTORE goes back to the menu from any event.

02 · The events

What the stick does in each event

EventFireStick
Triple Jumpstarts the run, and picks the joystickright: the hop and the step; left: the jump; up in the air: lift
Rowingready at the startleft lifts the oars, right pulls them through, in rhythm; read every fourth frame
Javelinstarts the run; each new press adds speedleft starts the throw and, held, raises the angle 9° at a time; right lowers it
Show jumpingstarts the count; gets up after a fallup: start and quicken; down: slow, or back away after a refusal; right at a fence: the jump; left at the landing: stay on
High Jumpjumps in the take-off zoneright: start and speed up the run; left: halve the speed, or pass a height; up and down: move the run-up line; up in the flight: clear the bar
Fencingheld: attackwith fire: step, thrust, or lunge; without: one of nine guards
Cyclingready at the startround, clockwise: right, down, left, up, with the crank
Kayakingstarts the runup: stroke forward; down: stroke back; left and right: sweep to turn
Swimmingready at the start; in the water, the stroke's timingright: dive; left at the far wall: turn

The manuals and reviews describe a few controls the code does not read the way they say. Show jumping's fire button makes no jump in the code: the fence's own code and the stick decide it (st_ride). Rowing reads F5 and F3, which set and clear a flag that nothing reads (Discoveries).

03 · Keys

Three keys for the other title

Holding F3, DEL, and cursor down as the title loads shows BUMMER GAMES II (title_start).
