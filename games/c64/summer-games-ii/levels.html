Summer Games II: the kayak course

Kayaking is the one event with a course to learn: a river 196 rows long with fifteen gates, three of them to be passed facing back toward the start. Here it is whole, drawn from the event's own map.

01 · The river

Fifteen gates from the bottom to the top

The run starts at the bottom and ends past the top. Each white label sits between its gate's two numbered flags, found on the map from the gate's place in the event's tables (gate_x and gate_y, 15 rows above the flags); a gate marked ↓ is passed facing back toward the start (gate_facing).

The river's characters, 40 to a row from river_chars, each coloured from river_colours 8 KB on, drawn with the river's multicolour character set at $4000 and the background colours the event sets: light blue water, white, and brown. The water's own characters are redrawn every eight frames in play to make it move; this is their first frame.

The game scrolls this map down the screen as the kayak climbs it, copying a new row in from the map whenever a whole row has passed, onto the screen not on show, and then swapping the two screens. Every gate but one is entered from below. Gate 10 sits two rows below gate 9, so the kayak passes gate 9, turns, and comes down through gate 10 from above, facing downstream (gate_entry_side). Gates 6 and 13 are entered from below like the rest but facing downstream, so the kayak goes through them backwards. The map itself marks all three: gates 6 and 13 have a yellow flag lettered R in place of the red one, and gate 10's red and blue flags stand the other way round. The map of the water's flow, which would push the kayak downstream, is empty in this release (Discoveries).
