1991 · Infogrames

North & South

Every picture that moves in the game, drawn from its own files: the soldiers, riders and cannons of the battle, the fighters of the fort and the train, the figures and panels of the map, and the title's.

01 · The battle

Every soldier, rider and cannon, black on the field

The 20 objects of a battle are not sprites. Each picture is cut into the field's bitmap as a black shape, so a side's whole army is drawn from 39 pictures, the Confederacy's facing left.

Never drawn

The pictures are 24 × 21 sprite images (shapes, $6400), and every set pixel becomes black (draw_mask, $93A4). Each card plays one action with the pictures the game's tables give it: infantry $8640-$8660, cavalry $866C-$8672, cannon $8682-$868A. The infantry and the cannon step every 10 frames ($9E15, $9DEC), the cavalry every 6 ($95A8), at the soonest: a picture changes only when the main loop comes round to it, so with every unit moving the steps stretch to 17 or 18 frames. A cannon's hit takes one picture each time the loop comes round to its side ($96CF), about every 11 frames.

The infantry's two walking pictures alternate as the line marches. A soldier fires in two pictures, aiming and then the flash, standing or kneeling by his place in the line (inf_aim_pic, $8654), and a soldier hit shows two pictures of his fall before his slot is emptied (infantry_pictures, $9519).

A rider who is hit counts on through eight pictures ($9651): six of him falling, then two of the riderless horse, which repeat as it runs on to the enemy's edge. Falling back, the riders take the other side's walking pictures, which face the other way ($95D2), so no side needs a set for riding home.

Six of each side's pictures are never drawn. The charge's first picture is one: cavalry_pictures moves the frame count on before it reads it, so a charge starts at its second picture ($95AD, $9618). The rider fallen into the river is another: hazards marks the slot to go at the same time ($A16C), so draw_objects empties it before the picture can be drawn ($91D8).

02 · The fort and the train

Each fighter is two sprites, posed by the game's own code

The attacker and the guard are each an upper and a lower sprite. Ten routines, one for each thing a fighter can be doing, choose the pair and how they sit, and the cards here call those routines once a frame, as the game does.

Run by the fort's own pose routines (pose_lo, $8CC1) on the site's 6502 over the fort's memory, with the fighter's timer counted down once a frame as fort_timers ($8934) does. The Confederacy's pictures are the Union's 60 pointers on (pose_uniform, $8CF9), in the uniform colours at $80FC; the fort sets the sprites' shared colours to yellow and black ($83B1). The train holds the same 120 images byte for byte and the same routines at other addresses.

Standing and walking, the halves sit one above the other. A walk steps through six pairs, one every four frames (pose_walk, $8D0D), and the jump borrows the third of them for the time in the air, its height from the arc at $8BF9. Knocked over, dropping or lying down, the two sprites lie side by side.

The dog (dog_sprite, $97CB) is two sprites 24 pixels apart, three frames every five; the barrel (barrel_sprite, $979F) explodes in nine pictures, one every three frames ($9721); the flag waves every eight (fort_flag_wave, $8B10) in the defender's colour. The clock and the boot run along the bar under the fort, where the shared colours are light red and brown ($835F); the clock rings and the boot walks once the game is decided, five frames a picture ($8945, $899D).

Never shown in the fort or the train

The train's only figures of its own are at the locomotive. Its wheels are not sprites: twelve characters of the train's set are rewritten every two frames with one of three frames (train_wheels, $899F), and two white sprites, the rods, go round over them through four places, one every three frames (train_rods, $8A14; $80E8, $80EC). The driver in the cab is one sprite of two frames, five frames each (train_driver, $8AAE).

The locomotive's end of the train's map (train_map, $2610), drawn as the Maps tab draws it, with the wheels' characters, the rods and the driver moving as the train moves them. The rods sit at the tile column $26F4 names, the driver 29 pixels after them at line $7F.

03 · The map

Every figure on the map, at the game's pace

Up to 32 sprites move over the strategic map, shared out among seven hardware sprites by a multiplexer. Each frame, every figure that is showing adds its sprites to a list, and the multiplexer draws the list down the screen.

Drawn from the map's sprite images ($6400-$7FFF) in the colours each figure's routine gives, with the shared colours light red and black ($0FDB). Most figures move across the map as they play and here stay in place; the interception is the game's own routines run on the site's 6502 over the map's memory, so its two figures move as they do on the map. The supply train steps every 6 frames while it moves, and on the next frame each time it turns onto a new line (train_anim, $1C10; $1D5E); the cloud every 7 (storm_step, $15D7), one time in 16 as lightning; the Mexican's bomb every 7 ($17BD), the tomahawk and the interception every 6, the Indians' signal every 10. The interception's raider is in the other side's colour.

Never shown

The cannon's smoke before a battle is drawn into a sprite as it shows: smoke_sprite clears image $96 and smoke_build copies the smoke from image $94 into it in three steps, two frames apart ($222A). After 13 frames as image $96, the smoke changes between images $95 and $94 every five frames (smoke_anim, $21E1).

04 · The panels

The map's animations are pictures drawn over pictures

Some of the map's moving pictures are not sprites. A routine copies a block of bitmap into a window of the screen (draw_picture, $1447), saving what was there first, and an animation draws the next picture into the same window.

05 · The title and the menu

The title's figure plays four animations at random

Over the title's scene a figure of four sprites, two by two, plays one of four short animations, a step every five frames, and picks the next at random when it ends (anim_pick, $8B02). In the third it throws something, a fifth sprite spinning up and down an arc.

The animations' first images (anim_base, $8A38) and their steps ($8A3F-$8A4C), blue with light blue and black ($8145-$815D); the thrown sprite's eight frames step every third frame and its arc is nineteen moves of three frames ($8A50).

The menu's characters, one head for each side and level (head_sprites, $6400), four sprites each: the interrupt at line $55 moves the upper pair down and points it at the lower images (menu_irq_heads, $15AA). Blue for the Union and grey for the Confederacy, with light red and black ($140D-$1424). The cursor is sprite 0, white and the only one in high resolution ($1400, $16AF).
