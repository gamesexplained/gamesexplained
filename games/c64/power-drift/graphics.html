1989 · Activision

Power Drift

Every kart is three body sprites and a head, the player's and the near rivals' alike, and a rival far up the road is one sprite or two. The faces along the top are characters, and so is everything beside the road, cut out of a sheet of them with a picture for each of 16 distances.

01 · Everything that moves

Every kart, head and light, put together as the game puts them

The karts here are built by the game's own routines, run on the kit's 6502 in this page over the listing's memory, and drawn where the sprite tables place them. They move at the game's own rate: a race frame is about three video frames.

The karts are put together by the game's own code in the built site.

The player's kart

The kart is band 0 of the sprite table: the driver's head from behind, orange, and three body sprites side by side in the kart's colour. The stick turns it a frame every fourth race frame, from hard left to hard right, and with the stick at rest it turns back a frame every other race frame. Every eight video frames the body takes its second set of pictures, so the wheels turn; the straight kart has only one.

Spinning out, and overtaking

A spin steps through eight frames, one every other race frame, and the kart turns right round: for five of them the head is the driver's face from the front. When the player overtakes, the driver turns to look back.

The rivals

A rival has a picture for each of its 32 distances, from rival_pictures, and a routine for each pair of distances adds its other sprites: a whole kart with a head up to distance 5, a second sprite up to 11, nothing after that. Up close a rival is the player's own kart with its driver's head. Each picture here is a different rival, in its driver's colour.

The start

Before the race, bands 3 and 2 of the sprite table hold the START sign and three lights, unlit. After two seconds the first lights red and the second yellow; two seconds later the second goes out and the third lights green, and a second after that both bands are switched off.

The podium

Bands 1 to 3 take the finishers. Each is a kart in its driver's colour, its middle sprite $56 where the player's kart has $8B, with the driver's face from the front, and place_finisher writes the place, 1st, 2nd or 3rd, over the top five lines of that face's sprite. The first to finish goes at the top.

Shapes nothing shows

Shapes $40 to $A2 are 64 bytes each from $D000 in the video bank at $C000, and every sprite is multicolour, with black and light grey shared. Those drawn above are all the game uses; these are in no table or routine the game reads. Blocks $80-$82 are left out: they hold rows of the panel's screen, not shapes.

The player's kart is set_kart_frame ($1A40) run on the 6502, from kart_body_frames ($1A68) and kart_head_x ($1A72), with its head from head_behind ($1779); the spin from spin_kart_1 to spin_head_tables ($1749-$1778), as kart_spin reads them; overtaking from head_looking_back ($1785). A rival's records are made by place_rivals' routine for its distance, from rival_picture_routines ($3B63), run on the 6502, in the colour driver_kart_colours ($3722) gives. The start is start_band3 and start_band2 ($17F3, $1804) with the colours and waits start_lights ($179D) writes. The podium is place_finisher ($1994) run on the 6502 for three finishers. The shared colours are setup_video's.

02 · The faces

Twelve drivers, two faces each, in characters

The faces on the select screen and along the race's panel are blocks of three by three characters from the panel's character set at $F000. The chequered marker is a sprite behind them.

Top: the faces from face_cells ($129D), with the select screen's cursor, sprite 7 at pointer $83, moving to the next face every 21 frames as driver_cursor_loop moves it. Bottom: the second faces from face2_cells ($1321). Drawn in the panel's colours, the multicolours light grey and orange that race_irq_top sets, on the sky of course B, stage 1.

The marker sits behind the characters, so it shows only where a face is blank or light grey. In a race the same sprite marks the player's own face on the panel. Every sixteenth race frame panel_faces_tick gives a face from 5th place to 12th its second picture and puts the last one back, so the faces along the panel change their expressions one at a time.

03 · The roadside

Every roadside object, coming nearer

Each of the 24 kinds of object has a picture for each of 16 distances, a block of characters cut out of the tile sheet. Here each comes from distance 15 to 0, a step every race frame, as fast as the kart's top speed brings them.

Each picture is drawn from scenery_pictures ($3E0B): for each kind, 16 records of a width and a height in characters and where the top-left character sits in the tile sheet ($6E00-$B7F5), whose rows are 256 bytes apart. The characters are the race set's, with the block of 175 the stage loads before the object first appears, in the object's own colour (scenery_colours, $3E3D). A character 0 is left out, as draw_tiles leaves it out. The colours are those of course B, stage 1.

A step is the track moving on by 32 of the position's units, so at speed level 32, high gear's top, it comes once a race frame, and at 16 every other one. Each of the four scenery slots comes a distance nearer with each step, and a slot that passes the kart takes the next record of the stage's scenery list (scenery_tick). How the view is put together from these pictures, the road and the horizon is on How it works.
