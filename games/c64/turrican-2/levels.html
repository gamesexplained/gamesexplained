Turrican II: The Final Fight

The stored terrain of every level, from the countryside to the final vertical climb.

01 · worlds

The eleven levels

Each square below expands into four rows of four characters. The map byte exchanges its two four-bit halves before selecting that square. Hatched cells hold separately identified code or tables, or lack a terrain definition.

The complete stored rectangles are shown. The two narrow flying strips use their active five-row extent; level 5-2 uses 150 rows. Source tiles are the handover state, before runtime animation and destruction. Some bosses share their nominal map space with control data.
