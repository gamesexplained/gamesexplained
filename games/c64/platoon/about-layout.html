About this page

The copy studied

The PAL disk, both sides. Side 1 holds the first section (OVL1); side 2 holds the three section programs O1, O2 and O3 and their loaders LAY1 to LAY3. Each program is read from memory at the moment its loader hands over to it.

Sources

  6. Andy Wilton, “Platoon”, ACE #7, April 1988, page 59: the five-man structure, treatment of the war, and reported C64 gameplay faults. The C64 rating is 801/1000; 530/1000 belongs to the Spectrum version.

Memory footprint
