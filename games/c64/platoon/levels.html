Commodore 64 · 1987 · Ocean

Platoon: maps

Every map of the game, drawn in the page from the bytes each section loads: the jungle and village scroll, the tunnel map, and the 120 rooms of the last jungle.

01 · Section one

The jungle and the village

The whole map of the first section: 20 rows of 90 blocks from $A000, each block 8 by 6 characters from $8000. The screen shows five blocks across and three down at a time. The top three rows are drawn with the character set and colours the game swaps in when the soldier climbs up to them ($BAB6), the rest with the jungle's ($BAFD).

Scroll sideways to follow the map. White tags mark where things happen ($3B25): ▶ the start, ■ the bridge, the explosives and the villager, ▼ the trapdoor down to the tunnels. Under each hut is what a search finds there ($CC8D): ★ the torch or the map, ✕ a booby trap, · an ordinary find. Tags sit where the soldier stands, two blocks into the scene; that offset is read from where the huts and their search ranges line up, not traced. The background and shared colours are the jungle band's: black, brown and light green ($057A-$058E).

02 · Section two

The tunnel map

The map in the corner of the tunnel screen is nine windows of 19 by 19 characters from $9C20, one shown at a time and swapped as the soldier nears an edge ($146B). Each character is a square: open tunnel (code 2), water (code 3), the walls' edges (codes 4 to 9), or rock.

Drawn by square kind, not with the game's characters: open tunnel pale, water marked with waves, edges grey, rock dark. Windows are numbered in the order of the table at $1390, whose last four entries point at room pictures instead.

03 · Section three

The map of the last jungle

The rooms sit on a grid ten wide and twelve high, numbered across from 0 ($3591). Each room has a kind from 1 to 16; kind 0 is outside the maze. The kind picks one of ten maps of 30 by 25 characters ($3609, $361A) and a list of trees, huts and other shapes drawn over it ($36D1), so rooms of the same kind look alike.

The whole maze, every room drawn at its place on the grid with its number; the soldier starts in room 105 (START), and the three rooms of kind 16 (BUNKER) are Barnes's, whose edges the room test at $33E7 handles apart from the others. Empty squares are outside the maze. Press a room to draw it larger below.
