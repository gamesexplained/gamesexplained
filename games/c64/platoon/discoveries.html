Commodore 64 · 1987 · Ocean

Platoon: discoveries

A programmer's cheat, a difficulty setting nobody can change, and an editor's placeholder left among the sprites.

01 · Cheat

Secret: ZACH1 stops enemies and their bullets from killing the soldier

In the first section, holding Z, A, C, H and 1 together makes the main loop at $07B5 write rts over the first byte of $2BC8. That routine is the only test of whether the second figure has caught the soldier or a bullet in the enemy's shot slot has hit him, and with it gone neither can start his fall ($2CB7). Holding the five keys in the emulator changed $2BC8 from $EA to $60 (7 October 2026); N writes the $EA back.

The cheat does not cover everything that hurts him. A booby trap still springs and takes three hits ($39C1), walking on to the blown bridge still drops him into the river ($3D38), and the patrol still ends the game if the bridge stands ($35CB). The later sections, each a program of its own, carry no copy of the test.

02 · Quirk

A skill setting fixed at 2

The byte at $1EC6 is set to 2 when the game starts and never changed. With 2, the scroll moves two pixels a step instead of one ($B12A), enemies walk at 3 while the soldier walks the same way, and a jump while walking left holds 15 passes instead of 20. Unreached code at $1E80 once set it with F1 and F3, keys the finished game uses to switch men instead.

03 · Leftover

An ICON among the sprites

Sprite shape $A7, stored at $69C0 in the first section, is a filled block with the word ICON cut into it: an editor's placeholder that shipped with the game.
