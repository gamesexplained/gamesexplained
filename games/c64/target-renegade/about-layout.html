About this page

The edition examined, the evidence behind the explanations, and the people and tools that contributed to this minisite.

The copy studied

The Source tab and the analysis come from a supplied EasyFlash cartridge image, the OneLoad64 edition of the game.7 It starts with five lives where the manual says three (Discoveries).

A second copy, a disk image headed "ASS PRESENTS:", opens with a cracker's logo before the game.8 Its picture file, TARGET REN. SPIC, drew the loading picture shown on the overview when run on its own. The cartridge's own loader also shows a picture before the title. The two pictures have not been compared.

Footprint

Every byte of the C64's 64 KB, one pixel each, 512 to a row. Lit pixels are what this game is: its code, graphics, level data, sound, text, tables, and variables, exactly where they sit in memory. Dim areas are screen and working memory; dark is unused.
