Commodore 64 · 1985 · Activision

Little Computer People

The man is four sprites, the dog two, and every picture they wear is unpacked when it is needed from 183 packed images. Here they move by the game's own code: its walking, its stairs, its turning head, its exercise and its talking.

01 · In the house

He walks where you send him, the dog where it likes

Click anywhere in the house and he walks to the nearest of the spots he walks to, by the stairs if it is on another floor. The dog picks its own spots, rests between walks, and eats when it finds food in its bowl.

Each pass of the main loop runs the game's update_figure_sprites ($0FD2), which unpacks at most one new picture and writes the eight sprites at raster line $FA; move_figure ($16C4) for the man and then the dog; and upper_body_turn ($ACF3), which turns his head a notch at a time toward a spot it picks at random near where his body faces. The loop makes at most one pass a frame, about 46 a second, and so does this view. A click sets his target location and movement state 2, as the activities do. Left alone, he is sent to a spot at random every few seconds. Fill the dog's bowl runs fill_dog_bowl ($2A63); four of the dog's 21 destinations are the bowl. The moods choose which of the three sets of faces his head is unpacked from ($035F).

02 · In one place

Turning, exercising and talking

Two of his activities, run from the phase where he starts to move, here from his back. To exercise he first turns to face you, one view at a time: three-quarter back, side, three-quarter front, front. To talk he turns until his face is to you, then changes his mouth every tenth pass.

Exercise is activity 27 from its phase 2: exercise_start ($2E10) turns him to the front with turn_to_pose, which steps through the canonical pose of each view (view_class_poses, $2541) on every fourth tick; then exercise_move ($2E2D) sets the next of exercise_poses ($2E6B) every eighth pass, a random number of times, 64 or more; here the activity starts again when it ends. Talk is the talking sub-machine act41_talk ($237E): talk_mouth picks one of the mouths in talk_mouth_poses ($250D). The widest mouth comes only while his speech sound is not playing, so here the game's sound requests and its sound driver's frame routine run too, with nothing sent to the speaker. Both run only on passes when nothing is waiting to be redrawn, as the activity dispatcher does.

03 · The other pictures

The poses of his other activities, and the props

The images above are 83 of the 183. The other 100 are the poses his other activities set, his pictures for carrying something in front of him, five heads that do not change with his mood, and the props he carries or moves.

Decoded by this page from the packed bytes at $E5C1–$FFB9, found through the table of offsets at $E453, with the colours of the house: light green body, blue ($D025) and skin ($D026). Click an image.
