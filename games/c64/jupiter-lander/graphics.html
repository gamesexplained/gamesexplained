Jupiter Lander

A lander, two flames, two jets and a fireball, each one moving here at the speed the game moves it.

01 · The lander

The flames are sprites of their own

The lander is sprite 1. The main flame is sprite 2 and a side jet is sprite 3, and place_sprites ($E3F9) puts all three at the same position, so the flames are stacked on the hull rather than placed beside it. A flame's sprite is switched on only while its thruster is held. The main flame swaps between shapes $F8 and $F9 on every pass of the game, about 29 times a second, and that swap is the flicker.

Sprite 1 is cyan, sprite 2 red and sprite 3 orange, and the lowest number is in front. A pushes the lander right and D pushes it left: each key names the jet that fires, $FA on the left or $FB on the right, and when both are held D wins. In a close-up, close_view_sprites ($E3A1) writes $0E to $D017 and $D01D, which doubles sprites 1 to 3 both ways; that is all the zoom does to the lander. Shapes $FC to $FF are empty.

02 · The explosion

The lander turns into its own fireball

Hitting rock does not swap the ship for a picture of an explosion. explode ($E44B) switches the flames off and adds one to sprite 1's pointer seven times, from the lander at $F0 to $F7. Between pictures, flash_explosion_colour ($E4F3) moves the sprite's colour on by two, four times, three delay units apart. Each picture shows four colours, and the fireball runs through the eight odd-numbered colours of the palette. A colour lasts about 33 ms and a picture about 131 ms, so the explosion is over in 0.92 seconds. Then the screen stays empty for 200 delay units, about two seconds, before the next lander.

The lander is shown for a moment before each explosion, and most of the empty pause after it is left out.
