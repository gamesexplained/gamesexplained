One joystick, and a few keys for pausing and restarting.

01 · Joystick

Joystick port 1 does everything

Plug the joystick into port 1. Port 2 is read only for a second player.

InputAt the titleIn play
Left and rightChoose the cave: A, E, I or M, at levels 1 to 3; levels 4 and 5 start in cave A ($884A)Walk left and right ($75CE)
Up and downChoose the level, 1 to 5Walk up and down
FireStart the game, once F1 has shown the menu or the demonstration has played ($8918)With a direction, take what is in the next cell, or push a boulder, without moving

On the first title screen, the one with First Star Software's logo, fire does nothing. F1 swaps the logo for the menu, and from then on fire starts the game (Discoveries).

02 · Keys

Function keys, space and run/stop

KeyEffect
F3 at the titleCycle through one player, two players on one joystick and two players on two ($880F)
SPACEPause, and press again to carry on ($80C3, $8103)
RUN/STOPGive up the cave in progress, losing a man, once its opening countdown is over ($8103)
F1In play, restart the whole program ($8103); on the first title screen, show the menu ($88CA)

With two players the turn passes to the other player each time a man is lost, as long as that player has men left, and each player's men, score and cave are saved at that point ($8B05).
