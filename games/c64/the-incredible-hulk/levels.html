1984 · Adventure International for Commodore

The Incredible Hulk

Every room of the adventure and every picture on its disk, drawn by the page from the disk's own files with a copy of the game's decoder.

01 · The rooms

Twenty rooms, ten pictures

Each room as the game draws it when a new game starts: its picture, then the items lying there at the start, each a small picture of its own laid over it.

A room's picture is the file R01 and its picture number on the disk, and its items are the files B01, the item's number and R. Some rooms share a picture: the table at $7A72 sends rooms 13 and 14 to the dome's picture, 5 and 6 to the tunnel's, 7 and 8 to the field's, 10 and 11 to the first hole's and 17 and 18 to the fuzzy area's. What tells those rooms apart is what lies in them, and in rooms 7, 8, 13 and 15 the table at $7A0E swaps an item's picture for another, so that the same dome or hole looks right from each side. Rooms 0 and 1 have no exits in the table: room 0 is where items wait until an action brings them out, and the player leaves room 1, the chair, only by an action.

The exits table at $A4D0 holds few of the ways between rooms. Most moves are actions: GO DOME, GO HOLE and GO CRACK each test what is here and send the player on, and the two warps move the player on at the end of every turn, room 18 to one of four rooms by chance. The list beside each room gives both.

02 · The other pictures

Scenes, close-ups and the inventory

Pictures 81 to 99 belong to no room. Actions show the scenes, LOOK at certain things shows a close-up, and the inventory has a picture with each carried item laid over it.

All drawn by the page from the disk's files, with the same decoder as the rooms above. The inventory, picture 98, is drawn here with every item that has an inventory piece carried, as the game would draw it.

The scenes come from the commands that show a picture by number (command 90 and command 89). Picture 80, which command 89 shows, is not on the disk, and no action uses that command. The close-ups come from a table at $6A12: LOOK at the dome, the bio gem, the egg, the ants, the bees, the ring, the cage, the gas outlet or a gem shows one when the thing is here, from 90 to 97. Picture 0 is the dark, shown in place of a room when the player cannot see.

03 · The items

Forty pieces for fifty-five items

Every piece file on the disk: those that end in R lie over a room, those that end in I over the inventory.

Each piece drawn alone on black, the size of the whole picture, so that its place in the picture shows. Its header gives its first and last column and its top and bottom row.

A piece is an item's number: the room drawing asks the disk for B01, the number and the letter, and the disk's loader answers that there is no such file when the item has no picture there. The game then notes the number in a table of 256 at $7675 and does not ask again. Fifty-five items share forty files, and the last is numbered 250, which no item can ask for.
