1987 · Palace Software

Barbarian: The Ultimate Warrior

The two barbarians are painted into the picture, nine blocks to a frame, and sprites carry only what rises above them and the computer's fighter's coloured clothes. The blood, the rolling head, the goblin and the ending's figures are sprites with pictures of their own.

01 · Everything that moves

Every frame of the fight, drawn by the game's own code

A barbarian has 91 frames, and each of the game's 30 moves is a list of them. Here every move plays its list, five frames a second as in a fight, each frame drawn into the arena by the game's own routines, with the sprites it lays over the arena. The frames no move shows stand at the end.

The frames are drawn by the game's own code in the built site.

The 30 moves

The right-hand fighter wears a sprite over its body in the skill level's colour, which is how the computer's warriors differ. Move $95 is in the list of moves, but nothing starts it.

The frames no move shows

Frames $27 and $2A have pictures of their own and sit between the frames of the two rolls; $15 and $47 share another frame's picture.

The sprites with pictures of their own

The head, its shadow, the blood and the goblin, and the figures of side 2's ending: each a sprite stepped through its list of pictures by the game's own routine, at its own rate of fifty steps a second. The goblin is two sprites, a top half and a bottom half; Mariana and Drax are three or four, placed as the ending's records place them.

The sprite pieces the frames are put together from

Each frame of a move adds up to four sprites to its blocks: the parts that rise above the band, such as the top of the head, a fist or the sword, in shapes $20-$39, and the pieces the right-hand fighter wears over its body, in $53-$7F, in the skill level's colour. The two drawn faint are body pieces no frame names. The head, the blood, the goblin and the ending's figures are whole pictures, and play above.

The frames are drawn by the game's own routines on the kit's 6502, over side 1's memory as the listing holds it: clear_compose ($88D3), compose_frame ($88E3), draw_fighter ($8A10), place_overlays ($8E83), show_strip ($8B64) and sprite_registers ($97AD), and the screen by the site's C64 library from the memory and the video chip's registers. The moves' lists are move_frames ($BB70). The sprites' pictures are stepped by step_sequence ($9608) through picture_sequences ($CAE8), the ending's after swap_shape_bank ($8C93) has brought its shapes in, and placed from scene_records ($CB9D).

02 · One frame

Nine blocks, a mask and up to four sprites

A frame names nine blocks of 63 bytes, stored the way sprite shapes are, in three rows of three. Choose a frame to see its blocks, the frame drawn into the arena, and the frame with its sprites over it.

The blocks are drawn as stored, facing right, in the arena band's own colours: brown behind, grey, black and light red. The frame is drawn for the fighter, skill level and background chosen above.

Each fighter is drawn from blocks of 63 bytes, nine to a frame in three rows of three, 0 for none (compose_frame, $88E3). The game builds the frame in a buffer nine bytes wide and 63 lines tall, flips it through a table for a fighter facing left (mirror_compose, $892C), and draws it into a copy of the arena's band through a mask, so that the background shows round the fighter (draw_fighter, $8A10). The band is 32 characters wide and 8 tall, 2,560 bytes of the bitmap, and it starts each pass as a clean copy kept since the backdrop was set up (clear_strip, $8B2E).

A frame also names up to four sprites to lay over it (place_overlays, $8E83). Most are the parts of a fighter that rise above the band, the top of the head, a fist, the raised sword, drawn in pairs 24 pixels apart, with a mirrored copy of each shape 13 shapes on for a fighter facing left (shape_offset, $7F46). The right-hand fighter also wears one sprite as a body piece in the skill level's colour (level_colours, $C78A): 45 shapes, which the game mirrors all at once whenever that fighter turns round (flip_sprite_shapes, $8B9A). The ending needs 13 more shapes, for Mariana, Drax and his star, and side 2 swaps them in over the first 13 body pieces from under the system ROM (swap_shape_bank, $8C93).

How a whole screen is put together, the band in the bitmap and the sprites over it, is on How it works.
