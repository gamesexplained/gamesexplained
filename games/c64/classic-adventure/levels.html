01 · The map

How the rooms join

The game keeps no map. Each room has a list of exits, and rules move the player where the exits do not reach. Drawn out, they make this cave. Click a room to read it here and open it in the room browser below.

  • An exit in the room's list

  • XYZY 11A magic word and the room it leads to
  • Lit without the lamp
  • A treasure lies here at the start

Drawn from the exit pairs at $4D63 and from the command rules that go to a room. A solid line is an exit for a direction, UP, DOWN, IN, OUT, CLIMB or the like. A dashed line is a rule's move, and many hold only sometimes: the grate has to be open, the troll gone or the door oiled, or a chance roll has to come up. A word that names a place, such as PIT, DEBRIS, BEDQUILT, VIEW or BARREN, also moves the player, often across a region; where such a word is the only link between two rooms, no line is drawn. Twelve rooms are not on the map, because each is a message, or nothing, that a status rule takes the player out of before the next command. In the maze of passages all different, every room leads to every other but 107 and 112. The game keeps no positions for its rooms, so these are set out by hand.
