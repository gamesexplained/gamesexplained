Maps

All 120 rooms of the factory, drawn on this page from the game's own room records, with the pick-ups where a new game puts them.

01 · The whole map

Ten rooms across, twelve down

Harry starts in room 1, top left. Walking off the right edge of a room adds 1 to the room number, and off the left edge takes 1 away. Climbing off the top takes 10 away, and dropping off the bottom adds 10. So each row of the map is ten rooms in a row.

A yellow number marks a room described on this site: the vats, the generator, dispatch, the lifts and the start. Click any room to see it larger below. Every room is drawn by a port of the game's drawing code, $4000, from its record. A test, test_rooms.js, runs the game's own code on a 6502 simulator for all 120 rooms and checks that the port gives the same characters, colours and property map. The creatures and the lifts are sprites, so they are not here. Click a room to see it full size below.

02 · One room

Room 1

The row of rooms 71 to 80 is the odd one out. Leaving room 80 to the right leads back to room 71, not on to room 81, and leaving room 71 to the left leads to room 80 ($4CDA, $4D07). That row is the train track, and the train goes round the same loop ($6F70). Everywhere else a room on the edge of the map just leads into the next row's room.
