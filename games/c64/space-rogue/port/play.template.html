The whole game, playable in the browser: the title, the story, flight and combat, Navigation Control, the starbases with their shops, bars and HIVE!, and the ending. Every routine is ported from the game's own 6502 code and was run beside it until the two agreed.

Loading the game's data…
  • ← → ↑ ↓ the joystick: steer, walk, choose
  • Right Ctrl, Right Alt or Enter on the number pad: fire
  • W X pitch, A D turn, C Z roll, S stop turning
  • + - speed, F7 cruise or Newtonian flight
  • T next target, R next weapon, Space fire it
  • V view, I TAC data, L automatic fire
  • N Navigation Control, G surrender, J jettison cargo
  • Esc or F1 pause; Tab is the C64's CTRL: Tab+S saves in space

The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the video chip would draw it. A save (Tab+S in space) is kept in this browser, and O at "Old or New Game?" loads it. Before an old game the game asks for a word from its manual; the page shows that word beside the buttons. Skip to the cockpit starts a new game and runs the title and the story as fast as it can, as pressing a key through them would. Native resolution draws the 3D view (the same ships, dots and arrows, from the same positions) as sharp shapes at the screen's own resolution. Smooth motion draws the 3D view at the screen's own frame rate, moving it from each of the game's passes to the next (section 02).
