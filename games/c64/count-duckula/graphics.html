1989 · Alternative Software · Commodore 64

Count Duckula

Everyone in the pyramid, moving at the game’s own speed, and every sprite shape the game stores.

01 · Count Duckula

The cast, at the game’s own speed

Every moving thing in the game, drawn from its sprites in the game’s colours. The objects bob a step every two frames; the pedestal stays still.

Every sprite is 24 pixels wide and 21 high. Duckula is three at once: one for his top half, one for his legs, and a third laid over the first to add the green of his hair and collar. Mummies and crows are two sprites each, and Igor is six. The machine has eight hardware sprites. Duckula keeps three, and the game reuses the other five further down the screen as it draws each frame, so many more can share a room.

02 · Count Duckula

Every shape the game stores

The game stores 104 shapes, each in the colour the game gives it here. Each pixel is see-through, white, black, or the sprite’s own colour; white and black are shared by every sprite in the game. Nine of the shapes, stored after the doors, are Duckula turning round, and the game never shows them. The Discoveries tab has them.
