1989 · Alternative Software · Commodore 64

Count Duckula

Igor’s gift of half an hour that takes half an hour away, an ankh that pays out before it is used, a turn Duckula never makes, and the cheat that turns the whole cast dark.

01 · Count Duckula

Bug: Igor’s half-hour gift takes half an hour away while the clock reads 9:30 or later

Every fourth time Duckula walks into one of four rooms in the lower half of the pyramid, Igor walks in from the left with a plate held out. Touching him once puts half an hour back on the clock, and until Duckula leaves the room every mummy, crow, rock and bat in it is gone. After a visit Igor stays away for the next fifteen rooms Duckula enters.

The game adds the half hour as three tens of minutes. When the tens pass five it takes six of them off and adds an hour, but only if the hour is below 9, because the clock has no room for a tenth hour. At 9:30 or later the hour is already 9: the six tens come off, the hour stays where it is, and the clock goes back half an hour instead of forward. Meeting Igor at 9:36 leaves Duckula at 9:06.

Igor, drawn from his six sprites, and the two additions the game makes to the clock.

That needs Igor within the first half hour of the night, and he can come that early. His rooms are 47, 57, 59 and 72, on the lower three floors. The count of visits does not start again with a new game, so in a second game he can arrive the first time Duckula walks into one of them.

02 · Count Duckula

Secret: the ankh pays its two hours the moment Duckula picks it up

The manual says the ankh turns the clock back three hours when it is used. The game adds two hours as soon as Duckula touches it, and turns the ankh brown to show it is spent. Choosing it from the carrying slots afterwards does nothing.

The clock cannot show more than 9:59, and the ankh stops there. Its two hours are worth having in full only once the clock reads 7:59 or less. Picked up at 8:30 it gives an hour and twenty-nine minutes, and at 9:40 nineteen minutes. Leaving it where it lies until the clock reads 7:59 gets the full two hours. The calculator in the first section shows any other time.

03 · Count Duckula

Cut: three frames of Duckula turning round that the game never shows

Left to right: the standing frame the game shows, the three it never shows, and the first step of walking right.

When Duckula stands still, the game shows him facing the player. When he moves, it cuts straight to a side view. Stored after the doors are three more frames of him, built from three sprites the way the others are: facing the player wrapped in his cape, turned three-quarters, and side on, facing right.

Nothing in the game ever puts them on screen. Every place in the code that sets a sprite’s shape picks one of the others, and none of these nine. They look like the steps of a turn from standing into the walk that was never wired up. Four more shapes at the very end, striped like the doors, are never shown either.

04 · Count Duckula

Secret: typing BURN THE WITCH turns the white of every sprite black

The same moment in the second room, drawn by the game, without the cheat and with it.

With no direction held, type BURN THE WITCH and a space. From then on Duckula walks through closed doors, and he turns black. Both are well known.

The mummy turns black as well, and so does the door. Until the cheat, the game’s main loop does nothing but wait for it. Once the phrase is typed, the loop writes black into Duckula’s three colours and into the two colours all of the game’s sprites share, over and over, as fast as it can. Those two shared colours are the white and the black that every sprite uses, so every bandage, every door stripe and every highlight in the pyramid goes black too.
