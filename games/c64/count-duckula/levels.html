Count Duckula

The whole pyramid in one picture, and every room large, with the objects where a new game puts them.

01 · Count Duckula

The whole pyramid, with every object in its room

All 48 rooms in their places. The rings mark the nine keys, the ankh, the death mask, the sandbag, the canopic jar, the hammer and the pedestal, where a new game puts them. Choose a room to see it large below.

The castle stands outside the pyramid at the bottom left, where the game begins. The two doors that need more than a key are in room 6, at the top, and room 34, in the middle: the death mask opens the first and the sandbag the second. The saxophone’s keyhole is in room 6 as well.

02 · Count Duckula

The pyramid, room by room

Each room as a new game first draws it. Doors, mummies, bats and the walls Nanny breaks down are drawn over it as the game runs.

A room is twenty tiles across and ten down, and each tile is a 16-by-16-pixel picture with a number for each quarter of it. The numbers are what Duckula’s walking and jumping code reads, and a mummy walks only along a 3.
