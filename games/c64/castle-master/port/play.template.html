The whole game, playable in the browser: the title and its menus, the Wilderness and every area of Eternity Castle, the riddles, the spirits, the information screen and saved games. Every routine is ported from the game's own 6502 code and was run beside it until the two agreed.

  • 1 2 3 and Enter: the language, then the prince or the princess
  • ← → ↑ ↓ the joystick: turn, walk forward and back
  • Right Ctrl, Right Alt or Shift: throw a rock
  • Z X turn, Left Ctrl with a turn: a quarter turn, U turn about
  • P L look up and down, F face forward
  • C W R crawl, walk, run
  • A act: eat, drink, read, push, open, collect
  • Space the pointer on and off, - the sight
  • I the information screen: S save, L load, F music or effects, K keys, Q quit

The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the video chip would draw it, at the C64's own pace: a new view takes the game one to two seconds to draw, and appears when it is done. A saved game (I, then S) is kept in this browser under the name you type, whether you choose tape or disk, and I, then L and the same name loads it. Native resolution draws the 3D view (the same objects, from the same place, in the same order) as sharp shapes at the screen's own resolution. Fast CPU runs the game as a C64 with a much faster processor would, a new view 60 times a second, every step at one pace whatever is in view: Pace sets it to one step a second, the C64's typical pace, two, or five (section 03).
