01 · The loading picture

The castle comes up before the game does

Domark's 1990 disk keeps the screen blank while it reads its picture, then puts it up whole, 31 seconds after the disk starts: Castle Master in yellow script over the castle seen from its gate, blue walls and red-roofed towers climbing towards a sky full of stars. It stays up, in silence, for two and a third minutes while the game loads.

The disk's own bytes, recorded in VICE with the picture up and drawn as the video chip draws them.

Domark's cassette shows no picture: only the loader's stripes in every colour, for four minutes, until the game's first menu.
