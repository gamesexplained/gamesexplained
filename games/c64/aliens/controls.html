A joystick for every level, RETURN for the story, and a few keys that only one level reads. The engine reads the keyboard and both joystick ports itself.

01 · The joystick

Either joystick port works

The manual says to plug the joystick into port 1. The game reads both ports at once, so a joystick in port 2 works just as well.

read_joysticks ($0BFE) reads port A and port B of CIA 1, the chip both control ports are wired to, and combines them, so a direction or the fire button on either stick counts. It keeps what is held in $0222 and what was newly pressed in $0223, which every level reads.

02 · The keys

Keys for the story, the marines, and the flares

The engine scans the keyboard itself, eight rows at a time (read_keys, $0B96), and turns the first new key into a character for the levels to read.

KeyWhat it does
RETURNstarts the game from the title, turns the pages of the story, and enters a status code
Letters and digits, DELtype a status code at the start; DEL rubs out a character
F1, F3, F5, F7switch between the four marines in level 2 (marine_switch, $899F), unless the one on the monitors is under attack
Spacedrops a flare in level 5 (flare_drop, $917E), one to a room
RUN/STOPpauses and unpauses, stopping the music's timer and silencing the three voices (restore_choose, $09B0)
RUN/STOP held, then RESTOREstarts the level again, with the marines it began with (nmi_restart, $0959)
Up-arrow held, then RESTOREskips the level, and gives up the ending (why); not in the lift scene

RESTORE is not an ordinary key: it is wired to the processor's non-maskable interrupt. Each frame frame_tick ($099D) points that interrupt at one of three handlers by what is held: one that ignores it, nmi_abort ($0956) with the up-arrow key, or nmi_restart with RUN/STOP. Left alone for a long while, the game turns the volume down and blanks the screen until a key is pressed.
